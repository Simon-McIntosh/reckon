A landing-group member is released when its group lands

A finished landing group unlinks its journal and leaves its outcome record behind. A member pointer that outlives the journal had no owner the landing pass would reach: the promoted-pointer routine refuses any pointer that carries a landing group, and the group routine is reached only through the journal that no longer exists. The member therefore kept its claim, and a section wait reads a live pointer as unlanded, so every request waiting on that member stayed held with nothing left to release it.

Reproduction

A temporary repository and crew home hold a three-member group whose heads are merged onto the primary branch, one request waiting on each member's section, and only the group's outcome record where its journal used to be. At the base revision one pass leaves all three member pointers and all three requests waiting (2 failed, EXIT=1). With the change one pass leaves no member pointer and moves all three requests to queued.

What the change does

The landing pass now reads a finished group's own outcome, which names every member beside the head it landed, and releases each member whose recorded head is an ancestor of the primary branch. The release itself runs through the existing integrated-release path, so the workspace check, the receipt and the section-waiter release are the same ones a live-journal release uses. A member whose recorded head is not on the branch keeps its pointer and its waiters.
