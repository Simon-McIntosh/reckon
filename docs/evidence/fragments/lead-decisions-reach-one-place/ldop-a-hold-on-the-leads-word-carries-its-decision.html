A hold on the lead's word carries its decision

What changed. A queue hold now records what it waits on. Naming lead requires a decision recorded through crew decision record: the hold refuses before any pointer is written when no record is cited, when the cited record is already answered, when it cannot be read, or when the question, context, owner or the two-to-four options with one recommendation are missing, and the refusal names the field at fault. The bound decision id travels on the request's queue_hold, so the decision read shows that record holding the request. A hold naming any other reason is unchanged and needs no record.

Measure. Nine tests in tests/test_a_hold_on_the_leads_word_carries_its_decision.py against a temporary crew home: a complete record is accepted and the read shows it holding the request by name; a plan-wide hold binds every waiting request to the same record; no record is refused naming the required fields and leaves the pointer queued; a record with one option, five options or no recommendation is refused naming options or recommended and holds nothing; another stated reason holds without a record; and the CLI refuses and accepts the same two cases as the API. Neighbouring suites (tests/test_queue_hold_release.py, tests/test_a_held_request_names_its_hold.py, tests/test_lead_decision_read.py) stay green, including the CLI/MCP document parity case.

Negative control. At base revision 5417b373 the hold API has no way to name the lead at the time of the hold, so crew queue hold --run lead-request --reason "the lead chooses" is accepted, exits 0 and leaves the pointer held with queue_hold.reason and no decision. The red run is docs/evidence/fragments/lead-decisions-reach-one-place/s2.html (8 failing ids, EXIT=1).
