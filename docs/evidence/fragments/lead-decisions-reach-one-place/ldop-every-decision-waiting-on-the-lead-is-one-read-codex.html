Decision states, release context and provenance

The updated read separates ready decisions, pending questions and taken_and_reversible choices. A pending record names a run or gate in pending_on; only its own recorded result makes it ready. Missing pointers and partial or foreign exit records do not release it. A reversible choice retains its standing_rule and stays apart from ready questions. An authored plan answer also suppresses a crew record linked through decision_key.

The read retains held_runs, risk, lead_action, recommended_by and recommendation_source. The existing plan-coordinator resolver supplies owner_session, plan_owner and plan_has_live_session, including plans with no queued request.
