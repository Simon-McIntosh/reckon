Open lead decisions across mounted projects

The shared crew home now stores a decision's question, context, options, recommendation, owning project/session/plan/section, recording time and held work. reckon crew decision record --record FILE creates a record; reckon crew view decisions and crew(view="decisions") read the same ordered document. --project narrows the read.

The first head gate passed 70 tests on all_debug, including three records across two mounted projects holding 4, 1 and 0 named requests, returned in that order. An unanswered decision authored only in plan HTML appears with its plan and section; answered decisions do not appear. Launched or missing requests do not inflate held work. The ranking counts distinct waiting requests plus declared held sections.

Plan-only decisions do not store a recording timestamp or owning session. The read reports that timestamp as null, carries the plan's modified date separately, and derives an owning session only when its matching held requests agree. These unknown values are preserved explicitly.
