A pass spends one launch budget, ends, and lets the next pass continue

What changed. admit_once in reckon/crew/admission/selection.py resolves a launch budget — the new budget_seconds argument, else flight.admission_pass_budget_seconds, else 60 s — and reads a deadline once at the head of its launch loop. A launch is started only while the deadline is unspent; when it is spent the pass ends and the remaining queued pointers are left exactly as they were, with no attempt, no hold and no verdict row, so the next pass begins where this one stopped. The pass result carries launch_budget_seconds and launch_budget_exhausted, which the pass verdict record now stores.

Every launch says what it cost. The launch loop accumulates elapsed time per step — handover, dispatch, stamp — on a monotonic clock seam passed as clock (defaulting to time.monotonic), and each launched verdict row carries launch_seconds, slowest_step and slowest_step_seconds. The row builder in reckon/crew/admission/verdicts.py adds those three keys only when they are present, so held and refused rows are unchanged and no schema was touched.

The default lives in reckon/flight.py as ADMISSION_KEY = "admission" and ADMISSION_BUDGET_SECONDS_KEY = "pass_budget_seconds" and DEFAULT_ADMISSION_PASS_BUDGET_SECONDS = 60.0, read by admission_pass_budget_seconds, which falls back to the default for anything that is not a finite positive number.
