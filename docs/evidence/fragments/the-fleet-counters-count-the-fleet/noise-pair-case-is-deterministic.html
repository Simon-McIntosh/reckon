A hypothesis that the measurement withdrew

The home fixture derived the follower's state directory from pytest's tmp_path, which sits under the basetemp root that pytest prunes to its three newest entries whenever a run starts. Failing runs at high concurrency were observed with a basetemp that had been pruned out from under them, and a six-run sample moved from four failures to none after moving the directory. That contrast was drawn across two different command shapes at six runs each, so it measured the shapes.

The like-for-like measurement, fifty whole-file runs at a time under one concurrency with a basetemp per run, withdraws it: nine failures before the change and fourteen after. The change was reverted in the session that made it, and the test file is byte-identical to the dispatch base.

Hypotheses falsified or unresolved

  • Shared pruned basetemp root — falsified: explicit per-run basetemps still fail, 9 of 50.
  • An unreadable per-process stat record read as a dead owner — falsified: 4.8 million probe passes against a live parent returned zero unreadable reads and zero would-end verdicts, with a median probe cost of 61 µs.
  • Arming latency against the 0.75 s lifetime — falsified at idle: the first delivered row arrives 5–22 ms after the arming starts.
  • The policy itself in reckon/crew/ticker.py — not implicated: the policy is pure and takes its clock as a parameter, and 600 in-process replicas of the case ran clean in the same process that carries the failure.
  • The source of the residual whole-file failure — unresolved. It is independent of the home's location, appears only under whole-file concurrency, and its trigger was not found inside the time fence.

What a next attempt should carry

The reproduction is cheap and stable: fifty concurrent whole-file runs yield roughly one failure in five, on one case, with an empty result. The open question is what makes one arming of the resumed path deliver nothing, and it is answerable by recording, inside the resumed arming, the resolved resume mode, the boundary byte, and which condition ended the loop — state that no current log carries. The named case should not be treated as the defect until such a log shows it failing.
