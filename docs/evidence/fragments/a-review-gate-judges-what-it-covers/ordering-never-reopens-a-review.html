Ordering never re-opens a review

The document unit of a plan review digested the plan's dependency wiring and effort figures beside its decisions, gates and summary, so re-ordering a queue re-opened a review of text nobody had edited. PLAN_ORDERING_KEYS in reckon/crew/plan_review.py orders out depends_on, blocks, after, informs, the plan-section-depends-on mapping, the legacy effort letter and the wall-clock estimate; a section record is digested without its status, effort estimate and attempt count. Decisions, gates, the summary and section prose stay, because they are the contract a node builds against.

Coverage also accepts a review's stored document digest under the previous normalisation of the present content. Without it the definition move alone uncovered the document unit on 34 of reckon's 110 live plans that carry an answered review, 10 of them byte-identical to the bytes their review read; with it, none of the 39 plans whose only change since their review was ordering or bookkeeping is uncovered.
