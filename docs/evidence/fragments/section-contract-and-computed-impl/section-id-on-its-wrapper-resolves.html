The defect, reproduced at base ca630c06e894. The change, at head 6e5371720f5b. In reckon/_store.py, _section_heading falls back to a new _wrapper_section_heading: a <section id=…> whose data-reckon is absent or section and whose heading has no id of its own; the collapse replaces that wrapper with the landed card and carries the id onto the heading the card keeps, so the identity survives the write and a repeat collapse refreshes the card in place. In reckon/mcp_views.py, _authored_section_headings lists those wrapper identities in document order and the section read selects the wrapper's heading, bounded by the wrapper so no neighbouring section's prose leaks in. Structured-state regions (decisions, followups, comments) are not addresses and are excluded.
