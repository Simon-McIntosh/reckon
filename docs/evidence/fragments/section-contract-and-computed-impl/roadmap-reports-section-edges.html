Roadmap reports section-level edges

The roadmap's path and blocking edges report a section-level edge when a plan is held by one section of another: the edge's ref carries the target anchor (plan#sN) rather than the plan name alone, the raw view's open_paths entries name the section and gate beside the plan, and a plan-level depends_on keeps its plan-level edge. critical_path itself keeps the seven keys every summary has always carried.

What changed

reckon/roadmap.py resolves a gate's outcome source into dependency rows and graph edges. Three authored forms are read:

  • a declared outcome source — gating_plan — carrying the #section anchor;
  • a #section ref written into the gate's own measure or evidence text;
  • a section named in words beside the plan it belongs to, as in cut-cell-current-attribution section 6 or a bare sections 1 and 2 whose plan is read from the words beside them. This form is marked "source": "gate-text" and binds only when the named section exists on the named plan, so a passing mention never invents an edge.

Each resolved ref produces a dependency row carrying the waiting section, the target ref with its anchor, and the gate it came from. Those rows join the raw view's depends_on, section_depends_on and section_readiness; an unsatisfied one also enters the path graph, and each path entry in the raw view's open_paths gains an edges list naming the section and gate on each step, the critical path's own entry included. The summary's critical_path payload keeps its established keys: the edges stay on the path entry in open_paths, one list lookup away, rather than growing a key every reader has always seen without. A plan-level depends_on yields no entry: its edge reads with an empty section and gate.

Repair: the summary keeps its shape

An independent review of this node reported two blocking findings: the new edges key on critical_path broke the critical-path test in tests/test_roadmap.py and the byte-exact summary contract in tests/test_sprint_summary_lists_pending.py. Both checks pass at the base revision and failed at the round-1 head, so the added key was a regression rather than a contract change.

Commit 7363fe596 keeps the section edges on the raw view's open_paths entries and restores critical_path to the seven keys every reader has always seen — plans, length_hours, length_unit, worker_hours, effort_unit, uncalibrated_plans, uncalibrated_count. Without the repair the two named checks fail (2 failed, 51 passed); with it all three files pass (60 passed) and the two named files pass at base (53 passed), so each finding's check fails without the repair and passes with it. A live re-read at the repair revision shows the same dependency rows as above on the two nova gates, the s6 edge on open_paths[2], and the abbreviated critical_path key set.
