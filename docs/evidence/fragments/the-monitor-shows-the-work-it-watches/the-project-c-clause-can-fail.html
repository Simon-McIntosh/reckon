The project-C clause publishes C's producer, so only the newest-transition selection keeps its stale entry out

tests/test_a_pane_counts_its_session_across_projects.py drives three projects, the third of which, C, carries the session on an older transition (5 working) and another session on its newest. The clause asserts the session-wide total stays 8 rather than 13, so C's older entry of 5 is never read. C's producer was never published through the obligation snapshot, so _other_project_producer_is_live excluded C whatever _newest_session_event returned — the clause could not fail on the newest-transition selection it names. The fix is one line in the fixture: _publish("C", SESSION) beside the existing publishes of A and B, so C's liveness no longer masks the selection and the only thing that can keep the 5 out is the newest-transition choice.
