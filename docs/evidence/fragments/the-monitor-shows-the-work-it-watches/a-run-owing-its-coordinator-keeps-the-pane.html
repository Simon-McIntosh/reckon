A run owing its coordinator keeps the pane

What changed. live_work_populations in reckon/crew/station_subscriber.py no longer settles a terminal run by the review it alone owes. terminal_run_duty_items derives a terminal run's own duties through the obligations module's own helpers and kind tables, and run_owes_its_coordinator holds the session live while any of those duties carries that run's own run_id. A promotion owed by a delivered review, a promotable run, a refused landing, an owed repair and a run that ended needs-help therefore keep their coordinator's pane; a project-wide notice with an empty run_id keeps nothing.

Measure. tests/test_a_run_owing_its_coordinator_keeps_the_pane.py — 8 passed, EXIT=0. The declared negative control, restoring the review-only gate, fails 4 of the 5 keep-live cases and leaves only the review-owing one passing, which is the case that rule was written for.
