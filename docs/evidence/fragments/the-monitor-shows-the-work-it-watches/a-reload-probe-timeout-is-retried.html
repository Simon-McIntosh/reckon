A timed-out reload probe is named a timeout and retried at the next check

checkout_trust.reckon_imports now reports a probe that ran out of its bound as ImportProbeTimedOut rather than as a failed import, and _FollowerReloader.poll leaves deferred_stamp unset on that answer, echoes "the import probe timed out after N s" instead of "does not import", and probes again at the next poll past the freshness window; a genuine import failure still defers until the code moves. At base c752ea8eb the same call returned (False, "TimeoutExpired: Command 'probe' timed out after 30.0 seconds") with a plain-str reason, which the follower could not tell from a failed import. The new tests/test_a_reload_probe_timeout_is_not_a_broken_image.py passes 2/2 with EXIT=0; the declared mutation (setting deferred_stamp on a timeout) turns the retry case red, EXIT=1.
