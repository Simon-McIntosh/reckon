A resume that delivers at the same head settles that head

A resume records the revision it replaced and withdrew the review and promotion duties that speak for it, waiting for the run to move past it. A worker resumed only to record an outcome can finish at the revision it already carried instead: the run's own last word on that head is then a manifest rewrite at the same revision, and reading it as still superseded left a finished run with no review and no duty. Measured on 2026-10-23 in additional-work-is-a-section §4: run r-20261023T012524464845-a-plan-write-refuses-the-bucket was resumed at 03:31:24Z, rewrote its manifest to complete at 03:33:39Z at the unchanged head 6d14eefbc, and the reflex then refused to compose its review while the obligations derivation listed no duty for it. supersedes_head in reckon/crew/recovery_review_dispatch.py now answers the pair: the run carries the superseded revision and the resumed attempt has not delivered since the resume. A manifest that reads a delivered status (complete or failed) and whose write belongs to the resumed attempt is that attempt's own verdict, so the head is settled and the review duty returns; a manifest still in flight, or one not written by this attempt, withholds as before. The write discriminates because the status word alone cannot tell a fresh delivery from the head-reset copy the resume itself leaves behind — resumption._reset_delivered_manifest_status sets a delivered manifest to in-progress before the new attempt spawns. Whether a write belongs to the attempt is runs._manifest_freshness, the one owner of that rule for the rest of the system, asked against the manifest generation the resume door records before it starts the attempt.

Which write. The resume already records the manifest generation the attempt began from, in manifest_baseline_mtime_ns, and comparing the manifest's own write against it needs no clock. That comparison is the leg that decides in production; the recorded instant is kept only as the fallback for a record carrying no generation, and it cannot order a write inside the second the resume was stamped in, because it is stored at whole-second resolution (run_paths._utc_now). The fallback therefore requires the write to clear the resume's whole second by a one-second margin, so a manifest the resume itself rewrote never counts as a delivery. An earlier revision of this change compared against the recorded instant alone and turned three existing §81 withholding cases red by reading a manifest written milliseconds earlier in the same second as a delivery.
