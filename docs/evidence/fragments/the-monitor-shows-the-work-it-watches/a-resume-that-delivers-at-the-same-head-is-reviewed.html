A resume that delivers at the same head settles that head

A resume records the revision it replaced and withdrew the review and promotion duties that speak for it, waiting for the run to move past it. A worker resumed only to record an outcome can finish at the revision it already carried instead: the run's own last word on that head is then a manifest rewrite at the same revision, and reading it as still superseded left a finished run with no review and no duty. Measured on 2026-10-23 in additional-work-is-a-section §4: run r-20261023T012524464845-a-plan-write-refuses-the-bucket was resumed at 03:31:24Z, rewrote its manifest to complete at 03:33:39Z at the unchanged head 6d14eefbc, and the reflex then refused to compose its review while the obligations derivation listed no duty for it. supersedes_head in reckon/crew/recovery_review_dispatch.py now answers the pair: the run carries the superseded revision and the resumed attempt has not delivered since the resume. A manifest that reads a delivered status (complete or failed) and was written after the resume is the resumed attempt's own verdict, so the head is settled and the review duty returns; a manifest still in flight, or last written before the resume, withholds as before. The write time discriminates because the status word alone cannot tell a fresh delivery from the head-reset copy the resume itself leaves behind — resumption._reset_delivered_manifest_status sets a delivered manifest to in-progress before the new attempt spawns.

Which write time. The resume already records the manifest generation the attempt began from, in manifest_baseline_mtime_ns, and comparing the manifest's own write against it needs no clock; a manifest written in the same second as the resume cannot be ordered against the recorded instant, because that instant is stored at whole-second resolution (run_paths._utc_now). The baseline is therefore read first, the recorded instant is kept as the fallback for a record that carries no baseline, and a fallback comparison requires the write to clear the resume's whole second by a one-second margin, so a manifest the resume itself rewrote never counts as a delivery. An earlier revision of this change compared against the recorded instant alone and turned three existing §81 withholding cases red by reading a manifest written milliseconds earlier in the same second as a delivery.
