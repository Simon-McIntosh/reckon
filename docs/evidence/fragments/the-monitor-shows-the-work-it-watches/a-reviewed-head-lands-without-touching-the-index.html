A reviewed head lands without touching the shared index

``reckon crew merge --project <p> --head <sha> --message <subject> --body <text>`` pins the base at HEAD, builds the merged tree with ``git merge-tree --write-tree`` and the commit with ``git commit-tree``, lands it with ``git merge --ff-only`` (retrying a held ``index.lock`` or a moved HEAD), and pushes, confirming the remote head matches. A conflicting head is refused naming the conflicted paths with nothing written, and ``--candidate`` lands an already-built merge commit whose parents are the pinned HEAD and the head. The verb is named in the reckon-build skill's Integrate step.
