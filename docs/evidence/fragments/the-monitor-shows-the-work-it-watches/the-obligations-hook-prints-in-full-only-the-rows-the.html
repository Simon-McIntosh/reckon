The hook prints the actionable duties and counts the rest

The obligations hook reprinted the session's whole list on every turn, so queued requests, stalled requests and reviews an armed reflex had already taken pushed the few rows that needed action off the checklist, and a reflex-owned review-missing held the stop open for a wait nothing at the session could shorten. _row_is_actionable in reckon/hooks/coordinator_obligations.py now reads the decision from a row's own fields: a kind in _NON_BLOCKING_KINDS is never actionable, and a reflex-owned review is actionable only once its age_seconds reaches its reflex_window_seconds. Actionable rows still print in full through _work_lines; the rest collapse into one line counting each kind with its oldest age. _blocking_items reads the same predicate, so the stop verdict holds only on the actionable set.

The refusal is not a wait. In reckon/crew/obligations.py, _review_dispatch_refusal reads the run's own refusal record. A refusal naming a head counts while that head is the run's current one; a refusal naming none counts from its instant against the run's last delivery, the instant _row_age counts from, because a refusal older than the delivery speaks for a revision the run has left behind. While a refusal counts and a stored review of the run is open with an undisposed blocking finding, the duty carries no review_owner, its reason is the refusal's own, and its next_command renders the fold and exempt commands from _undisposed_blocking_findings and _finding_dispose_commands — the same two helpers the refusal itself uses. A reflex-owned row now also carries reflex_window_seconds, from the same _reflex_review_window_seconds() that writes its text, and that name joined DUTY_ROW_FIELDS, so the published snapshot projects it.

Gate. tests/test_the_hook_lists_only_actionable_rows.py: a run-queued, a review-queued and a reflex-owned review-missing row under its window print exactly one summary line naming the three kinds and leave the stop verdict empty; one promotable row prints in full and holds the stop; a reflex row whose age equals its window prints and blocks while the same row one second younger collapses. Through the derivation: a refusal recorded at the run's delivery yields a row with no review_owner, the refusal's reason and a next_command naming both dispose commands; the same refusal an hour earlier leaves the row reflex-owned; and with no refusal the row carries review_owner=reflex with reflex_window_seconds equal to _reflex_review_window_seconds(), both surviving project_run_rows(..., fields=DUTY_ROW_FIELDS). 6 passed. ruff check and ruff format --check add no findings on the three changed files (the two S105 hits in the hook are present at the base revision).

Negative control. Each declared mutation was applied to the tree and the gate ran against it: printing every row, treating every row as non-blocking, comparing the window with > rather than >=, returning no refusal, and removing reflex_window_seconds from DUTY_ROW_FIELDS. 5 of 5 were caught, each by the case declared for it, EXIT=1. Log under the run directory: negative-control.log.

Population.
