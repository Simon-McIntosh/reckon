The small-node record ratio, one week after the rule

Recorded in the small-node census: 178 small nodes over the seven days after the rule reached the worker prompt landed 7,093 record lines against 1,574 product lines, a ratio of 4.51. The target of at most one is not met on that population. The same week's 73 small nodes that change product landed 395 record lines against those 1,574 product lines, a ratio of 0.25, and 105 small nodes carry no product line at all — 54 of them admitted only by the rule's 300-line no-product arm, carrying 5,567 of the record lines. A second reading of the same week, by each node's net tree diff, gives 3.97 and 0.26 respectively, so the verdict does not depend on the unit chosen.
