A worker prompt never both asks for its landing record and forbids it

The defect, reproduced

An implement prompt carried two clauses that met as a contradiction. The landing contract asked the worker to write its evidence anchor into its scope's fragment under docs/evidence/fragments/<plan>/<node-id>.html; the artifact clause said the node wrote no figure and no evidence prose. Composed at the base revision by the new check's own composer, both prompts — the unnamed-artifact one and the figure-named one — carried exactly two sentences mentioning the prompt's evidence prose, and neither excepted the fragment:

A small node writes one `landing:` line in its manifest, stating what changed and its
measure's figure, and writes neither evidence prose nor a figure unless its done-when
names that artifact; naming one does not permit the other.
Your done-when names no artifact, so this node writes no figure and no evidence prose.

The base-arm log records the refusal: three failing test ids, a non-zero exit line.

The fix, stated once

The landing fragment is the node's evidence anchor and is always permitted at its scope path; a figure still needs the done-when to name it; the withheld evidence prose is the prose outside the fragment. The exception is carried by the small-node rule and by both artifact clauses, so no clause forbids the prose the landing contract asks for. The named clause reads:

Your done-when names an artifact, so this node may write that artifact. It permits no
other figure and no evidence prose outside the landing fragment, which is always
permitted.

Gate

tests/test_a_worker_prompt_never_forbids_its_landing_record.py composes the implement prompt for a node whose done-when names no figure and for one whose done-when names a figure: both carry the landing-fragment instruction, neither carries a sentence forbidding evidence prose without excepting the fragment (the named clause is held to the same test), and the figure-named prompt still grants its figure. It scans every sentence that mentions the prompt's evidence prose and requires each to except the fragment, so a rewording that keeps the rule passes and one that reinstates the ban fails.

The clause-wording assertions in tests/test_brief_prompt.py (the plan-prompt snapshot) and tests/test_small_node_prompt.py are updated to the new wording. The head arm passed: 40 passed, exit 0.
