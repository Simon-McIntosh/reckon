A resume withdraws the review of the head it replaced

The defect reproduced. A review's dispatch record is written at the end of the compose, after the worker launches, so a resume that lands inside that window leaves the record reading newer than the resume. withdraw_superseded_review in reckon/crew/recovery_review_dispatch.py compared the two instants — resumed_at <= at — and returned no withdrawal, so a review of the head the resume had just replaced stayed standing and would have scored code about to move. With the record-time gate restored, the new test's sequence-2 case fails with report is None: the queued review is left for the lane to launch against the replaced head.

The repair orders the attempt by the head it composed for, never by when its record was written.
