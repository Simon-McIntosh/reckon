A coordinator's move off Jev's pick counts against the pick

A row whose lineage records a lane change away from the backend its picker_selection chose is a move: its outcome is kept out of the receiving candidate's outcomes and outcomes_at_requirement and tallied under that candidate's moved_in, while the candidate Jev chose gains moved_off. With the three fixture rows (a Jev-routed clive run that passed, a clive pick a coordinator moved to codex that failed, and a Jev-routed codex run that passed), the clive candidate reads 1 passed with moved_off 1 and the codex candidate reads 1 passed with moved_in 1 failed. A row with a lane change but no pick counts under its final lane as before. Gate tests/test_a_move_counts_against_the_pick.py 5 passed; negative control (lineage ignored) 3 failed (EXIT=1).
