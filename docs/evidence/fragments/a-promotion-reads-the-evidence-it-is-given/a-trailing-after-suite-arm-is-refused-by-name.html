A promotion refuses an after-suite arm recorded at a trailing revision

What changed. _evaluate_suite_delta compared each arm's command and the baseline arm's revision, but never the after arm's revision. A run that measured halfway through its own work and then committed again therefore promoted an after arm about a tree nobody ships, and the refusal it did get named neither the revision recorded nor the one required. The run's head is now read once by the delta reader, through the review path's own resolvers — _run_head_for_review for the head (the worktree's HEAD while it survives, otherwise the carried head by ancestry) and _review_tree for the tree that revision resolves in — rather than by a second reader that could disagree with a review of the same run. It is passed into _arm_missing_fields. An after arm recorded at any other revision is refused as after_suite.revision_matches_head, and the message names the revision recorded, the run's head, and the armed command resolved at that head to rerun.

What the base revision did. Handed a manifest whose after_suite.revision is the base while the worktree head has moved past it, the base revision accepted the pair:

base=94a2db3689f096854ef70906870376832751c20e
head=e9e1539c53ade36014a61ac2a5d1b7734d4a567f
after_suite.revision=94a2db3689f096854ef70906870376832751c20e (trails the head)
ACCEPTED: {"added_failure_ids": [], ..., "status": "clean", ...}

(excerpt; the full line is in base-revision-trailing-arm.log, whose header names the revision of the code it imported, 0ce01d29a8ad44e784545b4b47348f48de1ec811). The same probe run at the head, importing 861a1b71dd55, refuses and names both revisions and the command to rerun at the head:

REFUSED SuiteDeltaError: armed promotion requires complete baseline and after suite evidence; missing after_suite.revision_matches_head. The after arm was recorded at 674a894d5f337fe2afd3361950929e00d0d47fe8, which is not the run's head e9e1539c53ade36014a61ac2a5d1b7734d4a567f: re-run the armed command at that head and record the after arm at the head's revision: PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests/test_alpha.py

Measured

tests/test_a_trailing_after_suite_arm_is_refused_by_name.py drives the promotion's own delta reader over a scratch repository whose worktree head has moved past its base. Four cases: an after arm at the base is refused naming both revisions and the head's command; an after arm at the head is not refused on revision; with the linked worktree released the same trailing arm is still refused, the head then coming from the manifest's citations; and a citation list written newest-first still names the true head, because the cited commits carry no order and are compared by ancestry rather than by position. All four pass at the head.

The node's suite pair, each arm resolved over its own tree: the base arm at 0ce01d29a8 names only the census test and reports 51 passed, exit 0; the after arm at the head names the new test and the census test and reports 55 passed, exit 0. The test files importing reckon.crew.promotion_records report the same six failures at both revisions (6 failed, 145 passed at the base, 6 failed, 149 passed at the head), all pre-existing in tests/test_crew_promotion.py and tests/test_promotion_records_the_pick.py, so the change adds no failure.

Negative control. Removing the revision comparison makes the three trailing cases accept an arm, as the base revision was recorded doing: 3 failed, 1 passed, exit 1. The citation-order case is itself a control on the head reader: with the ancestry comparison removed it fails on its own (1 failed, 3 passed), because the last entry of a newest-first list is the base.

A narrowing worth stating. The comparison refuses only when both sides resolve. A citation that names no commit, and a head that cannot be read, are unreadable instruments rather than evidence that an arm trails — the module's other probes already report nothing when they cannot answer, and refusing otherwise would turn a placeholder revision in an existing promotion fixture into a failed promotion. Two fixtures in tests/test_the_consequence_gate_runs_the_nodes_tests.py record placeholder arm revisions (after-abc) against a fixture repository, and they stay green under this rule.

Ruff by delta. ruff check and ruff format --check report no finding on either staged path.
