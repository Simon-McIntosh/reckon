A promotion refuses an after-suite arm recorded at a trailing revision

What changed. _evaluate_suite_delta compared each arm's command and the baseline arm's revision, but never the after arm's revision. A run that measured halfway through its own work and then committed again therefore promoted an after arm about a tree nobody ships, and the refusal it did get named neither the revision recorded nor the one required. The run's head is now read once by the delta reader — the run worktree's HEAD while it survives, otherwise the newest commit its manifest cites, read through git rev-list --topo-order — and passed into _arm_missing_fields. An after arm recorded at any other revision is refused as after_suite.revision_matches_head, and the message names the revision recorded, the run's head, and the armed command resolved at that head to rerun.

What the base revision did. Handed a manifest whose after_suite.revision is the base while the worktree head has moved past it, the base revision accepted the pair:

ACCEPTED: {"added_failure_ids": [], "status": "clean", ...,
 "after_suite": {"revision": "dae2725811d3b2f667848155f4466b3402b596bf", ...}}

(excerpt; the full line is in the log) with the run's head at ab28456cd03e. The same probe at the head refuses: missing after_suite.revision_matches_head. The after arm was recorded at dae2725811d3 …, which is not the run's head ab28456cd03e: re-run the armed command at that head and record the after arm at the head's revision: PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests/test_alpha.py.

Measured

tests/test_a_trailing_after_suite_arm_is_refused_by_name.py drives the promotion's own delta reader over a scratch repository whose worktree head has moved past its base. Three cases: an after arm at the base is refused naming both revisions and the head's command; an after arm at the head is not refused on revision; and with the linked worktree released the head is read from the newest cited commit, so the same trailing arm is still refused. All three pass at the head.

The node's suite pair, each arm resolved over its own tree: the base arm at 0ce01d29a8 names only the census test and reports 51 passed, exit 0; the after arm at the head names the new test and the census test and reports 54 passed, exit 0. The test files importing reckon.crew.promotion_records report the same six failures at both revisions (6 failed, 145 passed at the base, 6 failed, 148 passed at the head), all pre-existing in tests/test_crew_promotion.py and tests/test_promotion_records_the_pick.py, so the change adds no failure.

Negative control. Removing the revision comparison makes the first and third cases accept a trailing arm, as the base revision was recorded doing: 2 failed, 1 passed, exit 1.

A narrowing worth stating. The comparison refuses only when both sides resolve. A citation that names no commit, and a head that cannot be read, are unreadable instruments rather than evidence that an arm trails — the module's other probes already report nothing when they cannot answer, and refusing otherwise would turn a placeholder revision in an existing promotion fixture into a failed promotion. Two fixtures in tests/test_the_consequence_gate_runs_the_nodes_tests.py record placeholder arm revisions (after-abc) against a fixture repository, and they stay green under this rule.

Ruff by delta. ruff check and ruff format --check report no finding on either staged path.
