The census test reads its own fixture

The doctor now reads its census log and transcript roots from the mcp_census module at call time instead of through the function's import-time default arguments, so a test can point the census at its own temporary tree. tests/test_mcp_census.py::test_doctor_prints_the_census_figures builds the connections and transcript blocks in a temporary root with now-relative stamps, computes every expected line from those records, and asserts the real configuration home is unchanged: 6 passed at head against 1 failed, 5 passed at base.
