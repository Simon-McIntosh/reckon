the-view-parity-case-reads-the-budget-at-one-instant — the parity case drives the budget clock instead of waiting for load to move it

Defect. tests/test_crew_view_serves_every_view.py::test_every_declared_view_answers_the_same_document_on_both_surfaces drives every crew view once through the crew tool and once through reckon crew view and requires the two documents to match after the keys in _VOLATILE_KEYS are dropped. reckon.budget.preflight stamps its report's top-level checked_at from the instant it composes (reckon/budget.py, the moment = _now(now) at the head staged into the report mapping). Both surfaces call the same function, so their documents agree exactly when they agree on that instant and differ otherwise. A loaded arm produced the difference by chance and the case then failed naming budget; an idle arm never produced it. The case therefore reported a defect only at load, which is a case that cannot be relied on in either direction.

Fix, one commit. The parity case drives the clock itself. budget._now is pinned to one instant for the first call of each pair and to 30 seconds past it for the second, through _hold_the_budget_clock_at, so the divergence the case guards against happens on every run rather than on some. The budget view's top-level checked_at is then dropped before the two documents are compared, by view and at the document's own top level only (_TOP_LEVEL_COMPOSITION_STAMPS, _without_the_composition_instant).

Why not _VOLATILE_KEYS. That set is dropped by key name at every depth of every view. The review views carry a checked_at copied from a stored review record (reckon/mcp_views.py), at a nested position, and it records what was written rather than when the surface ran — dropping it by name would stop comparing a stored value the two surfaces must still agree on. Scoping the drop to one view's own top level removes the composition stamp and nothing else.

The advance is asserted, not assumed. Driving a value is not the same as requiring it. With the advance shrunk to zero both calls of a pair read one instant, the two documents agree on an empty margin, and the case passes having compared nothing the budget view could not already answer — the drop it exists to exercise then goes unexercised. The parity case therefore reads the advance back off the pair's own documents before anything is dropped and asserts the budget view composed its two surfaces 30 seconds apart. The expectation is written out rather than taken from the driven constant, because an expectation that moves with the value under test holds at zero as well: the first version of this assertion compared the advance against the very constant the drive uses, and the zero-advance control below is what showed it passing while it should have failed.

New case. tests/test_crew_view_serves_every_view.py::test_only_the_budget_views_own_stamp_is_dropped drives the parity case's own filter with a document of each shape: a view other than budget carrying a nested checked_at keeps it, a budget view's nested checked_at keeps it, and only the budget view's top-level checked_at goes. The confinement the drop claims is therefore asserted rather than assumed.

Measure. Ten runs of PYTHONPATH=$PWD .venv/bin/python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q -n 12 tests/test_crew_view_serves_every_view.py tests/test_crew_lanes_view.py tests/test_crew_runs_view.py tests/test_crew_velocity_view.py tests/test_mcp_views.py tests/test_read_verbs_retire_behind_crew_view.py at 194970022 in the foreground on the fleet node: 10 of 10 passed, each run 105 passed with EXIT=0 (gate-run-01.log … gate-run-10.log under the run directory).

Reproduction and negative control. A probe on the case's own fixture read budget._now 6 times per composition, and holding the pair 30 seconds apart moved exactly one leaf between the two documents — .checked_at — while age_seconds, seconds_until_reset, resume_after_seconds and mean_age_seconds did not move; the sweep over every view and argument vector under the advance produced that one disagreement on budget and none on any other view (probe-sweep.log). The declared mutation — stop dropping the budget view's top-level checked_at while keeping the 30-second advance — leaves the case red and naming the view: AssertionError: these view/argument pairs answer differently on the command line and on the crew tool: ['budget with mine', 'budget with phase', 'budget with read', 'budget with session'], all four vectors, 2 failed, 4 passed, EXIT=1 (negative-control.log). The second failure is the confinement case above, which the same mutation drives red.

Second control: the advance itself. The second declared mutation sets the advance to zero, so both calls of a pair pin budget._now to one instant. The parity case then fails on the new assertion, naming the pair and the expectation: the budget view with read composed its two surfaces 0:00:00 apart, and the case requires the second call of each pair to read 30 seconds after the first — 1 failed, 5 passed, EXIT=1 (negative-control-zero-advance.log). The confinement case still passes there, which is the separation worth having: the zero advance leaves the filter's own case green and is caught only by the assertion that the drive moved the pair. This control is what caught the first version of the assertion, which compared the advance against the constant the drive uses and passed at zero; the expectation is now a literal 30 seconds.
