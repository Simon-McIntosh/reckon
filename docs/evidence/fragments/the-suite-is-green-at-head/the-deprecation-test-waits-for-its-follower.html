The deprecation test waits long enough for its follower.

Cause. tests/test_removed_flag_migration.py::test_the_deprecation_announcement_reaches_stderr_and_not_the_stream starts reckon crew follow as a subprocess, which imports reckon.cli, and awaits its first stdout row and its stderr notice through _wait_for_the_first_delivery. That helper already returns the moment both facts appear, but it was given a ceiling of 1.5 s. On a loaded node the subprocess wrote nothing inside that window, so the helper spent its whole ceiling and the assertions then read empty files. The ceiling bought nothing on a fast host — the helper exits early either way — and failed on a slow one.

Fix. The call now passes bound=WATCHER_LOAD_BOUND_SECONDS, the repository's own ceiling for how long the follower may take under load (30.0 s in reckon/crew/dispatch_watch.py, already imported by this test and used by its sibling helper). The helper's early return is unchanged, so a fast host still resolves in under a second; only the ceiling that a slow host runs into was raised. No assertion changed.

Measure. Logs are under the run directory. Base arm (ceiling 1.5 s, test otherwise unchanged, node load average 15–20): 1 of 10 passed — the recorded defect reproduces. Head arm (ceiling WATCHER_LOAD_BOUND_SECONDS): 10 of 10 passed while pytest -n 8 ran over the 334 test files importing reckon/crew/dispatch.py; load average 19.74 at the sample and 71.17 as the load ramped, with the target's own runtimes stretching from 3.92 s to 16.98 s under that contention. Negative control — the declared mutation, the subprocess pointed at a program that writes nothing (-c "pass"): 1 failed in 34.32 s, EXIT=1, the target id red. ruff check adds no finding; ruff format --check finds the same three pre-existing unformatted regions (lines 160, 208, 327) at base and at head, so the delta is zero.
