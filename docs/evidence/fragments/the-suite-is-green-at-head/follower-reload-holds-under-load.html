follower-reload-holds-under-load

Outcome. The re-parented reload case in tests/test_follower_checks_its_owner.py no longer races a transient replacement image. Commit f03d27f9 test(crew): keep the reload observable while its owner is still live.

Cause, named. test_a_reload_under_a_live_subreaper_keeps_the_dead_owner armed its follower with an owner that was also its parent: killing the parent to force the re-parent to the subreaper also killed the owner. The replacement image therefore resolved a dead owner and left almost at once, and the only way the case could confirm the image replacement was _observe_reload, which samples /proc/<pid>/cmdline every 5 ms. The window in which the launcher marker is visible is the replacement's own short lifetime, so on a loaded node the sampling loop could be descheduled across it and the case failed with the follower left without replacing its image; argv='' even though the reload had happened. The same window drove the sibling flake the replacement image was still running 3.0 s after its owner was killed, which a slow node produced instead (reproduced here under five concurrent -n 8 loads; the failing follower's trace showed poll:exec — the reload occurred).

Repair. The case now arms the follower with an owner of its own that is neither its parent nor its parent's parent (_start_subreaper_stub(…, owner_env=…), with the stub passing it through), keeps that owner alive until _observe_reload returns, and only then kills it. The replacement runs on the armed owner rather than for the length of its own imports, so watching for it is a wait on a live process instead of a race against a transient image. Killing the owner then proves which process the replacement resolved, because the subreaper it is re-parented to stays alive; the exit bound is relaxed to 20 s because the direction it must catch — a replacement that re-derived its owner from the parent — never leaves at all.

Measured. Ten consecutive -n 8 runs of the file: 5 passed each, 10 of 10 (logs/head-1.log…logs/head-10.log). Five concurrent -n 8 runs under load: 5 passed each (logs/head-load-*.log). Alone: logs/head-alone-1.log 5 passed. Declared negative control, executed and logged — restore the original read: derive the follower's owner from os.getppid() rather than the recorded environment in _FollowerOwnerCache.resolve: the target id fails (logs/neg-1.log, EXIT=1, 2 failed, 3 passed, the reload case among them). Prescribed reproduction arm: ten -n 8 runs of this file with tests/test_crew.py, logged logs/repro-1.log… logs/repro-4.log (211 passed each; the target branch did not reproduce on an unloaded 28-core node, so the load arm above is the reproduction: the sibling bound branch was captured instead, which the traces attribute to the same transient window).

Follow-ons. None in this node's scope. The exit-bound flake (still running 3.0s) is removed by the same change; no product defect was found — every reload-case follower that reached poll:exec reloaded.
