The producer stubs are the real producer record

Cause. The seat's teardown, join_watch_sweeps in reckon/crew/runs.py, joins both threads a registered producer owns: producer.sweep_thread and producer.deferred_retry. Two cases registered hand-built stand-ins for the producer — _ProducerStub in tests/test_a_limit_reset_reaches_every_pane.py and a SimpleNamespace built by _Producers.__missing__ in tests/test_a_model_notice_reaches_the_session.py — each carrying only the fields its case happened to read. When the producer gained the deferral-retry thread, the teardown stopped being able to read those stand-ins, so three cases errored at teardown after their assertions had already passed.

Reproduction. The base arm (extraction of 1c1080d0c, the main checkout's .env linked) reports 18 passed, 3 errors with EXIT=1, every error an AttributeError on deferred_retry raised from reckon/crew/runs.py:2680. The three ids are test_the_registered_producer_writes_the_row, test_a_sweep_past_the_refresh_age_fetches_once_and_within_it_none and test_a_refresh_that_raises_leaves_the_producer_loop_running.

Repair. Each stand-in becomes the record the seat itself builds: runs._WatchStreamProducer(path=…, known={}, stall_window=runs.DEFAULT_WATCH_STALL_WINDOW), constructed with the path the case already resolves. No production file changes. A field the record gains later therefore reaches the loop and its teardown intact, which is the property the stand-ins lacked.

Measure. Head arm at 5dbe8eca3, an extraction with .env linked: 18 passed in 2.68 s, no teardown error, EXIT=0. Base arm at 1c1080d0c: 18 passed, 3 errors, EXIT=1. Negative control restores the hand-built _ProducerStub in a scratch extraction of the head, never committed: 18 passed, 1 error, EXIT=1, the error being AttributeError: '_ProducerStub' object has no attribute 'deferred_retry' on the id the head arm does not fail. ruff check and ruff format --check add no findings to the two staged test files.
