Fix. Two parts, one commit (b62d9fde). tests/test_concurrency_bound_refuses_by_name.py now writes a recording scheduler beside its backend-command stub and prepends it to PATH, so the admitted case's ensure is answered by a granted job id and its assertion is unchanged. tests/conftest.py gained a session guard: a refusing stub for each of salloc, sbatch, srun, squeue, scontrol, scancel goes first on PATH, each records its own argv and its PYTEST_CURRENT_TEST, and a per-test fixture fails the test that reached one, naming the verb and its argv. A test's own recording scheduler, prepended later, still takes precedence. No assertion was deleted.
