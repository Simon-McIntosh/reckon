A deferred watch tick is retried on the producer's own beat

Reproduction at the base. tests/test_producer_loop_survives_a_config_change.py::test_the_producer_defers_a_broken_config_and_recovers failed at base dea3758c1 in the node's worktree, in 25.05 s: the deferral line printed once and the producer kept delivering rows for node-2 and node-3, but the durable stream carried {'r-1'} and never gained {'r-2', 'r-3'} after the layer was repaired.

Mechanism. The deferral itself was correct: fleet_transitions raises FlightConfigError from the strict rate read, _publish_watch_transitions catches it, announces once, and commits nothing — the fold's memory and the stream are untouched. The defect is in what was expected to retry it. The stream is written from list_live, and the seat's ticker lists the fleet only when _crew_home_change_token moves — pointers, run directories, handovers and the lift document. A repaired flight.yaml is not one of those documents, so on a fleet that is quiet through the repair no listing follows and the owed transitions are never republished. Instrumenting the child process showed five list_live calls up to the deferral and none in the 25 s after the layer was removed.

Repair. reckon/crew/runs.py: a deferred tick now arms a daemon retry that republishes on the producer's own beat (WATCH_DEFERRAL_RETRY_SECONDS = 1.0) until a tick reads the config cleanly, with the thread stopping at once if the registry no longer maps the project to that producer, and join_watch_sweeps joins it as it joins the obligation sweep. The announce-once rule is preserved by reading the episode flag before setting it. Nothing else in the fold, the strict read or the published rows changed.
