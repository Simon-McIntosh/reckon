§22 — A test that starts a watch producer stops it

Why. test_the_dispatch_admission_route_writes_the_log_its_seat_names drives the real _ensure_watch_producer, so it arms a detached supervisor and a producer under its tmp_path home and never stops them. The session reaper reaped_watch_producers SIGTERMs what it finds and fails the session on a producer still alive after its grace, reported against whichever test ran last on the same xdist worker.

Mechanism. The stop goes through dispatch_watch._stop_watch_producer_within in a finally, so a failing assertion still stops the producer. The test's driver now handles SIGTERM the way the real crew watch does, raising SystemExit(128 + signum) (exit 143). The supervisor restarts a producer that dies by signal (a negative status) or with a code in 1..127, but a code above 127 is fatal to it — so the stop ends the whole producer rather than spawning a replacement. Measured with a probe: without the handler the supervisor restarts the watcher ~5 s after the stop (the producer lingers past the reaper); with it, the whole tree exits on the stop.

Head arm. Four runs of tests/test_unmanaged_producer_keeps_a_log.py under -n 4 at d674fe8d end 7 passed, EXIT=0, no session error (logs/head-n4-run1..4.log, logs/head-n4-final.log).

Negative control. Removing the stop (and the stop-handling the stop depends on) reverts the file to base; a run under -n 4 then reports the leaked producer by pid and home — pid 1630534 (RECKON_HOME=…/pytest-of-mcintos/pytest-3/popen-gw1/test_the_dispatch_admission_ro0/config) — with 7 passed, 1 error, EXIT=1 (logs/negative-control-no-stop.log).
