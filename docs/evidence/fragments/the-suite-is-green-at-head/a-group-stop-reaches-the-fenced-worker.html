A group stop after the spawn is recorded as that stop's exit

Reproduction at the base (c9aab06ac8, node-local extraction): tests/test_stop_before_spawn_spawns_nothing.py::test_a_group_stop_after_the_spawn_is_recorded_as_that_stops_exit fails with the stub worker never ran. That is the first layer only. With the case's marker written where a fenced worker can create it, the case reaches the plan's own assertion and fails intermittently (one of five runs at the same revision) with the supervisor recorded no exit for the stopped worker <pid> within 45.0 s.

Mechanism. The spawn retry _spawn_worker_retrying_a_vanished_bind holds the launch for the whole sandbox startup window (SANDBOX_STARTUP_WINDOW_SECONDS, 30 s) whenever the worker writes nothing to its stream, and the supervisor wrote worker.json only after that watch returned. Measured on a failing run: the worker's stream opened at 13:07:03.4 and worker.json was written at 13:07:33.5, 30.1 s later. The case waits for worker.json before delivering its stop, so the stop landed at 13:07:33.5 — after the 30-second stub had already exited at 13:07:33.4. The reaper then recorded the leftover's own exit (worker_pid 1774, exit_code 0) rather than the stop's, and ended_pids was empty because no stop signal was ever sent.

Repair. The spawn calls back as each try starts and the supervisor writes the worker record there, so the run directory names its worker as soon as the worker exists. A stop delivered after the spawn now reaches a live worker and the reaper records the signal that ended it.

Measure. The id passes ten consecutive serial runs at the head (slowest 6.2 s) and fails at the base arm.
