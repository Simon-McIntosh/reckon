The gc scratch test cannot trip on its own basetemp

The fixture's own tree is now excluded from the real-root guard, so a pytest basetemp placed under the real scratch root no longer trips it: _outside_fixture_in_real_root reports a path only when it is inside the real root and not inside the fixture's own tree. test_gc_sweeps_orphan_scratch.py::test_sweep_reports_and_removes_only_old_unclaimed_scratch passes with --basetemp under /tmp/reckon-crew-scratch and outside it (1 passed/EXIT=0 each), and fails (EXIT=1) under a mutation that makes the sweep also remove a real-root path outside the fixture.
