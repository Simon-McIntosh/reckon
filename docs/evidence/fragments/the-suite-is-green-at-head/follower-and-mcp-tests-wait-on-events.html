Follower and MCP deadline tests wait on their observed conditions

The fixed waits the scout named in three files now wait on the condition each was standing in for, bounded by the window the sleep always spent. In tests/test_follow_observes_other_sessions.py the shared collector _follow stops on a caller's condition instead of a fixed settle: the cases that expect a known number of deliveries pass until (three and two events) and end as soon as those arrivals land. Two cases whose claim is an absence — a peer session's run must not reach the follower, and an own row must not be repeated — keep the whole window as a negative-continuity read (_hold_the_window), because there is no positive event to wait on. A startup dwell in the observed-session row case had no observable condition and is removed outright, the tightest bound available. In tests/test_mcp_reads_have_deadlines.py the three waits for an entered call (the loop-blocking probe), a started worker read, and an attempted landing stat now poll for that fact and end as soon as it holds; the two delays that are the subject of their measure — the probe that must hold the loop, and the write that must land inside its grace window — are kept fixed and now carry a comment. In tests/test_follower_checks_its_owner.py the single fixed wait, the subreaper reload dwell at line 896, keeps its window: the follower's reload check is rate-limited to one poll per FOLLOWER_FRESHNESS_SECONDS, the follower is stopped so it exposes no state to observe, and the delay is the condition, so its bound cannot be tightened below the freshness window. No test is skipped, xfailed, deleted or weakened; no product file is touched.
