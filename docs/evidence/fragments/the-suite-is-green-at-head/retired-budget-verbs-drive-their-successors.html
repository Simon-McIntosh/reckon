Retired budget verbs drive their successors.

Cause. The budget verb family was folded behind one parent: crew preflight, crew budget-lift, crew budget-reset and crew budget-lifts are now tombstones that print one JSON document naming the successor under crew budget and exit TOMBSTONE_EXIT_CODE, 10. Thirteen tests in five files still drove the retired names, so each failed at its first assertion on the tombstone document rather than on the behaviour it was written to pin. Twelve of the thirteen are those calls; the thirteenth, test_each_budget_flag_gives_the_answer_the_old_verb_gave, already drives the folded flags and failed because its recorded answer — captured before the fold — no longer matched the landed shape.

Base arm. All thirteen ids were confirmed failing at the base with each driving a retired name returning exit_code: 10, and the thirteenth failing on its recorded answer. Twelve reproduction lines read assert 10 == <expected> with <Result SystemExit(10)>.exit_code; the thirteenth differed by the reset-record fields the recorded answer lacked.

Fix. Each test now drives the successor flag its tombstone names with the arguments it was written to pin, and asserts what it asserted before: crew budget --lift in tests/test_a_new_lift_replaces_the_standing_one.py, crew budget --check in tests/test_budget.py and tests/test_preflight_pace.py, crew budget --reset in tests/test_codex_banked_reset.py. The tombstone's own behaviour is asserted once, by test_each_retired_budget_name_prints_its_successor, rather than per test. No product code changed.

Recorded answer. test_each_budget_flag_gives_the_answer_the_old_verb_gave holds the folded flags to the retired verbs' captured answers. Two captures had drifted from the landed shape and are updated to it, with every field still asserted: the --list answer now carries reset_count and resets, the two fields a limit reset gained when it became one recorded event, and the --check group's allowance block now carries advisory. The reset round trip in tests/test_codex_banked_reset.py passes the --reason the --used case requires, since the test pins the flag's round trip rather than the absence of that argument.
