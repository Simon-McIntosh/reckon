Delivery and watcher tests wait on their observed conditions

Eight fixed waits stood in the four delivery and watcher test files. Six carried an observable condition and became bounded waits on it: the two delivery collectors (tests/test_crew_session_delivery.py _follow/_follow_all) wait for the expected receipts instead of a settle, the mid-write registration holder releases on the reader's completion rather than after 0.4 s, the cold-watch case polls project_watch_visibility until the seat is live, the removed-flag case waits for the follower's first row and its deprecation notice in the files it was given, and the reaped-watcher measure polls /proc until no survivor remains. Each is bounded by the window the former sleep always spent, so the worst case is unchanged and the typical case pays only the condition's latency. Two sites are negative-continuity anti-event windows — the follower that must report nothing over an empty fleet, and the re-attach that must emit no repeat — where the delay is the observation itself and there is no positive event to wait on; both are kept and now carry a comment naming why.

Measured at c8087d0bc5 in this worktree: the four files run with -n 4 report 37 passed, 1 skipped on three consecutive runs (head-run1.log, head-run2.log, head-run3.log), and the base arm at the same revision reports 37 passed, 1 skipped (base-run.log), so the change adds zero failures. The negative control restores the removed-flag site as a fixed time.sleep(0.05), shorter than the follower's startup: the bounded wait passes while the fixed sleep fails on assert rows (negative-control.log, arm 2 EXIT=1).
