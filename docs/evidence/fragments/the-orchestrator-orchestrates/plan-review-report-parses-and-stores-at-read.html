Landing: a delivered plan-review report is parsed, stored and read by the gate

A plan review runs as its own dispatch and leaves a report plus a sidecar on a durable path. The build gate stores that report at read, so the coordinator never runs a store step.

What landed

  • reckon/crew/plan_review.py — parse_review_report reads the RUBRIC/FINDING grammar the two prompt files specify into the store's record shape (stable ids, type, anchor, would-change verdict and reason, absent items); review_report_directory, write_review_sidecar, delivered_reports and store_delivered_report join the report to its sidecar and write through store_plan_review, keyed by content fingerprint.
  • reckon/crew/routing.py — require_plan_reviewed stores at read: when no stored review matches the plan's fingerprint it stores the newest delivered report whose sidecar carries that fingerprint and judges the result. Refusal wording and the report/enforce modes are unchanged.
  • tests/test_plan_review_dispatch.py — four cases: two findings parse with ids, types and verdicts; a missing RUBRIC line is named absent; the store keys a record the reader finds by fingerprint; enforce mode stores a delivered report, refuses by the unanswered finding's name and admits the build once both are answered.

Measure

  • Gate, revision aba2f8d3c: 46 passed, EXIT=0 across the six suites (dispatch, gate, store, prompt, routing-visibility, brief-dispatch). Log: gate.log in the run directory.
