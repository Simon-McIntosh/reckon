A follower on another host is published

reckon/crew/follower_registration.py records the host a registration was written on, spelled as a producer seat spells its own (runs._seat_host), and a registration naming another host is never put to this host's process table: its pids read unknown rather than dead, and its liveness is the one fact that crosses the shared home — the advisory lock the follower holds for as long as it delivers. A session whose follower is elsewhere therefore keeps its obligations snapshot, its hook prints the checklist instead of no producer, and the review reflex dispatches for a scoring run whose session's follower is on the second host. The judgement is shaped as watch_observer_alive's unknown-not-dead rule, and both consumers read the one follower_state value. tests/test_a_snapshot_is_published_for_every_host.py passes 5 tests with a bare EXIT=0; reverting the foreign-host decision to this host's process table fails 3 of them — the hook, the reflex, and the held-lock judgement.
