The clone detector reads revisions through velocity's one sources reader

The detector's _git runner, its archive-based revised_python reader and its _cache_root resolver are retired. reckon/clones.py now imports run_git and read_sources from reckon/velocity.py and its cache from _store.cache_root. _plan_documents became a call of the generalised tree lister _tree_paths(repo, revision, prefix, suffix); the new public read_sources(repo, revision, prefix=) composes that lister with read_blobs; the counter's read_trees parses read_sources output. The detector takes no prefix (every tracked .py), the counter passes reckon. Both consumers keep the scope they measured before.
