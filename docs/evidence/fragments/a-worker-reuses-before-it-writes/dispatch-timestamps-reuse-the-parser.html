Dispatch timestamps read through the shared parser — evidence

Three of the four timestamp readers in reckon/crew/dispatch.py now resolve through reckon._timestamps: the budget-hold evidence stamp and the repository clock parse through parse_utc, and _lane_advisory_instant through parse_iso. Each reproduces its base-revision output on all six inputs of tests/test_dispatch_timestamps_reuse_the_parser.py. The lane reading keeps its own datetime.fromisoformat call so its refusal can quote the parser's exception text, which the shared parser swallows.
