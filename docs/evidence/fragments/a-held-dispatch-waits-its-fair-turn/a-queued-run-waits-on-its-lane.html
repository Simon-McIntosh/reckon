A queued review waits on the lane that queued it

The site where a queued review's recorded backend parts from the lane that admits it is the in-place update of an already-queued request: a re-dispatch recomposed the request onto its own resolved lane (reckon/crew/dispatch.py, the _answer_existing_node_run call), so a request queued on the local lane came to record the metered lane while keeping local: true. The re-queue now keeps the lane the request is already queued on. Reproduced at base (queued backend codex against local clive) and verified at head by tests/test_a_queued_run_waits_on_its_lane.py, whose local admission pass launches the request through a stub once a slot frees.
