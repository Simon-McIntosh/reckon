A queued pointer without its time reads no place

The place a queued request holds is derived from when it arrived, and the ordering that derives it refuses a pointer carrying no readable queued_at. queued_placements let that refusal propagate, so one malformed pointer cost every queued request on the lane its place and, through crew view live and the obligations read, took the whole read down with it. Reproduced at base revision 9cb55dea5: a three-pointer snapshot whose third carries no queued_at raises ValueError("queued pointer has no readable queued_at").

The refusal now belongs to the pointer rather than to the read. Before ordering, queued_placements drops any queued pointer whose queued_at does not parse; the rest are ordered exactly as before, so each keeps the place it will be launched at and the third reads no place at all. Its run is still listed as queued, because the classification and the listing read the pointer rather than the ordering. The ordering the admission pass ranks by, queue_order.admission_order, is unchanged and still refuses such a read, and a case holds that so the narrowing cannot drift out of the reader and into the pass.

Over a temporary crew home holding two placeable queued pointers and one without its time: queued_placements returns {older: position 1 rank 1, newer: position 2 rank 2} with the third absent and no exception raised, and crew view live lists all three with classification queued, the two carrying their position and rank and the third carrying neither.

The declared mutation restores the propagating call in queued_placements. Under it the two cases that read the placement fail with the original ValueError, 2 failed, 1 passed, ending EXIT=1; the fix reverted, the file passes 3 passed, EXIT=0.
