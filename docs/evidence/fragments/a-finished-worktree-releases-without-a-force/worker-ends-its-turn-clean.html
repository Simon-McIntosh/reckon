Refuse a worker's turn end while its worktree is dirty

The stop hook now resolves the run's worktree, lists its uncommitted paths, and refuses the turn end until each is committed or discarded. The .venv and .env provisioning links and everything under the run directory are exempt; a blocked or failed manifest may end dirty once it names every path it leaves; a stop reaching the refusal cap records the uncommitted paths before it is allowed.

Reproduction on the base revision

A worktree holding M tracked.txt and ?? notes.txt with a manifest reading complete stopped silently:

blocked: False
reason: None

Refusal on the changed revision

blocked: True
reason: worker stop refused: the worktree ... holds uncommitted changes outside the run directory: tracked.txt, notes.txt. Commit each path or discard it before the turn can end. Refusal 1 of 3.
