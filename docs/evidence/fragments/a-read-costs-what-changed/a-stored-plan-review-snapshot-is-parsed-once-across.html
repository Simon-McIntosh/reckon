A stored review snapshot is parsed once across processes

A stored plan review's snapshot was parsed four times on every read, twice through _plan_html.read_state and twice through the structural span reader, and nothing was kept between calls or between processes. The three parts of the evidence — unit digests, prose texts and section declarations — are now computed and persisted together through metadata_index.persisted_memo, keyed by the snapshot's content digest and guarded by the source digests of the review and plan-HTML parsers, so an entry recorded by another revision of either parser reads as absent. A restarted process answers from the stored evidence instead of parsing the snapshot again.
