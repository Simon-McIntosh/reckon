A promotion folds its row without reading the ledger

The promotion hands the run-time profile refresh the row it has just written beside the ledger state it observed before appending. The refresh extends the stored fold samples with that row and reads nothing else, so no index decode and no run-document read happens while the promotion lock is held. When a stored profile exists but that anchor does not account for it — a moved source, a different date, a rewritten history — the fold declines and leaves the served profile alone instead of rebuilding it under the lock. A project with no published profile has nothing to fold into and still takes one build, which is the cost of a cold home paid once per project.

The new case drives crew complete --gate not-run for three review runs against a 200-row ledger, once with a fresh index and once with an index held stale, and counts calls to _index_payload, _last_good_index_data and _merge_run_documents made from the profile path while the promotion lock is held. Both cases record none, and the promotion's own ledger reads are still observed, so the instrument is proven to see something. A pick made afterwards serves a profile of 203 rows with a group per promoted backend.

The negative control restores the current refresh: the stale-index case then makes three _merge_run_documents calls and two index decodes per promotion from the profile path, and the case fails. Its log is logs/negative-control-stale-index.log in the run directory.

Five promotions an arm, on all_debug against a home seeded at the live ledger's 3124 aggregate rows (24 KB of retained narrative each), 8192 run documents and an index held stale. Base 3801df115c took a median 4.840 s and head 3.190 s, at a load average of 1.4 to 1.9 logged per promotion. Timing the refresh phase on its own, base cost a median 2.565 s and head 1.072 s — the phase this node changes falls to 42% of base, and the log is logs/timing3-base.log beside timing3-head.log. The node's stated bar, that a promotion's total median fall below half the base's, is not met: it holds at 0.67×. The arithmetic of why is worth recording, because the shortfall is not in the profile. A promotion's own append rewrites the whole live-sized aggregate, and that work is identical in both arms: 3.170 s minus the 1.072 s refresh leaves 2.098 s, against a base half of 2.370 s, so the bar turns on the fold costing under 0.27 s. The residual is the fold's tail, which filters and re-maps all 11,316 stored samples and writes the served profile — memory and one write, no ledger read, but still linear in the sample count. Removing that re-map is a follow-on, not part of this node's deliverable, which is that no index decode or document read happens under the lock.

The node's own cases ran in matched arms: 23 passed at base 3801df115c and 25 at head, with no failure at either. Across the 141 test files importing reckon.crew.promotion or reckon.crew.picker.lane_context, separate all_debug jobs 1311680 and 1311681 reported 42 failed and 930 passed at base against 42 failed and 932 passed at head, with identical failing test identities: zero added failures. Ruff check and ruff format --check add no finding on the staged paths.
