The reservation can be replaced

The automatic ensure replaces the published record only when its own job has left the queue, so a fleet could not be pointed at a new allocation while the old one still ran. reckon crew placement gains --replace JOB, which names the target job explicitly, reads the target's state and owner from the scheduler, composes the fleet node's own allocation-shape reader for its size, and refuses a job that is pending, that belongs to another user, or that the scheduler does not know — each with its own message and with the record left untouched. On success it publishes the record naming the new job, records the job it replaced beside it, and prints both ids. Steps already running in the replaced job are untouched: only the record later dispatches and resumes read changes, so a dispatch composed after the replacement places its worker under the new job id (--overlap --jobid=<new>) and never the old one.

The focused gate — tests/test_crew_placement_reservation.py together with tests/test_ensure_holds_one_reservation.py — is green at 20 passed, exit 0. A declared negative control that makes the replacement accept a pending job turns the pending-job case red: test_replacement_refuses_a_pending_job fails with DID NOT RAISE CrewError while the head arm passes it.
