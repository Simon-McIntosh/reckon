A run whose remedy is a repair is a duty of its session

Two recovery readings carry the repair verb: unreadable, the declaration the probe reader refused, and exited-unfinished, a worker that exited with commits beyond its base while the manifest still carried a non-terminal verdict. Neither reached the obligations view, because the map from a recovery reading to a duty kind listed only needs-help and launch-failed. So a run whose wait was refused left its session idle with the coordinator told nothing — the case reported against r-20261106T084812488886-polygon-near-band-quadrature, where crew view obligations returned count 0 with unreconciled_runs 1 while the awaited scheduler job went to TIMEOUT unseen.

The two readings are now entries in RECOVERY_CLASSIFICATION_DUTY_KINDS, keyed by the row's own recovery_classification rather than by its verb, and the projection carries the classifier's detail into the item's existing reason for both. The obligation therefore says what to repair — the reader's refusal sentence, or the verdict the manifest is stuck on — instead of only naming the run.
