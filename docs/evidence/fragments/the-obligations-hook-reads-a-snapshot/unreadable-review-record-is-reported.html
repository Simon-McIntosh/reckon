An unreadable review record is skipped, named, and cannot stop a sweep

A review worker writes its record file itself, so a reader can meet one mid-write. stored_record in reckon/crew/review.py parsed each candidate with a bare json.loads, and a truncated file raised json.decoder.JSONDecodeError out of read_review into the producer's sweep through the sub-floor lookup, killing the sweep thread — so every session's snapshot stayed stale behind the one unreadable file, for as long as it stayed unreadable.

The reader now skips a candidate it cannot read or parse, answers from the records it could read, and keeps each skipped path with its error for drain_read_failures. The published snapshot document carries those rows under findings, so a reader sees the path and the parse error rather than a run that reads as carrying no review at all. A failure deriving the fleet state, or slicing one session, is caught in obligation_snapshot.sweep, logged to stderr with its traceback, and costs only that sweep or session; the next sweep publishes as usual. Also folded in from the section's review: the unused resolve() left coordinator_obligations.py, and the new-session worker-slots fallback is gated on the admission block being present rather than on a session map.
