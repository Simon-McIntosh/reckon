The reuse region and its close-before-append already sat in the tree at the base revision. This node supplies the measure the section asks for, over both shapes a promotion takes, and holds the region to the freshness it must not cost. A review promotion and an implement landing are each driven over a 2,000-row ledger, and every ledger-index decode is counted per read key before the append: 4 when the region serves nothing, 1 with it in place.

The freshness half is shown too. A peer process appends its own row to the same ledger between the promotion's append and the read after it; that read returns both the promoted row and the peer's row. A mutant that lets the region survive its own close fails this case with the read after the append missed the promoted row, so the check is known to discriminate rather than merely to pass.

The decode the region saves is not bought with a different answer: the landing preconditions and the fleet-state reading answer byte-identical results through the region and with the region removed. Each reading is given the ledger it actually reads — the preconditions the checkout root the promotion hands them, the fleet reading the config-home ledger its rootless lookup resolves — and the fleet value is shown to carry a median rate over a 12-row cohort before it is compared. A mutant that seeds that ledger empty fails both cases on the positive control with [null, 0], and a row appended between two samples moves the reading, so the equality is known to discriminate.
