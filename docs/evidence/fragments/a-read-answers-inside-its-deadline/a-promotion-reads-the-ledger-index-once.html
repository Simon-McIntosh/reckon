A promotion builds the ledger index once per read key before its append

A promotion reads the ledger four times before it writes its row: the landing checks read it from complete for the existing-row probe, again through _landing_preconditions, and the locked body reads it a third time to build the row, with the tree-boundary scan reaching a fourth. Each read ran its own decode of the whole index, so the before-append stage was the only part of a promotion whose cost grew with the ledger: _index_payload held 49.8% of the promotion body at 7,787 rows against 7.1% at one twentieth of that.

reckon/crew/promotion.py now opens one ledger.index_reuse() region in complete, before the independent checks run, and hands it to _complete_locked as an ExitStack. The locked body exits that region immediately before ledger.append_run, so every read before the append shares one decode per read key and a read after the append decodes afresh and sees the row just written. A direct caller of _complete_locked opens a region of its own, so both entry points carry the property. No second cache is added: the region is the existing one the obligation snapshot already uses.

tests/test_a_promotion_does_not_read_the_whole_ledger_index.py builds its own crew home, checkout and a 2,000-row ledger, warms the index with one read, then drives one in-process crew complete --gate not-run of a run whose deliverable is its report. A wrapped ledger._index_payload records each decode's read key and whether the append had happened, keyed by the index file and the projection asked for.

On the all_debug partition, over that 2,000-row fixture, the full-index key costs 4 decodes before the append at base e77152e3 and 1 at head f67f6551; a read after the append costs 1 in both arms and sees the promoted row (EXIT=0 on both arms). The declared negative control — removing the reuse region — raises the before-append count for that key back to 4 and fails test_a_promotion_decodes_each_index_key_once_before_its_append while the after-append test still passes (EXIT=1), so the head figure passed rather than the test resting on an unseen defect.

The fence suite over this node's write scope reads 53 passed at head (EXIT=0) and 49 passed at base. The gate over the 97 test files that import the changed module runs one arm at a time on all_debug with -n 4, and its figures are recorded in the node's manifest against the base revision.
