A cold read builds each plan file's soup once

read_state_file, parse_plan (through _parse_plan_uncached) and project state's _cached_plan_state now derive from the read_state_and_text_file parse instead of each building their own BeautifulSoup. On a distributed ten-plan corpus a cold roadmap read builds each plan file's soup 3 times at base and 1 time here; a cold discovery read builds it 2 times at base and 1 time here. A third case reads each plan through the shared parse and then composes the project state, whose sprint hydration reaches _cached_plan_state: 2 builds at base, 1 here. The negative control gives _cached_plan_state its own parse again, and all three count cases fail at 2.
