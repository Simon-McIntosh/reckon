The attempt memo names the stamp members it compares

The section-attempts memo compared the ledger index stamp by a positional slice, stamp[0][:4], so a member added to or reordered within ledger.index_stamp silently changed the state the memo keyed on. The ledger now exposes index_state_stamp: the same members without the index file's own identity, returned as a record keyed by member name. mcp_views._attempt_state reads that record by name. The index file's identity stays out of the comparison, because a read rewrites it.

Measure. tests/test_the_attempt_memo_names_its_stamp_members.py inserts an extra member at the front of the stamp the ledger builds and drives one memoised read. Head: memo holds on an unchanged stamp (1 aggregation over two reads), invalidates when the runs directory's identity moves (2), and holds when only the index file's identity moves (2) — 4 passed with the existing attempts-cache tests, EXIT=0. Base arm: the inserted member pushes the runs directory's identity out of the four-member slice, the memo misses the change, and the runs-directory assertion fails with assert 1 == 2, EXIT=1. ruff check and format add no findings on the changed files.
