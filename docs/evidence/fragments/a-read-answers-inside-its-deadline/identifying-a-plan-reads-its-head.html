§14 — Identifying a plan reads its head, not its body

One node's evidence: identity — type, slug and archived — is declared in the head, so identification reads the head and builds no soup. A slug resolve, its uniqueness check included, parses no plan body.

Outcome

The metadata parse is split into a head-only reader (_plan_html.parse_meta_head, whose fields come from _meta_head_fields) and the section-derived impl tail that parse_meta keeps. Identification (resources.identify_resource, its digest-shared metadata_index.parse_meta_head_shared, and the canonical-path checks in resources.py) now reads the head only. parse_meta is unchanged: a reader asking for impl still receives the figure derived through the parser.

Measure

A cold resources.resolve_resource of a record-carrying plan enters _plan_html.read_state zero times (it entered once per sibling at base), and identify_resource of a record-carrying plan enters it zero times. parse_meta of the same plan still returns impl and impl_source derived through the parser. A cold-process resolve_resource('reckon','lane-and-model-routing','plan') with empty memos fell from 5.1226 s at base to 0.4627 s at head.

Negative control

Identification routed back through the full parse_meta (metadata_index.parse_meta_shared): the two zero-entry cases fail (assert 1 == 0), EXIT=1. Log: negative-control.log under the run directory.
