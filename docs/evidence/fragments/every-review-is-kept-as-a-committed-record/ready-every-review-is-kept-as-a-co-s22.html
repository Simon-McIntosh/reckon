Landing

The delivery-time subject guard in reckon/crew/review.py was already present and fires from two callers: review_record_refusals behind crew check --manifest, and store_review_refusals behind the review-filing block in crew complete. Its only test, tests/test_a_subjectless_review_record_is_refused_at_delivery.py, failed at the base revision because its fixture manifest predated the messages_received delivery requirement — three cases red for a reason unrelated to the subject. The fixture is repaired, and two cases are added that invoke crew complete --dry-run, which is the gate that files or refuses the record rather than the pre-flight check alone. Five cases pass at head. Negative control: with the delivery-side subject clauses removed in a scratch tree, the subjectless record passes delivery (its dry-run report carries no review_filed refusal) while store_review still refuses it at promotion, EXIT=1. Gate logs and the control are under the run directory.
