Promoting a review keeps its dispositions

A review run delivers its record twice: once into the reviewed run's own review-records directory, and once into the host staging store, where the supervisor files it. A coordinator answering a duty disposes a finding and a sub-floor dimension on the store copy. Promoting the review run refiles the delivered copies into the store, and that filing writes the delivered body over the store file — so an answer recorded on the store copy alone is gone, and the obligations reading raises the same sub-floor duty again.

Reproduction, before the change

In a temporary crew home and committed tree, the record is delivered at both granted paths, a finding and an evidence dimension are disposed on the store copy, and the review run is promoted with crew.complete(..., gate="passed"). The write that drops them is file_delivered_run_review_report → store_review, reached from promotion's _file_delivered_run_reviews. Base-arm log base-arm.log: EXIT=1, the committed record carries no finding_dispositions key at all.

Change

  • reckon/crew/review_dispositions.py — a disposition is now written into every delivered copy of the record inside the reviewed run's review-records directory as well as into the store copy the call selected (_delivered_copies_of_record, a rubric- and run-id-matched selection; _carry_answer_to_delivered_copies, a per-name merge by the entry's own stamp that writes nothing when the copy already carries it). A later filing therefore carries the answer into the store rather than overwriting it.
  • tests/test_promoting_a_review_keeps_its_dispositions.py — the case above, asserting both dispositions in the committed record and no sub-floor duty in the obligations reading.

Measure

Gate, head arm: 1 passed, EXIT=0 (head-arm.log). Base arm, the same test over the exported base revision: 1 failed, EXIT=1 (base-arm.log). Declared mutation — the promoted record written without carrying the dispositions — is red on this case (negative-control.log).
