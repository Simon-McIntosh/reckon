One reader of a gate's declared plan refs

The audit tokenised a gate's required_evidence and treated every word as a candidate plan ref, so a prose word equal to a plan slug could silently wire a plan. That reader and the roadmap's gate-ref reader ran the same grammar twice. The declaration-form half of the grammar — a gate's gating_plan outcome source and a #section-anchored ref in its own text — now lives in one function, gate_declared_refs in reckon/doccheck.py, which gate_evidence_slugs_from_gates and the roadmap's _gate_section_refs both call; the prose half (a section named in words beside its plan) stays in the roadmap. Only a declared form wires a plan. The audit message also names plan-section-depends-on and plan-after, and the untested section_depends_on refusal input now has a case. Negative control: making the shared reader accept any whitespace token wires a prose-named plan and reddens the test (3 failed, EXIT=1).
