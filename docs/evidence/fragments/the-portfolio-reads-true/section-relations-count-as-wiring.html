A section relation, or an inbound gate, counts as wiring

The unwired-plan rule read only a plan's plan-level links, its gates and its standalone reason, so a plan whose only portfolio relation was a section-level one (plan-section-depends-on or a soft plan-after) was reported unwired, as was a plan some other plan's gate names as its required evidence. Both are wires the roadmap already follows, so the rule now counts them. unwired_plan_finding gains section_wires and inbound_evidence; its three callers pass them, the audit deriving the inbound set once over its corpus and the roadmap once over the project's rows. tests/test_section_relations_count_as_wiring.py passes EXIT=0 — 8 passed — on a synthetic project where plan x declares only a section wire and holds a gate naming y, y is named only by that gate and z declares nothing: z is reported unwired, x and y are not.
