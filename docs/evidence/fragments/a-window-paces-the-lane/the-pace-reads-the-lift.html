The pace reads the lift

A group's budget block is resolved through budget_lift.effective_budget once, in budget.group_pace, before the allowance is derived; every reader of a group entry — the hold, the ceiling and the pre-flight report — then reads the resolved figure.

  • With no lift the fixture is over pace and an implement dispatch is held; under a 1.8x lift the same reading is admitted.
  • A reading whose seven-day reset has moved ends the lift, because the resolver is handed the group's records in observed order rather than only the newest figure.
  • A drain-by lift holds above its own line and admits below it; an uncapped lift admits at any burn; a lift whose from is in the future is inert.
  • A session lift admits its own session's verdict and holds another session's on the same reading.
  • The pre-flight group entry carries the lifted multiple and the lift id.
  • The three reserves are zeroed by the resolver, so the ceiling a lifted group is measured against is its whole window.
  • A lift in force is read from the resolver's lift_id, not from the presence of a lift key. Resolved flight config declares the lift ceilings under budget.lift, so that key is present on every configuration that states them; the fixture carries the shipped ceilings and asserts a group under no lift, and a group whose lift has ended, reports no lift at all.

In pace_hold, the release instant divides by the banked-reset factor read from where _scale_for_banked_reset records it rather than a restated constant, and an allowance whose window cannot be placed reports no release instant.

Gate: 11 test files, 162 passed at head against 152 passed at base, EXIT=0 both arms, zero added failures. The declared negative control — replacing budget_lift.effective_budget with a function that returns config['budget'] unchanged — was applied on a scratch copy and observed red: EXIT=1, three failing ids the head arm does not fail, including the 1.8x-lift admit assertion.
