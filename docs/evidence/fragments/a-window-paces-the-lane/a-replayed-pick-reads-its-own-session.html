A replayed pick reads its own session

Each replayed dispatch is judged against the budget view composed for that dispatch's own session.

The dispatch replay now composes at most one budget view per distinct session in the replayed set and hands each record the view for its own session, so a dispatch carrying no session reads the session-less view as before and a session-scoped lift releases exactly that session's reserves. A lift scoped to session A over a group read at 95% leaves the A dispatch at the released ceiling (100) and the B dispatch held at the reserve ceiling (92), a one-session replay composes one view, and the negative control that restores the single session-less view reddens the A case (3 failed, EXIT=1).
