A lift resolves the effective budget

One function resolves a lift, and the resolver returns the lifted block with the multiple in force and all three reserves released, or the configured budget unchanged when no lift covers the group. Expiry is a pure function of the record, the readings and the clock, so nothing runs at the moment a lift ends.

What landed

reckon/crew/budget_lift.py records one control document per workstation at <config-home>/budget-lifts.json, written with reckon._store.write_json_atomically under a monotonically increasing version. Each lift carries the §10 record fields — id, group, pace_multiple, ends, granted_by, granted_at, reason, form, scope, starts_at, cleared_by, cleared_at — plus the drain-by target/u0/t0 and the projected exhaustion. effective_budget(config, *, group, readings, now, session) returns the budget block with the lifted multiple (or the drain-by hold, or the uncapped release) and resume_reserve_pct, coordinator_reserve_pct and bookend_reserve_pct all zero, or config["budget"] unchanged.
