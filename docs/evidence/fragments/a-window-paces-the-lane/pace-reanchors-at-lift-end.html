A lift that ends re-anchors the default pace hold

A lift lets a group spend ahead of pace. When it stops before the reset of the window it was anchored to, the utilisation spent stays in burn = utilisation / elapsed_fraction, and the default hold burn > multiple keeps holding although the group is back on the ordinary pace. The fix re-anchors the hold at the lift's end rather than granting a successor lift: allowance(t) = u_end + multiple × (1 − u_end) × (t − t_end) / (reset − t_end).

What landed

reckon/crew/budget_lift.py stamps a lift's end with the group's utilisation at that moment. clear() records it beside cleared_at from the published readings, and a lift that lapses by its stated end (END_AT) or its hard ceiling (END_CEILING) inside its window is stamped once, by the first budget read that resolves the group. The stamp is u_end beside the instant t_end, and re_anchor_origin(group=...) hands the pace path the latest such point. Nothing is granted — no record is created by the re-anchor, and the lift document is written only to carry the stamp on the lift that already exists.

reckon/budget.py gives group_pace's no-lift branch a re_anchor block in the allowance when a lift on the group ended inside the current window, and pace_hold then compares utilisation against the re-anchored line rather than against the plain burn. The reason names u_end, t_end and the allowance at the read, and releases_at is solved on the re-anchored line rather than on the plain burn.

A clear that predates the stamp is filled in on the next read. The lifts that motivated this node were cleared before it existed: they carry cleared_at and no u_end, so a stamp written only where a clear happens would never reach them and the group would stay on the cumulative comparison. The newest cleared lift on the group with no stamp is therefore filled in once by the next budget read, at t_end = cleared_at, taking u_end from the newest reading observed at or before the clear. With no such reading the current one is used and marked, by u_end_taken_at and u_end_late, as an account of the clear taken after it — worse than a measurement, and truer than continuing to hold on spend the lift authorised. A lift already cleared by a successor lands in this path too, which is what makes the re-anchor reach a chain of lifts rather than only the last clear. A clear the window's reset has already passed is left alone.

Two cases are deliberately outside the re-anchor. A group with no lift ended in the current window keeps today's comparison exactly. A lift that ended because its window reset is not a lift that ended inside its window, so it leaves no re-anchor behind — and the lapse path now checks the window start as well as the reset, so a lift that lapsed in an earlier window is not stamped either. Without that check a lapse the reset had already overtaken would be stamped on every workstation read, recording a point the re-anchor then rejects, which is write churn on a shared control document for nothing.

u_end and t_end travel together: a reading carrying no figure for the lift's own clock stamps neither, so a record with an unusable reading stays readable as unstamped and a later read can fill it in rather than leaving a t_end that claims an end with no figure beside it.

Why the burn floor does not close the window

At 4% elapsed the elapsed-fraction burn is unmeasurable — BURN_ELAPSED_FRACTION_FLOOR is 0.05 — so the plain path returns an allowance carrying no burn_multiple and pace_hold admits at that floor path. That is the shape the brief records: a group at 75% with 4% of its week gone is held by the burn ratio elsewhere in the week, and admitted where the ratio is unmeasurable. The re-anchor decides on its own line wherever the burn is measurable or not, because a window too young to project a burn can still have spent past what the lift left it. The measurement gate is therefore if not measured and not re_anchored, and a re-anchored verdict reports allowance and burn_multiple as None rather than inventing a burn, while the reason names the line that did decide.

Measurement

The fixture is the brief's shape: a seven-day window resetting 2026-10-27T06:32Z, a lift stopped at 4% of the week elapsed with the group at 75% used, and the configured multiple 1.1×. A cleared lift is stamped with u_end = 0.75 at the end instant, so the allowance at the end is 0.75 and the line rises toward 1.0 at the reset. Asserted:

  • 75% (the lift's own end figure) admits, 70% admits, and 76% — one point above the line — holds, with the reason naming 75% and the end instant;
  • the line stands above u_end at the window's halfway point and the same group admits there;
  • a lapse by a stated end is stamped once: the second budget read leaves the record byte-identical;
  • a window that has reset since the lift ended reverts to the plain comparison (0.60 over half the week is 1.2× burn, above 1.1×, so it holds) and carries no re_anchor block;
  • a group that never carried a lift is unchanged (21% spent after 22.0 h holds at 1.6× burn);
  • a lift carrying cleared_at and no stamp is backfilled on the first read — figure from the reading observed at the clear, not one taken after — the record is unchanged by a second read, and the pace then holds one point above the line that end anchors;
  • with no reading at or before the clear, the figure used is marked u_end_late and stamped with when it was taken.

Every test points RECKON_HOME at a temporary directory and asserts the workstation's own ~/.config/reckon/budget-lifts.json is byte-identical before and after, because a test that writes the live lift record is a monitor for the machine rather than a test of the code. All figures are derived from the above fixture at assertion time.

Checked against the live record, read-only

The document the re-anchor is for is the one it could not read. The workstation's own lift record was copied to a temporary home and the backfill run against the copy, so the live store was read and never written (its version still reads 16 after the check, asserted in the same run). On codex-sub the record the backfill picks is codex-sub-1792501561-9adb4a, cleared 2026-10-20T13:06:52Z inside a window that began 2026-10-20T06:32Z and resets 2026-10-27T06:32Z: it is stamped at that instant with u_end = 0.75 taken late, re_anchor_origin returns it as the group's newest end, and the allowance at 13:50Z reads 0.7512 — so the group is judged against its own remaining week rather than against spend the lift authorised. Two lapsed records from the previous window are deliberately left unstamped. Without the window-start guard they were stamped as well, which is how the guard was found.

Negative control

Removing the re-anchor branch from pace_hold (forcing re_anchored = False) fails the node's own gate: test_a_cleared_lift_re_anchors_the_hold_at_its_end_utilisation fails because the 75% read at 4% elapsed then admits through the unmeasured floor path and the 76% read admits with it, so the hold the re-anchor exists to impose is gone. 2 failed, 3 passed, exit 1. Restoring the branch returns the file to green.
