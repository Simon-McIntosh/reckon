A slow read names what it measured

A timed-out MCP read carried one label, storage-slow, whatever the deadline had caught. The read reported on 5 November held 13.1 s of CPU and 1.4 s runnable of its 30 s deadline: the node could not run it fast enough, while stat in home answered in 0.00 s and one task waited in cxiWaitEventWait. The label sent its reader to blame the mount.

The computing line moved from half the wall to a third, so 14.5 s of CPU and runnable time of 30 s reads as computing, and the error value follows the cause: a computing body reports read-slow, a blocked body keeps storage-slow. The three figures — CPU seconds, runnable seconds and wall seconds — were already measured and are carried beside the verdict.
