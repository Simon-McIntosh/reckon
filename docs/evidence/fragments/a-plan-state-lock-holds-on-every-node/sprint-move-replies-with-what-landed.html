A sprint move settles all-or-nothing and replies with what landed

The move wrote both sprint resources, then marked its journal committed and raised when that mark failed. The caller answered ok: false although both writes were on disk. The move now treats journal bookkeeping as bookkeeping: a failure there cannot undo landed writes, so the verdict and both versions are read back from the two sprint resources and returned, and the journal is retired best-effort with a warning when it cannot be.
