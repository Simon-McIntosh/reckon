§ — promoted review rows counted against the head they were dispatched to read

A read-only census reads every mounted project's committed ledger and counts the promoted run reviews whose recorded review.reviewed_head_sha is not the head the review was dispatched to read. The dispatched head is taken from the strongest source the row carries: a declared .at-<head> write path, read through the delivery code's own _review_record_subject, then the twelve-hexadecimal head suffix the node id mints, read back through the writer's own inverse round_node_source. A row whose recorded head does not begin with the dispatched head is mismatched; a row carrying no dispatched head from either source is unclassifiable.
