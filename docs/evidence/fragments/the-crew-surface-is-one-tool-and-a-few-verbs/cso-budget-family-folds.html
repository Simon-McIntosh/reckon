The budget verb family folds behind one parent

Change. Four sibling verbs become one crew budget command selected by flag, so the crew surface shrinks without any answer changing. The retired invocations and their folded forms are:

Retired verbFolded form
crew budget-liftcrew budget --lift
crew budget-liftscrew budget --list
crew budget-resetcrew budget --reset
crew preflightcrew budget --check

The four retired names answer through the tombstone registry in reckon/crew/tombstones.py. A tombstone gains a flags tuple: a folded family needs the successor's own flag ahead of the caller's arguments, because the caller's options alone do not select a case within the parent. So crew budget-lift --group g --multiple 1.8 prints the invocation reckon crew budget --lift --group g --multiple 1.8 and exits 10.

In reckon/crew_run_commands.py the three command bodies become helpers (_budget_reset_payload, _budget_lift_payload, _budget_lifts_payload) and one crew_budget command dispatches to them by flag. In reckon/crew_dispatch_commands.py the pre-flight command becomes _budget_check_report, a helper that composes the report and returns it with its exit status (3 when a backend is held), so the folded --check keeps pre-flight's exit code. reckon/cli.py re-exports the folded command in place of the four; reckon/crew/refusals.py and the skill and reference documents move their citations to the folded form.
