Disagreements counted

Four disagreements are counted. Three are with the owning coordinator's account and are wiring faults, all for one reason: the account is dated 2026-10-21 and describes 17 plans in sprint S3, while the snapshot describes 26 plans in sprint S4, so the plans have moved under the account. The fourth is between the base and head analysers on identical input and is a derivation defect.

  1. Critical path. The read names filter-recommendation-for-coil-health#s2; the coordinators named iter-switched-terminal-transients#s6. Wiring fault: the account is dated 2026-10-21 with 17 plans and sprint S3, while the snapshot holds 26 plans and sprint S4, so the two describe different plan sets and the account is stale rather than wrong.
  2. First-ranked item. Same pair of answers as above. Wiring fault, for the same reason.
  3. The undeclared wait. The coordinators said no plan declares a wait on shared-coil-supply-model#s4; the snapshot declares exactly that edge from coil-supply-report-rebuild#s21. Wiring fault, in the plans having changed since the account.
  4. The active sprint. On the identical snapshot the base brief prints No active sprint is declared. where the head brief prints Active sprint S4. Derivation defect, fixed between base and head: the sprint declaration is present in the data and the base analyser did not reach it.

Separately from those four, the why view's _waited_on_by enumeration never converges, so the question "what blocks a named section" has no answer on any mount. That is a derivation defect the plan set cannot explain, and it is the one item in this measure that needs a section of its own rather than a wiring repair.
