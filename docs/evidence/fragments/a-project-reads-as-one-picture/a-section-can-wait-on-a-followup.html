A section can wait on a followup

reckon/_schema.py extends the one plan-ref grammar to [project:]slug[#section][@followup]: PlanRef carries a followup beside stage, so a section can declare a wait on one followup of another plan (plan-section-depends-on holding {"s1": ["y@f-1"]}) rather than on the whole section that followup belongs to. In reckon/roadmap.py a followup ref resolves against the target plan's own followup records: the waiting section is held while that followup reads open and released when it is resolved, and a ref naming a followup the target does not carry is reported as dangling-hard-dependency while still holding its section. Section-scoped blockers are now classified by the section that declared them rather than by the presence of a section anchor, so a followup wait never vetoes the whole plan. PLAN-FORMAT.md, skills/reckon-edit/SKILL.md and the regenerated docs/_shared/plan.schema.json (written by write_json_schema(), never hand-edited) state the extended grammar in the same commit as the parser change, and every malformed-ref refusal names the @followup suffix. A ref that names both a section and a followup (y#s2@f-1) registers its section half as a declared wait, so prose naming y#s2 is not reported as an undeclared wait it had in fact declared, while a followup-only ref registers none.
