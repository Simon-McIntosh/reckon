Pending-plan limit at creation

The project configuration declares a pending-plan limit beside its schedule horizon, and a plan created while the project already holds that many pending plans is refused, naming the limit, the pending count, the three pending plans nearest to closing ranked by implementation, and the override. The override, a create-path argument carrying a reason, opens the plan and records the reason as a comment on the plan it opened. Pending is the velocity plan census's own closed definition, imported rather than re-derived, so the cap and the census cannot disagree. The refusal was reproduced by a declared negative control that skips the check and shows the third-create case failing.
