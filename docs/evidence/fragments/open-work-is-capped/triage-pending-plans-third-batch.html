Method

This is the third of four batches of seventeen plans staged against the open-work limit. The lead has locked that limit at twenty-five pending plans; four batches of seventeen cover the sixty-eight the sweep found, so a batch may propose at most six keeps. Nine of the seventeen are already at or near zero remaining work: they were dispatched alongside the first two batches and are either complete at head or superseded, so this batch spends its keep budget on the plans that still carry live, unfinished work.

A plan's denominator is its declared implementable section count, as reckon's own reader reports it: the plan-section-declarations head meta maps each authored section to done, implementable or deferred, and a section counts toward the denominator when it is declared implementable or done — §1 only when it is itself declared implementable. The command that prints each denominator, run read-only against the main checkout at head 032b03fc, is:

for S in consumers-read-reckon-through-verbs crew-observability-truth \

  git -C /home/ITER/mcintos/Code/reckon show "032b03fc:docs/plans/$S.html" \
  | python3 -c 'import sys,json,re;h=sys.stdin.read();m=re.search(r"plan-section-declarations\" content='"'"'(.*?)'"'"'",h,re.S);d=json.loads(m.group(1).replace(""","\""));print(sum(1 for k,v in d.items() if v!="deferred"))'
done

Presence is verified per section against the product tree at 032b03fc, never against a plan's own prose: every section counted present cites the file and symbol that carries it, located with git -C <worktree> grep -nE "<symbol>" 032b03fc -- <paths> and confirmed by reading the file at that revision. A section whose declared content is a scope exclusion or a framing statement — a rule about what the plan does not own — has no product symbol to cite; it is counted present when the plan declares it done and the exclusion is reflected in the tree, and the citation column records it as no deliverable. A test file alone is not a citation for a product deliverable. The remaining sections are counted absent and carry no citation.

Dispositions follow the batch rule: a plan whose deliverables are all present or no longer wanted is close, never keep; a plan whose remaining work belongs to another plan's subject is merge, naming the target. Every non-keep states why it is not in the top six.
