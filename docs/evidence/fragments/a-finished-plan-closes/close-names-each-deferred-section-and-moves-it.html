close names each deferred section and moves it

close still refuses a plan holding a deferred section, but the refusal now names both ways to clear one: a descoped declaration carrying a reason, or a descoped declaration carrying moved_to, the successor section that now owns the work. The second is a move, not a drop.

What the refusal says

_require_every_section_ticked in reckon/_store.py names every open section and then both remedies: {"status": "descoped", "reason": "<why>"} or {"status": "descoped", "reason": "<why>", "moved_to": "<plan>#<section>"}. On the base commit the text names neither, and the fixture's close test fails there.

What a move carries

The moved_to ref must resolve to a section the successor plan declares, or the write is refused before anything is written. The successor section then receives the moved section's authored prose, its effort_hours, its capability requirement (each dimension raised to the stricter of the two) and its open gates, and lists the move as an incoming link. The successor's half is a second plan write that lands only after the source plan has landed, so a refused source write moves nothing. The moved section's comments are re-anchored on the c-move-<section> record the write leaves on the closed plan, and no gate or comment on either plan names a section it does not declare.
