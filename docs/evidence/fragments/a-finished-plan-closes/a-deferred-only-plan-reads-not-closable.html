Every progress figure drawn from that figure reads complete — the roadmap row said 100 %, the crew flow drew a finished bar attached to the now line, and the plan card printed 100 % — while close still refused it on the two deferred sections. The surfaces were stating a finish the plan had not reached.

The fact is now derived once, in deferred_remainder beside closure_blockers in reckon/roadmap.py. Every other plan — no declarations, implementable work still admitted, or no deferred section — gets None, and the surfaces fall back to their progress figure.
