Shown on the fleet: the incident's call fails and the fleet survives a dead supervisor

A dispatched worker replayed the incident's three-argument call at a decoy chain of the incident's depth — directly, through sbatch, through srun and by the scheduler binary's absolute path — and the redirect was refused in all four, the decoy interpreter byte-identical afterwards. The same redirect at a decoy in a granted directory was refused by the file's own mode while a writable neighbour in that directory was written. The fleet health series read zero writable interpreters on every sample of the last 24 hours, and an allocation outlived two SIGKILLs of the supervisor it started. The census of worker-written gate wrappers does not hold, so the section stays open.

What was measured

  • The incident's call, three routes plus the absolute path. One all_debug job (1343880) composed the product's own reckon._worker_fence.fence_argv around a decoy chain of the incident's depth (worktree .venv → decoy environment → bin/python3 → decoy interpreter). The worker's gate shell wrote its $3 as a "log", which is the incident's argument shuffle. Every route returned the wrapper's own stderr, .../.venv/bin/python: Read-only file system, and the decoy's size, nanosecond mtime and sha256 were unchanged in all four. The srun route created no scheduler step — the shim re-entered the fence — and the absolute path reached the shim, which is §15's shield doing its job from a worker's own call.
  • The file's own permission, apart from the fence. Job 1343916: the same wrapper redirected onto a 0555 decoy in a granted directory and got Permission denied, while a 0755 decoy of the same shape in the same directory was replaced and a plain write in that directory succeeded. The pair attributes the refusal to the mode, not to the fence.
  • Health series, 24 hours. 1,169 samples ending 2026-11-24T08:44:10Z: 1,166 read 0 writable interpreters, 3 read unknown (the sampler said so rather than reporting a repair), and none read above zero.
  • An allocation outliving its supervisor. Job 1343953 ran the product's own fleet_node.generate_hold_script as its batch step with a replaceable Python supervisor. Two SIGKILLs produced three starts with the product's 5 s then 10 s backoff, the stand-in descendant stayed alive, and the queue state read RUNNING at every sample. The gated rehearsal's live srun step cannot be composed from inside a worker fence; two attempts record that boundary rather than hiding it.
  • The scoped gate at f584a969aea2: 28 passed, 1 skipped, 5 deselected in 26.21s, EXIT=0 (job 1343980), over the decoy-replay and supervisor-lifetime files this record's arms measure, with the scheduler-gated arms excluded because they need a scheduler root the arms supply themselves.
  • The census fails. Over the 200 run directories created after 0388ce623c, 11.0 % hold a worker-written gate-invoking script and 13.5 % any worker-written script, against 5.0 % and 7.0 % in the 200 before it. The section asks for under 5 %. The wrappers remaining are mostly base-versus-head measurement arms of other plans — the incident's shape almost exactly — and the original census tool is gone with its pruned run directory, so the section's 43-of-200 baseline cannot be reproduced by either instrument.
