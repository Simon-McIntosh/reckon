Group pacing reads the live account window

The account surface is now read beside the recorded receipts and the freshest dated reading of the two speaks for the group, so a dispatch is paced by the window the provider reports now rather than by a receipt from a window that has already reset. Previously a rolled-over weekly receipt paced the whole window (derived 1.0) and admitted an implement dispatch; the fresh account-surface reading paces by its own elapsed fraction.

What the change does

  • The dispatch pace record (budget.pace_row) merges the account surface with the recorded evidence, keeping the newest dated reading.
  • Any positive provider-reported window length is retained on the account-surface and rollout paths; a 1,440-minute window is no longer dropped and paced by its own elapsed fraction.
  • The bar is drawn from a primary-only account's operative window rather than reading unknown, so an account with no five-hour sibling still fills the bar.
  • A role-aware budget.pace_hold withholds non-bookend dispatch while the window stands above its elapsed allowance, naming utilisation, allowance and reset; the review and verify roles the reserve protects are admitted.

Two days into a 43,200-minute fixture the allowance is 7.33%; a 1,440-minute fixture at 25% elapsed allows 0.275 rather than being dropped.
