Codex pacing follows the provider window

The account reader now retains a long primary window even when it is neither the historical five-hour nor seven-day period. A primary-only account derives its cumulative allowance from the elapsed fraction of that window multiplied by budget.pace_multiple. Accounts that publish both historical clocks keep the existing next-window derivation. The operative reading is also supplied to the bookend reserve, so a missing five-hour sibling is not described as an unreadable account.
