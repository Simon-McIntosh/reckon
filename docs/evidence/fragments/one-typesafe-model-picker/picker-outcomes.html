Rules and limits

Success is a passed gate, or, without a closed gate, an outcome explicitly saying passed, complete, done, success or review scored. A failed gate fails. Other outcomes remain unknown and are excluded from rate denominators. The p50 latency is a median; p90 uses nearest rank. Repair or resume counts attempt kinds repair, resume and redispatch, or a recorded remedy.

The promoted ledger omits dispatch route mode. A matching shadow selection can therefore still enter the matched cohort; a route selection that launched another backend is excluded. The route mode needs a durable ledger field before causal attribution is exact. The 49 fallback rows all name timeout, while codex burn and pace values are absent in offered candidates. These absences are visible in the JSON and are not interpreted as zero spend or zero pressure.
