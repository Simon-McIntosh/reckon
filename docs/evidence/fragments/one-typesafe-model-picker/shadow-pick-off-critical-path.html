Explicit lane shadow pick after launch

Deterministic and explicitly named lanes launch without awaiting their advisory picker. A detached process bounds input construction and the pick to five seconds, then writes its selection or fallback to the live run pointer. Picker-routed dispatches still resolve their pick before launch. The launcher now merges its supervisor PID under the pointer lock, preserving a phase the supervisor may already have published. The supervisor and shadow picker check that the run still exists when they write, so a discard does not leave a recreated pointer.
