The per-test reaper reaches a producer that never wrote its seat record

The per-test reaper in tests/conftest.py found producers only through their seat records (reapable_watch_pids). A producer a test armed but which had not yet written its record when the test ended was never signalled; it survived to fail the session-end leaked_watch_producers scan at teardown, which is the load-dependent failure recorded on this node. The repair adds unrecorded_watch_producers_naming and per_test_reap_pids: the per-test reap is now the union of the record-based pids and the live crew watch producers whose own /proc/<pid>/environ names a RECKON_HOME under that test's tmp_path. A process whose environment cannot be read, or that names any other home, is still left alone; the session-end scan and its failure are unchanged as the backstop.

The new test tests/test_reaper_catches_unrecorded_producer.py arms a stand-in producer whose argv and environment match _live_watch_producers, lies under the test's tree, and writes no seat record. One arm shows the defect (the record reap returns nothing while the producer is live); the other shows the repaired reap signals it and reads the child's own exit as -SIGTERM (exit 15).

Gate: the new file, test_a_review_never_runs_in_harness.py, and the files naming reap_watch_producers, reapable_watch_pids, leaked_watch_producers or preserve_seat_records — 18 passed / 1 skipped at head against 17 passed / 1 failed / 1 skipped at base (the one failure is the new test against the unfixed reaper). Negative control: restrict the reaper to record-listed pids again and the unrecorded-stand-in test fails.
