A review is earned by significant change, measured per unit

Plan review now covers a unit — an authored section, or the document unit that carries decisions, gates and dependencies — by how much its prose changed since the review that last read it, not by any edit at all. reckon/crew/plan_review.py gains _review_coverage_report, the three-value predicate (§21's two-value _review_coverage is kept beside it for its existing callers): it compares each outstanding unit's present prose against the newest covering review's snapshot — exactly, through the digest recomputed from that snapshot, or within the declared fraction of the unit's words — and reports the uncovered units with their measured share. The word share is difflib.SequenceMatcher over dispatch._normalised_words, so the measure adds a comparison and no second tokeniser and both texts come from the one reader the digests use (_prose_texts). Three cases stay material whatever their size: a changed done-when (located by _done_when_paragraph and compared whitespace-normalised), a section that became implementable since the review (read from the snapshot's own declarations), and a unit absent from the review — a new section above all. A review with no snapshot covers only an exact digest match, its threshold read as zero.

The threshold is review.plan_change_threshold: 0.30, declared in reckon/schema/flight-defaults.yaml and read through the new public reckon/flight.py:plan_review_change_threshold. The schema cleaner and validate_layer now read one table, flight.REVIEW_OWNED_KEYS — plan_settle_seconds a non-negative integer, plan_change_threshold a number in [0, 1] — so a key added to one is set aside by the cleaner and checked by the validator in the same edit. The gate refusal in reckon/crew/routing.py names each uncovered unit beside its measured change (for example b changed by 35% of its words).
