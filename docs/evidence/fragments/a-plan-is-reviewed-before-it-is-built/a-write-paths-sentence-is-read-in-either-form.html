A section's write-paths label is read with a colon or a full stop

What changed. _WRITE_PATHS_SENTENCE in reckon/crew/plan_review.py matched only a colon after “Write path” or “Write paths”, so a section authored with the equally common Write paths. lead-in handed no paths to either of its readers: the section reader (code_review_paths_from_plan) and the batch entry reader (_PlanRead.write_paths in reckon/crew/dispatch_batch.py, which imports the same pattern). A crew dispatch --batch entry that omitted write_paths was therefore refused with “the section states no write-paths sentence and the entry gives none” for a section that states them. The pattern now accepts either punctuation, so both readers take the sentence once.

Audit coverage. reckon audit-doc gains a write-paths-sentence-unparsed warning: a plan section that opens a paragraph with the write-paths label in a form neither reader parses is reported when the plan is audited, rather than surfacing as a refused dispatch. The subject is the label sentence, not the phrase: a section that discusses write paths in prose raises nothing, and neither does a section whose prose already yields paths through the section reader. Over docs/plans and docs/evidence the check reports 296 and 274 sections on any prose mention, and 0 and 0 once it is narrowed to an unparseable label sentence.

Measured

The new test holds eight cases. A full-stop section yields its code-formatted path through the section reader and through the batch reader; the colon form still yields its paths through both; a batch entry for a full-stop section with no write_paths queues carrying the section's paths; and audit-doc warns on a label in neither form while accepting both parseable forms. The eighth case is the narrowing the review asked for: a section that mentions write paths in prose beside a parseable label raises no warning.
