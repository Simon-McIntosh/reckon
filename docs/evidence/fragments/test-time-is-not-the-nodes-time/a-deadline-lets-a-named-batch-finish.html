The rule, stated once

At the deadline a worker writes the checkpoint — the manifest's status, checkpoint and remaining work current on disk — and may continue only through a bounded step the manifest named before the deadline: one command under its own timeout, or a stated number of them. It finishes that step by the hard stop, updates the manifest and ends the turn. It starts no new work, fresh fix or unnamed step past the deadline. The hard stop is the launch instant plus the budget times fences.budget_grace_multiple; when that multiple is missing, invalid or at most 1.0 the hard stop is the deadline and no continuation is offered.

The sentence "Exceeding it means stop and report, never push on." is gone from every composed prompt, and the stop list now names the hard stop and unnamed remaining work in place of "the time budget is spent". time_fence_statement states the deadline, the hard stop and the continuation rule beside them; compose_prompt carries a grace_multiple keyword from the same fences block as needs_help_after_failures; a resumed or lane-changed attempt restates the hard stop from its own launch through _restate_time_fence.

One owner for the multiple

resolved_grace_multiple in reckon/crew/routing.py returns the configured number, 1.0 when the key is absent, and nothing when the value is not numeric. The prompt and the opt-in watchdog both read it, so the instant they act on cannot drift: _apply_budget_watchdog calls it in place of its own fences.get("budget_grace_multiple", 1.0) read. A non-numeric value leaves the watchdog inert and the prompt offering no continuation, exactly as an invalid value did before.
