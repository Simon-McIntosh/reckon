The browser harness finds the browser that is installed

installed_browser() resolves, in order, RECKON_BROWSER, the three names on PATH, and Playwright's Chromium headless shell under $PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH or ~/.cache/ms-playwright/chromium_headless_shell-*/chrome-headless-shell-linux64/chrome-headless-shell, newest installed revision first. Its skip message names every location it checked.

At the base revision (fef8ae2da) the population skipped 66 times with no supported browser binary is installed; at head (c2c060c0) the same population runs with no such skip.
