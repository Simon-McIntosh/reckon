A figure review is complete by its own rubric

What changed. The delivery check in reckon/crew/recovery_review_delivery.py judged every stored review record against the six landed-node score dimensions. A figure review records a verdict per item of its own rubric instead, so a figure record that carried all six of its measures was refused at its first promotion as an incomplete review, naming six dimensions it was never asked for. The check now reads the record's rubric through the same reader crew check --review applies: a figure record is complete when it carries a non-blank verdict for every item of FIGURE_REVIEW_ITEMS, and its refusal names the figure item it lacks. Every other record keeps the completeness rule the shared reader owns, so a text record and a claims record are judged as before. A rubric_items value that is not a mapping reports every item missing rather than raising, because the check runs on classification paths that must not take the pass down with them.
