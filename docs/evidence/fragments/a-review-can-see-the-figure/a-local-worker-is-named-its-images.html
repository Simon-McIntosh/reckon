A lane that declares image input is named its figures instead of refusing them

A claude-family lane that declares image in its input_modalities is now handed its attached figures in the prompt it reads rather than refused. launch_plan in reckon/_backends.py keys the decision on the lane's own declaration, never on the dialect: when the declaration admits an image and the dialect carries no attach flag, _prompt_naming_images appends each path with an instruction to open it with the Read tool, and the paths are kept off argv — the channel decision, made once, at the one place all three launch paths compose. A lane that declares nothing reaches the dialect unchanged and still refuses by name, which is what keeps the measured lane and the unmeasured lane beside it in one flag grammar distinguishable. The codex dialect is untouched: it carries images on its own -i flag and an undeclared codex lane still emits it.

A resume rebuilds the run's backend through _backend_settings in reckon/crew/dispatch_sessions.py, which re-applied only the window, model and effort from the configured lane and so dropped the declaration; it now carries input_modalities the same way, and the resumed launch names the same run-local PNGs the dispatch recorded.

docs/state/reckon/flight.yaml declares input_modalities: [text, image] on clive, citing the two 2026-10-21 local-lane probe runs and their matching node. The claude lane and every other claude-family lane stay undeclared until measured.
