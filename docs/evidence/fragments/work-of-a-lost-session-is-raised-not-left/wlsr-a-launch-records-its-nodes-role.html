A launch records its node's role

The launch's recorded treatment did not carry the role at all. WorkerBehaviour.dimensions, which is what a launch plan records and what a reader compares, named the lane, the inbox, the allowlist, the fence roots, the review duty and the landing route — and left the role out. Every review launch therefore read from its own record as an implement run: it owed a review of its own and declared the implement landing route, and no caller could have corrected that by passing a role because there was nowhere for it to land.

Two changes close it. The role is a recorded dimension. And the three routes that compose a worker launch pass the role the node was dispatched with: _launch_dispatch in reckon/crew/dispatching/launch.py reads node.role, which also serves the queued admission launch because _launch_queued hands its rebuilt node to the same dispatch; resume_plan and change_lane in reckon/crew/dispatch_sessions.py read it through one _recorded_role helper that prefers the recorded node and falls back to the pointer root. A run carries the role it was dispatched with, so a later configuration change cannot restate what an earlier attempt owed.

Each route is driven through its real door and the treatment read off the composed launch. A direct dispatch of a review node records role: review, review_duty: false and reviewed-under-its-lane. A queued request carrying a review node, launched by one admission pass, records the same. A stopped run whose record names the review role, resumed through resume_plan, records the same. An implement node on each of the three routes records role: implement, review_duty: true and promoted-under-its-lane.
