Promotion serialises its landing independently of pointer access

Commit 489d008e8e72bab60b193fac154836331b051c18 gives promotion a separate locks/promotion-<run>.lock flock and retains its renewable cross-host lease. Checks and waiter release retain those promotion claims while the run's pointer lock protects only its snapshot and atomic updates. Retirement rereads the pointer under its lock and refuses to delete an attempt whose identity changed during promotion. Withdrawal uses that same retirement, then releases its workspace outside the pointer lock; suite refusals mutate the current pointer so concurrent fields survive. Discard takes the promotion lock too, preserving its serialisation with promotion.
