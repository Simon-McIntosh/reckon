The lander leaves a run whose own evidence is red to its coordinator

What changed. The admission lander promoted a delivered run whose own manifest recorded an after_suite exit status of 1 with a failure its baseline did not name, and whose review carried added_failure_count: 1 and five open findings. It recorded gate passed and merged the red test onto the primary branch with no coordinator action, because the lander judged a run on its own measurement and the review's acceptance rule exempted its non-blocking findings itself.

reckon/crew/queue_landing.py now reads the run's own evidence before it measures anything, and records the existing refused status with a red-suite guard when that evidence is red or unreadable. Three arms state it. _manifest_suite_guard reads the manifest's baseline_suite/after_suite pair: _after_suite_arm reports an arm that is absent, that does not declare completed: true, or that records no exit_status as unreadable rather than clean; _after_suite_exit_guard refuses a nonzero exit; _after_suite_added_guard refuses a failure id the baseline does not name. _review_added_failure_guard refuses a review recording added_failure_count above zero, and _undisposed_finding_guard refuses a finding with no disposition. A manifest recording neither suite arm states nothing, so a run whose node armed no suite pair is judged on the pass's own measurement exactly as before.

A refusal is judged again when the record it read changes, so the suite arms now enter the refusal's review stamp through _suite_evidence_stamp: a coordinator who records the missing observation releases the refusal on the next pass rather than leaving it standing over evidence that has moved. No status was added — obligations.py already reports a refused admission_landing as landing-refused naming the guard, and the lander's skip path is unchanged.

Superseded behaviour. A review carrying nothing blocking used to land with its non-blocking findings exempted by the pass itself. That exemption was the pass's own judgement rather than the reviewer's or a coordinator's, so an undisposed finding now holds the run. Three assertions in tests/test_a_run_a_review_does_not_block_lands.py stated the old behaviour and were updated to the new contract: the undisposed case asserts the refusal, and the two landing cases dispose their findings first.
