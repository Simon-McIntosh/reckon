The landing pass runs beside the admission loop, bounded

What changed

reckon/crew/queue_admission.py stopped calling land_once between passes. _LandingWorker runs the landing pass in a thread of its own, named reckon-landing, under a lock taken without blocking: a tick that finds an action in flight starts nothing, so at most one landing action exists at a time and a slow landing delays only the next landing. An action that raises is recorded on the worker as an error result and the next tick starts a fresh one, so a landing defect never ends the service that runs the launches. --once still waits for the landing it starts, because a manual pass reports what it did; the service loop does not.

Each pass record now carries started_at, ended_at and release_seconds, launch_seconds and land_seconds — the three stages are held by different things (a lane slot, a merge, a suite), and one figure would not name which stalled. The landing action's own record carries its started_at, ended_at and seconds.

reckon/crew/queue_landing.py: _land bounds its srun step with the landing measure's timeout (_land_timeout, resolving the run's pointer and the flight config, defaulting to the measure's own default when the pointer cannot be read). subprocess.TimeoutExpired becomes a refusal whose guard names the bound and the stopped command, and land_once records it as refused on the live pointer with an owner obligation. _land_command passes --no-impl-change with LANDING_BEAT_REASON: the landing beat that advances the run's section runs only after the merge, so the plan's impl has not moved when crew complete judges the run, and the pass refused its own deliveries without it.

The six cases

  • An admission pass completes and releases a waiter while a landing it does not call is blocked on a patched _land; the loop runs in a thread and must return while the landing is still held.
  • A landing tick that finds three accepted deliveries runs one land and defers the other two, one landed per later tick.
  • A land step past its bound is stopped inside the bound and recorded as refused with the bound in the guard.
  • Each pass record carries started_at, ended_at and numeric release_seconds, launch_seconds and land_seconds, and the landing action its own start, end and duration.
  • A landing that raises is recorded on the worker, restarted on the next tick, and never stops the loop.
  • land_once over a run whose section is still implementable lands it with --no-impl-change naming the landing beat, and the section is then ticked done.
