A slow station still reads as alive

The station's heartbeat was written by the tick, after classify_tick, and the rest of a tick walks every mounted project across ledger reads and realpath calls on GPFS. On the night of 29 October a tick took about eight minutes against a 120-second stale window, so for most of each tick every follower and hook read the station as stopped and told its session to restart a process five py-spy samples had watched moving. Liveness and progress are now separate facts. StationBeat writes the heartbeat from a thread of its own, on its own store handle, once every STATION_BEAT_SECONDS (5 s) for as long as the process lives, and run_station starts it before the first tick. Each tick records the stage it has entered — store-tick, classify, heartbeat, per-project-refresh, publish — and its start instant in the store, and stamps a finish when it returns; the stage is written before the work it names, so a stage a reader sees is the one the tick has not yet finished.

The reader is obligation_snapshot.read_station_answer, which now answers a state beside the payload: a beat past the window is stopped, a fresh beat with a tick whose start is later than its last finish and older than the window is slow, and everything else is live. It also returns the tick's tick_stage and tick_age_seconds. The hook (reckon/hooks/coordinator_obligations.py) keeps stale_station_line — the reckon crew watch --ensure restart advice — for a stopped station only, and heads a slow station's checklist with slow_station_note, naming the stage and that stage's age and carrying no remedy. An exported snapshot names no tick stage, so an off-node reading is only ever stopped or live; it never claims progress it cannot see.

Measure: tests/test_a_slow_station_still_reads_as_alive.py, three arms, window shortened to 1.2 s and the beat to 0.05 s through the same constants the reader uses. A station started as its own process with its per-project publish blocked for three windows leaves a fresh beat (age ≤ the window) beside a tick aged ≥ three windows and reads slow with stage per-project-refresh; a station whose one tick has finished and whose beat is then read one window later reads stopped with no running age; and the prompt hook, driven as the harness drives it, emits the restart advice for the stopped station and never for the slow one, where the injected header carries the stage name and the age instead. Gate: 3 passed at head, EXIT=0. Negative control (the beat left to the tick instead of the thread): 1 failed, 2 passed, EXIT=1, the first arm reading state: stopped at heartbeat_age_seconds: 4 against the 1.2 s window. Importer population (18 test files importing reckon.crew.station or reckon.crew.obligation_snapshot): 127 passed, 2 failed at head; 124 passed, 2 failed at base — zero added failures, the two shared ones being a hook import-surface arm and a reload-image fixture directory left in /tmp by an earlier run. ruff check adds no finding on the changed paths (two pre-existing S105 findings in the hook remain) and ruff format --check passes on all four.
