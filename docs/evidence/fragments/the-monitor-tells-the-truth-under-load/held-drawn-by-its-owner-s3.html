What changed

The pane's word set files held under "needs you" while the counter counted every held classification as waiting, so a request the queue itself had parked — on a live run's claim, on the session's lane allowance, on a failed attempt's retry back-off — was drawn "needs you" with nothing to do, and was counted somewhere the word did not say. One derived fact now answers who lifts a hold, and the word, the counter bucket and the recovery verb all read it.

reckon/crew/recovery_watch.py gains hold_owner, which reads the hold's own record: queue_hold.automatic marks a hold the admission pass releases when its condition clears, a hold written by crew hold carries no such mark and only a coordinator releases it, and the one automatic hold whose lifting condition is a coordinator act is the owed plan review (plan-review-owed), whose release is composing and storing that review. _watch_verdict draws the row from that answer: a queue-parked request's state is waiting, so it sits in the waiting pane and its classification and verb agree with the word; a coordinator's hold stays held, drawn "needs you", and its row carries the act that lifts it — reckon crew queue release --run <id>, or the reckon crew review invocation for an owed plan review, never the release that would launch work over unreviewed plan text.

reckon/crew/ticker.py drops the classification test from _bucket_of, so a row is counted in the bucket whose word it is drawn with, and held joins NEEDS_ACTION so a coordinator's hold is counted under "needs you" and keeps its reason clause. _fleet_counts loses its own held special case for the same reason: the partition is taken from the state word alone.
