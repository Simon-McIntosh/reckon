What changed

The pane's word set files held under "needs you" while the counter counted every held classification as waiting, so a request the queue itself had parked — on a live run's claim, on the session's lane allowance, on a failed attempt's retry back-off — was drawn "needs you" with nothing to do, and the two surfaces disagreed about every held row. One derived fact now answers whose move the hold is, and the word and the counter bucket both read it.

reckon/crew/recovery_watch.py gains hold_owner, which reads the hold's own record: queue_hold.automatic marks a hold the admission pass releases when its condition clears, a hold written by crew hold carries no such mark and only a coordinator releases it, and the one automatic hold whose lifting condition is a coordinator act is the owed plan review (plan-review-owed), which lifts when a review of that plan is composed and stored. _watch_verdict draws the row from that answer: every hold the lane or the clock lifts — a parked request, a run the lane's window parks, a run stopped on a spent quota or a retry back-off — takes the waiting word, which is the pane it belongs in and the cell it is counted in, and its row hands no command: the release the classifier names for a held pointer would launch over the admission pass's own release path, and the wait ends without it.

A coordinator's hold stays held, drawn "needs you", and its row carries the act that lifts it — the classifier's own next_action for that pointer (reckon crew queue release --run <id>), read off the row rather than composed again in the pane, or the reckon crew review invocation for an owed plan review, never the release that would launch work over unreviewed plan text.

reckon/crew/ticker.py drops the classification test from _bucket_of, so a row is counted in the bucket whose word it is drawn with, and held joins NEEDS_ACTION so a coordinator's hold is counted under "needs you" and keeps its reason clause. _fleet_counts takes its n and q figures from that same helper rather than restating the partition beside it, so a regression in the bucket moves a count. A regression in either is a failing case here, not a rendering detail.
