A launch cut off before its worker releases the review claim it holds

The launch reap already worked at the base revision: a pointer held in a pre-spawn phase with no worker record, stream or launch log is dated from its claim and released by the admission pass, and drawn in the launch-failed family. The surviving defect was the review arm. A composed review whose launch was cut off left its own pointer at starting forever, and the review reflex answered the in-flight read only after the reviewed run's own classification gate. That gate read the reviewed run as running, so the reflex returned "the run is not awaiting review" and never reached the release, and the review was never composed again.

What changed

The standing review claim is now answered before the reviewed run's own reading is judged (_standing_review_report in reckon/crew/recovery_review_dispatch.py). A claim whose pointer has held a pre-spawn phase past the one stranded-launch bound (STRANDED_LAUNCH_BOUND_SECONDS) with no worker record is discarded with the reason recorded on its departure marker and on the reviewed run's dispatch record, and the claim no longer returns in flight. A claim inside that bound is still a young launch and is reported in flight.

Measure

The two review-arm tests in tests/test_stranded_launch_releases_its_claim.py fail at the base revision c00ffd3d20 and pass at the head; the other two cases in that file — the stranded and the young classification reads — were green before and stay green. tests/test_a_launch_stopped_at_its_claim_is_redispatched.py stays green. The failing assertion at base was the released pointer still present on disk, and the in-flight case reporting "the run is not awaiting review (running)" instead of the standing review it names.

The 112 test files importing reckon.crew.recovery_review_dispatch or reckon.crew.recovery_liveness were run over the whole population at both revisions: 29 failed, 785 passed, 1 skipped and 3 errored at the head, and every one of those 32 failing or erroring ids also fails at the base revision — no failure is added. One case moves the other way: tests/test_a_review_is_not_refused_for_its_run.py::test_a_never_launched_stranded_review_keeps_its_strand_reason fails at base and passes at the head, which is the release reached at all.
