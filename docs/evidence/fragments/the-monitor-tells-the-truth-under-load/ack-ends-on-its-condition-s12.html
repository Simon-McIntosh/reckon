The records remain in the existing acknowledgement stores.

The hook reconsiders both open and acknowledged snapshot rows. While a deferral holds, its reason and end condition are shown and it contributes no blocking duty. Once it ends, the original duty and reason return without a new snapshot. A stable acknowledgement does not repeat merely because its age advances.
