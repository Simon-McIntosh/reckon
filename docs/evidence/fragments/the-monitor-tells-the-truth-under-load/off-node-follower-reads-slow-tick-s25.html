Off-node followers keep reading a live station

The station heartbeat now publishes its four health markers independently of obligation derivation. One small atomic record per host and allocation carries the beat, tick stage, start and finish. An exported obligation reading uses only a matching record whose beat is at least as recent as its own. Missing, damaged, mismatched and older records retain the export's original beat. Obligation documents and their timestamps stay unchanged.

The follower keeps that exported health reading when its node cannot open the station store. Local and off-node readers therefore report the same slow tick, with no lost row and no re-arm remedy. A stopped heartbeat still draws a lost row and its remedy on both readers.
