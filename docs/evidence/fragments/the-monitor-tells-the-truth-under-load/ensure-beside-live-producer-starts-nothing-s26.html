An ensure reuses the serving producer

The watcher ensure reads the existing producer after stale-seat recovery and returns started: false, path: existing, and the producer's host and pid. It starts neither a service nor a fallback process beside a live producer, even when the local unit is inactive, changed, explicitly requested to restart, unreachable, or unable to enable lingering. With no producer, the service still starts and reports started: true. The unit definition can still be written; cycling a serving producer requires releasing its seat first.
