A worker cannot remove another run's scratch

What changed. One line of product behaviour was already correct and one fixture was not. The fence seals the shared scratch root read-only and re-binds only the run's own scratch directory writable after it, so a sibling run's scratch is readable but cannot be removed. The executed case of this node's test could not observe that, because the fixture let pytest's temporary root decide where its synthetic tree lived: on a compute node that root sits under /tmp, the fence seals the node tmp and — with the run's scratch beneath it — mounts that scratch over /tmp, so the fixture's stub read as absent inside the fence and bwrap refused the exec before the stub ran at all.

The reproduction, at base (5b0a1f537ef9bbca90907bd16f663732db844fb0): the file ran 1 failed, 2 passed, the failure being tests/test_a_worker_cannot_remove_another_runs_scratch.py::test_a_fenced_run_cannot_remove_a_siblings_scratch with FileNotFoundError on …/runs/r-20261016T120000000000-under-test/results.txt — the stub never wrote its record. Composing the same fence by hand showed the receipt behind it: bwrap: execvp …/stub.sh: No such file or directory, because the composed argv carried --bind …/reckon-crew-scratch over /tmp and the stub lived on the host tmp outside it.

Verdict: the test, not the product. The node-tmp seal and the mount of the run's scratch over the node tmp are the product's rule, documented in fence_argv, worker_tmp_mount_source and node_tmp_seal_root, and pinned by the node-tmp and store suites. What the fixture needed was to stop depending on the host /tmp. Scratch.point_the_scratch_root now pins NODE_TMP_ROOT and node_tmp_runtime_paths inside its own tree, beside the synthetic scratch root, exactly as those suites already do. No assertion was deleted or weakened: the two shape assertions and the executed refusal are unchanged, and the negative control still turns the executed case red.
