The pane's counter census reads the seven-cell block it prints

What changed. tests/test_the_pane_speaks_seven_words.py pins the counter block's cells against the pane's state words. The block grew a seventh cell when reckon/crew/ticker.py began to tell requested promotions apart from delivered work: _PROMOTION_CELL = "promotion queue". The cell is a figure printed beside the pane words, like the stuck alarm, not a state word of its own — PANE_STATES["queued"] holds promotion-queued, so a promotion-queued run still reads queued on the transition. The census asserted the six-cell table and failed on the seventh.

The cause, and its verdict. The product is right: the cell carries a letter of its own (p), a count field of its own (promotion_queued) and the oldest request's position after an @, and it is pinned by the cases the product's own commit added. The test was wrong: it encoded "only stuck stands beside the pane words", a rule the product no longer keeps. The repair states the rule the pane now keeps — the counter cells are the pane words plus two figures, the stuck alarm and the promotion queue — and pins the cell tuple and the count field exactly, so no assertion is deleted or weakened.
