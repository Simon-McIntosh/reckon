The repair, and the four causes it covers

The evidence a spawned worker leaves — a pid, a budget folded from that worker's stream, the supervisor's worker record, a stream, or the launch log — is now one predicate, _spawn_evidence, in reckon/crew/recovery_liveness.py. classify_pointer reads it before granting the pre-spawn reading, so a launch that reached a worker takes the liveness arms and its refusal, its pointer's manifest and its process decide the row, while a launch that has spawned nothing keeps the running reading that replaced the UnboundLocalError of 14 November. The stranded-launch test reads the same predicate, so its four evidence checks are stated once rather than twice.

One cause, four readings, each repaired at that one point:

  • A provider refusal (blocked): 7 ids — six through classify_pointer and one through the ticker's _watch_snapshot. The refusal stream now reaches _refusal_block whatever the stored phase, and that fold also supplies the resume remedy one of them reads (KeyError: 'resume_remedy'), which only the blocked arm attaches.
  • A present but unparseable manifest (unreadable): 8 ids — seven through the classifier and one through the ticker. The manifest read runs, and the liveness gate admits the parse error as a condition of the delivery.
  • No manifest and a dead process (abandoned): 3 ids. The liveness arms decide, and a dead run on an unreadable manifest stays unreadable rather than joining them.
  • The three-outcome discrimination: 1 id, which asserts all three words at once and so failed on whichever arm won the gate; it reads the three distinct outcomes again.

The two ticker ids are the same cause read on the second surface, not a second cause: the snapshot's state is the classifier's fleet_verdict, so once that verdict is the classifier's own, the reduction says blocked or unreadable where the classifier does instead of collapsing both to abandoned.

No test was edited, no assertion deleted or weakened: every one of the nineteen ids is a pre-existing case that encoded the rule the product had stopped keeping.
