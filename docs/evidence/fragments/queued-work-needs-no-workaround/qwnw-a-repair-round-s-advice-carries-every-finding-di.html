A repair round's advice carries every finding disposed into it

A repair round composed its advice from the review's open blocking findings and its sub-floor dimensions. A finding the coordinator disposed as folded:<node> is a closing disposition, so the filter that selects open work dropped it before the advice was written, and the node the finding was folded into was composed with no account of it. The advice now lists those findings beside the open ones, names their ids in its done-when and exposes them on the composed node, while the round that blocks nothing still composes no repair.

Why the fold was dropped

compose_repair_node built its list from blocking_findings(review) + sub_floor_repair_findings(review, floors) (reckon/crew/repair.py:772 at the base revision), and blocking_findings (repair.py:226) keeps a finding only when its disposition reads open. A fold is a closing disposition (reckon/crew/findings.py:60), so the finding was removed from the one list the brief, the done-when ids and the composed node are all built from.

The composer returns nothing at all when no blocking finding remains (repair.py:776), which is the deliberate shape of a decline-only round: a review whose findings were all non-blocking composes no repair. The change therefore widens the advice, not that gate, so a decline-only round behaves exactly as before.

What changed

  • folded_findings(review, *, node_ids) reads each finding's disposition through finding_disposition and parse_finding_disposition and returns the findings folded into the reviewed run's node or into the repair node composed for the round. A fold naming another node, an exemption and an open finding are not carried.
  • compose_repair_brief takes a folded sequence and lists those findings under a sentence saying they were folded into this node and are answered the same way as the open work.
  • compose_repair_node composes the brief with the folded list, builds the done-when ids over blocking plus folded, and exposes folded_findings on the returned node. A folded finding adds no write path, because the scope stays what repair_write_scope grants.
  • tests/test_repair_advice_carries_every_finding.py stores a review whose only blocking finding is a dimension under its floor, folds two non-blocking findings into the run's node, and reads all three ids and texts out of the advice. Two further cases hold the boundary: a fold naming another node stays out, and an exempted finding carries no work.

Notes for the coordinator

  • Which node id a fold must name. The reader matches the reviewed run's node id, the value the dispatch path passes as source_node, and the repair node composed for the round. A coordinator folding onto some other spelling would still drop the finding; the test pins the three-carrying case against the run's node, so a later change to that contract fails there.
  • Folded ids sit in the done-when, not in the scope. A repair that answers a folded finding must report it by id, but it is not granted that finding's path unless the finding blocks or the run's own fence carries it — the scope rule is unchanged.
