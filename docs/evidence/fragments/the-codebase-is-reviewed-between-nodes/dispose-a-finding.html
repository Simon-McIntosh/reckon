A review finding carries a disposition

reckon crew dispose takes --finding <n> beside --run as an alternative to --dimension, writing the closed set's entry under the record's finding_dispositions map keyed by the index the findings reader prints; the two options are mutually exclusive and one is required. The gate tests/test_dispose_a_finding.py stores a committed review carrying two follow-on findings and runs --finding 1 --kind folded --node fix-a, reading back folded:fix-a for finding 1 and open for finding 0, with an exemption carrying no reason, a call naming both options, and a call naming neither each exiting non-zero and leaving the record's bytes unchanged; the declared negative control writes the disposition into the dimension slot and fails three tests at EXIT=1.
