A neighbourhood health review emits six verdicts, classed findings and exactly one treatment

reckon/crew/prompts/health_review.md is the rubric for a read-only review of a neighbourhood of code, naming six items — known_findings, duplicate_capability, unreached, interface_growth, width, test_debt — with a RUBRIC_ITEMS line and the one-treatment rule. parse_health_review in reckon/crew/review.py reads the emitted form: six verdicts, findings carrying a class and a citation row, and exactly one TREATMENT line, either the warranted form with its write paths and done-when or TREATMENT: none — <reason>. The gate tests/test_health_review_parses.py passes nine tests at EXIT=0, parses the fixed output to six verdicts and the treatment's write paths, refuses a two-treatment output and a no-write-path treatment by name, and holds the prompt's RUBRIC_ITEMS line equal to the parser's item constant in both directions; the declared negative control, the one-treatment check removed, parses the two-treatment output and fails one test at EXIT=1 (the review parser cohort, 65 tests, stays green).
