portfolio-review-composes — one disposition per live plan

The review verb gains a portfolio subject: reckon crew review --project P --portfolio (and --sprint <id>, and --plan <slug> --rubric currency) composes one brief listing every non-shipped plan with its derived impl and its source, sprint, commit age and age source, open section declarations and followups, and the roadmap readiness verdict, then dispatches one read-only review node whose output is one DISPOSITION <slug>: keep | close | merge-into <slug> | retire | re-sprint <id> — <evidence> line per plan in scope. An output omitting a plan in scope is refused, naming the plan.

Measure: tests/test_portfolio_review_composes.py passes at EXIT=0 (4 passed); a fixed three-plan output parses to three dispositions; an output omitting progress is refused with missing == ["progress"]; the declared negative control (empty shipped-status filter) lists the shipped plan and fails the omission assertion.
