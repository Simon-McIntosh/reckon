A dead launch frees its hold

A launch that took its reservation and then ended before recording a supervisor left nothing on disk saying it had ended, so the reservation held the run for as long as the process that reserved it lived. When that process is a long-lived door — the admit service, a station — nothing retried the run and a relaunch was refused on a hold no worker stood behind. A review run was found in exactly that state after 1 h 50 min, its reservation naming the station and no worker ever recorded. Two changes close it: every path that ends a launch between its reservation and its supervisor now files the attempt's exit receipt naming the cause, and a reservation that recorded no supervisor, left no worker record and is older than a declared bound stops holding the run. The release is fenced, so a launch whose attempt was released and retaken starts no supervisor.

What changed

  • reckon/crew/dispatch_launch.py: _record_unreached_launch files the attempt's exit receipt — naming the run, the attempt and the cause — from the three paths that end a launch after its reservation and before its supervisor: a failed _prepare_attempt_records, a failed supervisor-spec write, and a failed supervisor start. It writes nothing once a supervisor is recorded, so a receipt can never free a hold with a running worker behind it.
  • reservation_in_flight now takes the run directory and attempt, and returns not-held for a reservation that names no supervisor, whose attempt left no worker record, and whose file is older than UNREACHED_LAUNCH_BOUND_SECONDS (1800 s). Its docstring states the new rule in place of the old "age is never a reason". The age is the reservation file's own mtime, so a copied or rewritten reservation is dated by when it landed rather than by a stamp it carries.
  • _refuse_superseded_launch is the fence, called immediately before _start_supervisor. It re-reads the reservation this launch took and refuses to start when the file has gone, when it names another pid, or when a later attempt has been reserved for the run. The refusing launch files its own exit receipt and starts nothing.
  • tests/test_a_launch_that_never_reached_its_worker_frees_its_hold.py (new) covers the five behaviours: a raising supervisor start leaves a receipt and frees the hold; a stale reservation naming a live pid is not held and a relaunch is admitted; a hold within the bound still holds; a hold past the bound with a live supervisor still holds; and a superseded launch starts no supervisor while leaving its receipt.
