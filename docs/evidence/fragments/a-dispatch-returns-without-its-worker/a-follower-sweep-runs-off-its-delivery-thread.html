A follower's sweep runs off its delivery thread

The follower's recovery sweep ran on its delivery thread on a fresh arming, and was joined against the arming's deadline on an arming that carried one. A sweep walks every registered worktree, and any launch it starts runs inside that walk, so a launch blocked on a storage read held the pane's next row and the next tick behind it. Both branches now start the sweep on its own thread, and a single-flight guard keeps one sweep in flight.

What the gate did, and why neither branch was safe

_sweep_on_cadence in reckon/crew_follow_commands.py had two branches at the cadence stamp. With no lifetime deadline — the common fresh-arming case and the one measured on 9 November — it called the sweep inline. With a deadline it started a thread and joined it for the time the arming had left, which bounded the sweep but also held the poll: a sweep still walking at the deadline ended the arming by being waited for, not by the arming's own clock. Both put the sweep's duration in front of the reader.

The cadence stamp is not a single-flight guard. It records when the last sweep started, and a sweep can outlive its cadence, so the gate alone would start a second sweep over the same fleet while the first was still walking — two passes that would resume the same lapsed runs.

What changed

  • reckon/crew_follow_commands.py: _sweep_on_cadence always starts the sweep on a daemon thread and never joins it. A thread kept beside the cadence stamp is the single-flight guard: while it is alive a cadence that comes due starts nothing and is retried on a later tick, and the next sweep starts normally once it returns. The arming's deadline is left to the wait pass.
  • reckon/crew_follow_commands.py: the arming's grant is measured once, in monotonic time, from what is left of the deadline at its start, and _check_lifetime ends the arming when either reading has passed. Taking the sweep off the delivery thread removed the one thing that had advanced an injected clock, so an arming whose clock a caller holds still never reached its deadline and its end line was never printed.
  • tests/test_a_follower_sweep_runs_off_its_delivery_thread.py (new): three cases over a temporary crew home, a stream in a temporary file and a sweep the test holds on an event. Rows are delivered and the tick after the gate completes while the sweep is held, on both former branches; a cadence due during the held sweep starts no second sweep, counted on the sweep entry point; and the guard releases, so a cadence after the held sweep returns starts the next one.
