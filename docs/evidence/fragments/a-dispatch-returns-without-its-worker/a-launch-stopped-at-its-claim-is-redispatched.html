A launch stopped at its claim is redispatched

A dispatch publishes its claim before it composes anything else, and a launch cut off at that step leaves a pointer holding a pre-spawn phase, no pid, and no attempt clock. Reading the age fell back to the claim or creation instant only for a cli launch, so a station launch had no clock at all: it never reached the stranded-launch bound, nothing freed its claim, and it read as a young launch for as long as its pointer lived. The claim is a clock every launch kind holds, and it now dates them all. The release is paired with a fence at the point a launch would write its reservation, so a launch merely slowed by storage abandons instead of running a second worker beside its own redispatch.

Measure

tests/test_a_launch_stopped_at_its_claim_is_redispatched.py builds a station launch (launch: "in-harness", no attempt clock) stopped at claim-published on a temporary crew home, in four cases. A dead holder, and a live holder whose claim is older than STRANDED_LAUNCH_BOUND_SECONDS, both read as an unreached launch and are released by the admission pass. A live holder inside the bound still reads starting and is not released. A launch that reaches its reservation after the release is refused and writes no reservation and no worker record.

The negative control restores the base classification — the claim fallback gated on launch == "cli" again — and runs red on both unreached-launch cases and on the fence case: 3 failed, 1 passed. The base arm and the head arm are matched: the head arm runs 55 passed; the base arm resolves the same command over its own tree, where this node's new test file does not exist and is dropped. All three logs are under the run directory.

Where the change lives

  • reckon/crew/recovery_liveness.py — _stranded_launch_age_seconds dates a launch with no attempt clock from its dispatch claim, or its creation instant, whatever its launch kind. The evidence gates in _stranded_launch — no pid, no worker record, no stream, no launch log — are what still separate a young launch from an abandoned one.
  • reckon/crew/dispatch_launch.py — reserve_launch_attempt re-reads the run's release before it writes the attempt reservation, so a launch that resumes after its release takes nothing and starts nothing.
  • tests/test_a_launch_stopped_at_its_claim_is_redispatched.py — the four cases, on a temporary crew home.
