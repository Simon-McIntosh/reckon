A launch span is dated and bounded

A launch blocked on a GPFS read held a claim no worker backed, and nothing inside that blocked span could observe its own bound. The claim now names the process running the span, the span's launch time and the step it reached, and the admission pass — a separate process — releases a span past STRANDED_LAUNCH_BOUND_SECONDS, recording a refusal that names the step before it gives the claim back and removes the run directory. The launch fences on that refusal record rather than on the claim, because the holder may still be alive and blocked, and reads it twice: before the spawn and again after it, so a release landing between the two hands the spawned pid to the rollback that signals it.

Measure

PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests/test_a_launch_span_is_dated_and_bounded.py — 4 passed, 0 failed. The four cases are: a launch held past the bound releases its claim, removes its run directory and records a refusal naming claim-published; the same launch, resumed after its release, refuses rather than spawning, with the launcher never entered; a release pinned inside the spawn window is caught by the post-spawn read and the spawned worker is signalled and gone; and claim.json names the holder pid, the host and the launch time, with the step advancing to identity-published.

The negative control applied both declared mutations and ran red: STRANDED_LAUNCH_BOUND_SECONDS raised past any age left the claim held and the run directory kept, and removing the post-spawn read of the refusal record left the pinned-release worker running. Both logs are under the run directory.

Where the change lives

  • reckon/crew/dispatch_claims.py — the claim carries holder pid, host, launch time and step; read_launch_claim and advance_launch_step sit beside the publisher.
  • reckon/crew/dispatch_refusals.py — the run-keyed launch refusal record and refuse_a_released_launch, the fence the launch reads.
  • reckon/crew/queue_admission.py — _release_stranded_launches, called once per admission pass before anything is ordered.
  • reckon/crew/dispatch.py — the step advances after the spawn and after the identity is published, and the refusal is read on both sides of the spawn.
