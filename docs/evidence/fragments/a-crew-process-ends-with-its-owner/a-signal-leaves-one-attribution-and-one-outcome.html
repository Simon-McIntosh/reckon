a signal leaves one attribution and one outcome

_signal_process_group is the single writer that owns a signal's attribution: the six run-bearing callers no longer pre-write an attribution record of their own, so a delivered signal leaves exactly one attribution record and one delivered outcome in the target run's senders.jsonl, and a guard refusal leaves exactly one refused record. crew unwatch no longer hand-writes its record either — it names the watch directory as its sender file and passes the watched project, written as its own project field rather than folded into outcome_detail, so its delivered signal and its refusal both land there through the same writer. tests/test_every_signal_caller_names_its_run_directory.py drives a delivered and a refused signal through one caller from each of dispatch, promotion and recovery and asserts the exact record set (8 passed), and tests/test_unwatch_leaves_a_sender_record.py asserts the same on the unwatch path (3 passed). The declared mutation (restore the dispatch run-stop pre-written attribution) leaves two attribution records for one delivered signal and fails the delivered case, EXIT=1. Base and head arms of the five named sibling files report 21 passed at base d3cedc615 and 25 passed at head with zero added failures.
