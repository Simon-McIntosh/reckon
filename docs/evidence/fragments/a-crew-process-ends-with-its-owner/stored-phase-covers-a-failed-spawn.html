A failed spawn leaves the stored phase at launch-failed

The supervisor's stored-phase writer advanced the pointer on the normal life — working once the worker was spawned, and the delivered manifest's terminal status once it exited — but the two branches that end a launch before any worker exists wrote their launch-failure record and returned without publishing. Those runs were left reading the pre-spawn starting, so a reader could not tell a launch that never reached a model from one still being placed. The spawn-failure branch (_run_supervisor, when the spawn raises) and the abandoned-before-spawn path (_record_launch_abandoned_before_spawn, a pre-spawn stop) now each call the writer with their own exit record, which carries ended_during: "launch" and so resolves to launch-failed. The writer's attempt guard and its discard guard — a spec for a different attempt, and a pointer a discard removed — are unchanged and now asserted: a foreign attempt leaves the phase as the successor wrote it, and a removed pointer is not recreated.

The gate at tests/test_stored_phase_advances.py adds three cases: a spawn failure and an abandoned launch each read launch-failed; a stale attempt's exit record does not move the phase; a discarded pointer stays absent. The declared control removes the spawn-failure branch's publish and that case reads starting: 1 failed / 4 passed (EXIT=1).
