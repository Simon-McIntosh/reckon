A landing guard tolerates the store's own writes

What changed

reckon.crew.promotion gains _plan_differs_only_by_the_stores_own_writes: the plan's working copy is compared to HEAD through its parsed state, not its bytes. Both sides are rendered through the store's own writer and parsed as documents; comment elements HEAD does not already carry are appended landing records and are dropped, the two version stamps are stripped, and whitespace left between the tags that held a removed section is collapsed. A re-encoded entity, an appended comment or a moved stamp is therefore not an authored change, while an added paragraph, an edited scalar, an edited section record or a changed existing comment still refuses.

_refuse_unrelated_plan_edit is called from _complete_locked before either store is written, so a refused promotion leaves no ledger row and no plan comment for the next promotion to mistake for an unrelated edit. Commit fc83a491a.

Why

An earlier guard (commit 4e2c05ce7, reverted at e70bb91cb) decided inclusion by rebuilding an expected working copy and matching it byte for byte. That refused every later promotion on a plan holding a re-encoded entity, a moved stamp or an earlier run's recorded comment, so a first refusal left its own comment on disk and the next promotion read it as an unrelated edit.
