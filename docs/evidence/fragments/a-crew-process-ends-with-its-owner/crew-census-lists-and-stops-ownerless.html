Recorded ownership determines the process census

reckon crew census lists local processes with a proven dead recorded owner and explains which recorded processes it spares. It reads follower registrations, producer lease registrations, live run pointers and explicit owner stamps. It reports each process's age, state, wait channel and working directory, and identifies sessions with duplicate followers.

--apply prints the target list before signalling, then rechecks process identity and ownership for every listed pid. Signals use a pidfd, with SIGTERM followed by bounded SIGKILL escalation when necessary. Each stopped, spared or refused target is printed. A renewed producer lease, live owner, unknown ownership, foreign-host record, unverified process identity and the census's own ancestry are preserved. Live pointers remain unchanged.
