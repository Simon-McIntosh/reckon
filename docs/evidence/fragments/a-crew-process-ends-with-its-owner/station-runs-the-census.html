The station applies the ownership census every minute

The station starts a dedicated census thread before its first tick and joins it on shutdown. The thread calls the existing census and apply functions, preserving their local ownership checks, ancestry exclusion, identity revalidation and pidfd signalling. Its monotonic interval is 60 seconds; census work cannot hold the heartbeat, review reflex or transition tick.

Every candidate is printed to station stderr before apply, followed by its stopped, spared or refused result. Each JSON record names the pid and recorded owner. An unreadable census prints its exception and retries on the next beat.
