The station applies the ownership census every minute

The station runs a dedicated census thread beside its tick and heartbeat, and only when its caller opts in. Starting the station no longer signals anything as a side effect: a station run that did not ask for the sweep never calls the census and never opens a process descriptor. The thread calls the existing census and apply functions, so local ownership checks, ancestry exclusion and pidfd signalling are unchanged. Its monotonic interval is 60 seconds, and its join is bounded, so a slow sweep cannot hold shutdown.

Apply revalidates a whole sweep against one walk of the process table instead of walking /proc once per listed pid, and every row is judged against that same table. The target's start tick is confirmed again after its descriptor is opened, so a pid recycled mid-sweep is spared rather than signalled. The stop line is printed only after that confirmation: stopping is followed by the result, each JSON record naming the pid and its recorded owner, and a spared row prints no stop line at all. An unreadable census prints its exception and retries on the next beat.
