A reflex-launched review outlives its follower

The review reflex fires from the follower's cadence and hands the composed review to dispatch, which starts the review's per-run supervisor through _start_supervisor. The mechanism that detaches that supervisor from its launcher (149d3c29a) is shared with a hand dispatch, and this gate is the measurement that the shared path covers the reflex: the review's supervisor is never a child of the follower's process tree, so a Monitor stop or expiry — which signals the follower's whole process group — cannot take the review with it. The two dispatch paths must not differ; only the hand-dispatched review survives that signal today, and the follower fix is what makes the reflex review survive it too.

The gate starts a real follower process in a session and process group of its own (start_new_session), builds a throwaway repository and configuration home, and lets the follower's reflex dispatch a review through the real dispatch_review_for_run with only the worktree creation and the supervisor's own launch target stubbed — the launch target is a long sleep, so the vector under test is how dispatch starts it, not what the review does afterwards. It then reads the supervisor pid dispatch recorded for the review, sends SIGTERM to the follower's whole process group (what a Monitor stop delivers), waits for the follower to be gone, and reads the kernel's own record: the review's supervisor is still alive, and it leads a process group and a session of its own rather than the follower's. The assertions are the kernel's facts from /proc/<pid>/stat — liveness by state, and the session column — never a source reading.

Positive control: the follower is a session and process-group leader, and the signal is killpg, so a review that stayed in the group would take the same signal the follower did. The declared mutation — the launch put back in-tree (a plain child of the follower, no intermediate and no new session, as the single-EXIT vector was before it was fixed) — makes the supervisor die with the group and the survival assertion fail.
