Promotion retires a run's disposable identity and reaps its process group

What changed

Promotion's release step now retires the run's own disposable identity and keeps a blocked run's identity and process group. This is the acceptance half of f-acpewio-worker-retired-at-acceptance.

  • reckon/crew/promotion.py: _release_run_workspace gains keep_process, so a run whose manifest reads blocked keeps its process group for a resume; the signal branch records process_stopped_pid. A new _retire_disposable_identity removes the run's own disposable-<run-id> roster row — and only that row — once the release receipt is committed, and _manifest_reads_blocked reads the resume-relevant status. _release_after_promotion threads the blocked decision.
  • tests/test_promotion_retires_a_disposable_identity.py: three cases — the disposable identity is gone and an explicitly named member is kept; a live process group is signalled, reaped and its pid recorded; a blocked run keeps its identity and process.
