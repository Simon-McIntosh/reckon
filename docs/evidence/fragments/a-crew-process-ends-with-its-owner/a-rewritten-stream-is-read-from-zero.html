§11 — a producer re-reads a stream from its first record when it was replaced or rewritten in place

A stream that was replaced at a new inode, or rewritten in place at the same inode, is re-read from its first record by the producer's cursor. The cursor already resumed only while the inode was unchanged and the file was at least as long as the offset; neither condition catches a stream extended under a cursor by a rewrite, so an in-place rewrite to a new opening resumed into bytes it never held. The cursor now records a bounded fingerprint of the stream's opening beside the offset, and it resumes only while the stream still opens with it: an append leaves the opening intact and still resumes; a replaced or rewritten stream reads whole. The commits-beyond-base cache drops entries for worktrees no longer on disk and is bounded by the live fleet.
