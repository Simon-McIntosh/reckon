§11 — the producer's admission check reads only what changed

The producer's poll called recovery._admission_refusal for every live run on every tick. It opened the run's stream and scanned every record to EOF with no cursor and no stat gate, so a poll over a fleet whose streams had not moved still re-read every transcript, and the per-poll cost grew with total stream size rather than with what had been appended. The refusal is consumed only where a run's process is gone, so the read is now taken only on that arm, and its result is memoised against the stream's stat identity: a stream nothing has appended to answers from the memo without opening the file. The bytes a scan does consume are counted in their own cell, so a stat-only poll reads nothing here and a reader can tell the two apart.
