The back half promoted behind reviews

What was measured

§5 of the plan asked whether every promoted node from its fifth section on carried a stored, parsed review, with no waiver. The population is read from the crew ledger by run id: every run of the plan whose section ordinal is five or more, whose role is not review, and whose work landed. A run counts as landed when its gate passed, or when it recorded a promoted revision or a commit — the §12 run recover-launches-only-what-it-is-asked landed under pre-existing-failure and the §14 run a-failed-staging-reads-its-row-back under negative-result, and each carries a review, so excluding them by gate would have mis-stated the population.

Each landed run's stored review is read from docs/state/reckon/reviews/run/<reviewed run>/<review run>.json. A run reviewed more than once has one record per head; the record that gated promotion is the one whose reviewed_base_sha/reviewed_head_sha pair matches the pair the ledger row's own review snapshot carries. The review's wall time is the reviewing run's wall_seconds from the ledger.

The ledger is read through reckon.ledger.load rather than by opening the aggregate file. The aggregate docs/state/reckon/crew.json carries 121 of this plan's 137 rows; the later §10 to §24 runs own individual files under docs/state/reckon/runs/<run id>.json, which only the project's own reader unions in. A census written against the aggregate alone would have reported the whole back half as absent.

Median review wall time

The median is 911.0 s, about fifteen minutes per review. The two extremes are the §12 review at 386 s and the §5c promotion-reads-the-review-of-the-head-it-promotes review at 3020 s.

Runs with no stored review, or with a waiver

No promoted run in this population lacks a stored review, and none used a waiver. Two runs are named because they are the cases the measure must not silently drop:

  • r-20260921T090942172410-a-review-records-the-revision-it-read (§5c) is the one recorded completion at §5 or later that did not land: gate failed, classification infrastructure-failure, no commit and no promoted revision, review null. It is not in the promoted population, and it carries no stored review. Its gate never fired the review guard, because _require_review_waiver computes unreviewed only when the verdict is passed; a failed completion is never gated on a review. That is the one hole this measure exposes, and it is a property of a completion that landed nothing.
  • Eleven review-role runs at §5 or later are exempt from the review gate by role, not by waiver, and are excluded from the population: one at §6 (gate passed), and ten whose gate is recorded not-run — the review runs at §5c (five, one of them a re-review), §5g, §9, §10 and §12. A review is not reviewed; the exemption is on the role and appears in no review_waiver field.

The earliest plan nodes predate the review requirement, which is why the count is not zero across the whole plan.

What this does not say

The measure answers whether a review was stored and read at the promoted head. It does not judge the review's quality, and it does not claim the findings were acted on: the acted-on list above names the ones with a landed change behind them and no others. It also does not reach a completion that landed nothing, because that path never consults a review — named above so the limit of the measure is on the record rather than implied.
