A floor repair's advice carries the review's evidence for the score

What changed. A dimension scored under its declared floor composes a finding of its own, and that finding's text was the score and the floor alone: a repair resumed with it read [dimension-reuse] reuse scored 6 below its declared floor 10 and nothing about what had scored 6, so the worker guessed what to fix. review_dispositions.sub_floor_evidence now returns each sub-floor row together with the review's own justification for that dimension and the findings the same record raised, and recovery_repair_dispatch._repair_resume_advice renders them beneath the brief for every dimension under its floor. A dimension the reviewer justified without raising a finding still carries its justification: the justification is the reviewer's account of the score whether or not a finding sits beside it.

The findings are not attributed to a dimension, because the record does not attribute them: one review covers one run, and every finding on it is part of what the reviewer observed while scoring. They are repeated on each sub-floor row rather than split by a relation the record does not hold.
