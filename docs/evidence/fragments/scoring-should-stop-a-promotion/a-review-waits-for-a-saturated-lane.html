A review waits for a lane its own document reports saturated

The defect

The reflex selected the lane it composes an automatic review onto from the ordering alone: the owning run's backend, then the locally served one, then the rest alphabetically. _review_lane_candidates never opened the document a serving lane publishes about its own occupancy, so a review was composed onto a lane already over the ceiling that lane had computed for itself. Measured 2026-09-30: the local lane sampled at running 30 against concurrent_requests 22 with waiting 0 — degrading rather than queueing — and runs composed onto it emitted turns and tool calls and produced nothing.

Reproduced at base (the reproduction script and every log named here are in ~/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20260930T214124855580-a-review-waits-for-a-saturated-lane/logs/, revision 1c20ca680a2b35119f6256431c5c62ef53b7bc34), with the positive control that the reader sees the saturation present in the document rather than a blind instrument:

callbasehead
read_lane_document_file (control) running=30 concurrent_requests=22 age_seconds=0 stale=False same
_review_lane_candidates(config), local saturated ['alpha', 'beta'] — the saturated lane leads [] — held
_no_lane_reason(...) on that hold names only the exclusion key names 'alpha' ... 30 running ... 22 concurrent_requests

The live project config makes this path live rather than theoretical: the local backend clive declares lane_document: ~/public/imas-ambix/lane.json in docs/state/reckon/flight.yaml, printed by the same reproduction.

The change

_review_lane_candidates in reckon/crew/recovery.py now walks the ordering through _review_lane_plan, reading each candidate's own declared lane_document when it is current. A lane whose fresh reading reports it running at or above its own concurrent_requests is passed over. A saturated local lane with nothing eligible ahead of it withholds the selection entirely: a review is local-lane-shaped work, so it waits rather than moving to a metered lane. The hold lands in the existing empty-candidate path, recording awaiting-lane with a reason naming the lane and the two figures its document published; because the hold is not recorded as a dropped attempt, the lane is free to carry the review once it drains.

Currency is what makes the reading usable. A document that is absent, unreadable, malformed, older than the lane's own suggested_shelf_life_seconds, or older than the reflex's 45-second bound answers no reading at all, and the lane keeps its place: the hold rests on a measurement the document made, never on its absence.

What this does not close

A saturated non-local lane reached only after the review path's own filter removes the previously-failed lane is not re-read; that filter runs after the candidate function, so the lane it exposes is composed onto without a document check. The printed _composed_review_lane command still falls back to --local when the ordering is empty, naming the saturated lane in a display-only artifact; the executed path holds.
