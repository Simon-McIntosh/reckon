The repair dispatch reads the figure review

The defect

A run that drew a figure carries two stored reviews at one head: its text review and its figure review under the figure rubric. dispatch_repair_for_run (reckon/crew/recovery_repair_dispatch.py) read the stored review through _stored_review, which selects the text record, and took three readings from it — whether any finding was raised, whether any of them blocks, and which paths the round's scope grants. Its composer, repair.compose_repair_for_run, folds the figure review in, but the reflex returned before reaching the composer whenever the text review carried no finding. So a clean text review hid an undisposed blocking figure finding: the round was never composed and the figure defect waited for a coordinator.

Reproduced before the repair: the gate's first case, a blocking figure finding behind a clean text review, got "the stored review carries no finding, so composes no repair" — 2 failed, EXIT=1 (logs/repro-clean-text-figure-finding.log). The other two cases passed unchanged at that revision, which is the positive control that the harness drives the real reflex path.

The change

The reflex merges the run's figure review at the same head into the record once, immediately after it reads and completeness-checks the stored review and before any of the three readings that decide the round:

review = repair_module.review_with_figure(review)
findings = repair_module.review_findings(review)

Merging later would leave the blocking check and the scope deciding over the text-only list and route the round to decline-only, which is exactly what §23 warns of: changing the first check alone still declines. The merged record keeps the text record's identity fields, so the round stays keyed to the text review's run and head; and review_with_figure re-keys the figure record's dispositions onto the appended positions, so a figure finding already disposed is not repaired twice.

Evidence

Gate: tests/test_a_clean_text_review_still_repairs_a_figure_finding.py passes — 4 passed, EXIT=0 (logs/gate-clean-text-figure-finding.log): a blocking figure finding behind a clean text review resumes the run, the advice naming the figure and the finding and the scope carrying that finding; the same finding once disposed on its own record resumes nothing and records decline-only; two clean reviews decline with today's reason verbatim; a text blocking finding gets the round it got before.

Negative control — the merged record swapped in only for the first findings check and not for the blocking scope: the first case is declined and the second fails too, 2 failed, EXIT=1 (logs/negative-control-merged-review-first-check-only.log).

Focused population at head — the new file plus the sibling repair/reflex/figure test modules that exercise dispatch_repair_for_run and the composer: 56 passed, EXIT=0 (logs/gate-repair-reflex-population.log). ruff check and ruff format --check add no finding on the changed paths: the only remaining check is a pre-existing ISC004 at line 446, present identically in the base blob.
