The imputed mean weighs only the buildable sections' hours

When a plan's records fall short of its declarations, the imputed hours a section without a record takes are now the mean over the sections declared done or implementable alone. A deferred section sits outside the denominator, so its recorded hours no longer weigh a buildable section that states none.

What changed

  • derive_impl_imputing_effort in reckon/_plan_html.py built its hours map from every section record carrying hours, including a deferred section's. It now reads a record's hours only when that record's id is in the scoped set — the sections declared done or implementable — so the mean it imputes from is taken over the denominator alone. The docstring states the rule by mechanism.
  • The fix is confined to the imputation path. Records covering every declared buildable section still answer under computed through derive_impl_from_sections, which already kept a deferred record outside both the numerator and the denominator.

The defect reproduced before the repair

A plan with s1 done at 2 h, s2 implementable with no hours, s3 implementable at 4 h and s4 deferred at 40 h, at base revision e57353abd:

imputed: 0.09374999999999999
expected 2/9 = 0.2222222222222222

The deferred section's 40 h entered the mean, raising it from (2+4)/2 = 3 h to 46/3 h and pushing the plan's figure down to 2 / 21.33. With the fix, the same plan reads 2 / (2+3+4) = 2/9 = 0.2222222222222222 under source declarations.
