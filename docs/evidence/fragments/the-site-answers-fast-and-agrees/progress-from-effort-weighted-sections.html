A plan's progress is its declared sections' effort-weighted done share

A plan's progress is now the done share of its declared sections weighted by each section's recorded effort, with a section whose hours no record states taking the mean of the hours present; where no section carries hours every section weighs the same, which is the count. The head-only reader that builds discovery rows derives from the declarations even when the document carries no section records, so a plan declared but not yet recorded serves one figure from the fast path and from the full parse.

What changed

  • derive_impl_with_source in reckon/_plan_html.py is the one entry point every reader of a plan calls. It returns the figure and its source. Records that cover every declared done or implementable section answer with the effort-weighted figure (computed), unchanged.
  • A new derive_impl_imputing_effort weighs the declared sections when the records fall short of the declarations: each section weighs its record's hours, an unrecorded declared section takes the mean of the hours present, and the result answers under source declarations. With no hours present at all the declaration count answers under the same source, and a plan with no declarations keeps its authored plan-impl (authored).
  • _parse_meta_uncached now derives from the head's plan-section-declarations meta even when the document carries no section records, via a new _head_declarations, instead of only through the full parse. A declared-but-unrecorded plan therefore reports the same figure and source from discovery rows as from the parse, the state the SPA disagreed on before.
  • Deferred declarations sit outside the denominator. The module docstrings state the rule by mechanism.

The gate

tests/test_progress_from_section_effort.py parses temporary plans for all five cases: every section recorded with hours, a partial set of hours (s1 done at 2 h, s2 declared with no record, s3 implementable at 4 h — s2 imputed 3 h, so the plan reads 2/9), no hours at all (the count), no declarations (the authored figure), and declarations with no records (both readers agree). tests/test_impl_derives_from_declarations.py is edited to the new rule, its partial-records case now asserting the imputed figure 2/9 where the declaration count alone would read 1/3.
