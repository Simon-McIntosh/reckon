A worker step carries a memory bound

Result. Every fleet worker step is now placed with an explicit --mem through placement.step_prefix, defaulting to 24 GB and honouring a bound declared through the existing dispatch --set override. A step declared past the 48 GB ceiling is placed as a job of its own on a debug partition rather than inside the fleet allocation, and its standard streams come back to the caller as a step's do. fleet_node.placement_argv now composes its prefix through step_prefix, so the bound is stated once. A probe step on the fleet allocation placed with --mem=1G shows its cgroup memory.max equal to that bound.

Measure. Honest evaluator of capacity and honesty of experimentally reporting: the focused gate ran 132 passed, 1 skipped in place on the fleet allocation (job 1281970 on host 98dci4-clu-2002), and the probe step read memory.max = 1073741824 for a declared --mem=1G.

The probe. On the fleet allocation, a step placed with srun --overlap -n1 --mem=1G reports step_XXXX/user/memory.max = 1073741824 — 1 GiB, the declared bound — and sizeof(bytearray) allocation past it drives the step's own memory.events max counter (6576) rather than the job's OOM counter. The bound is set on the step's user cgroup; the step cgroup and its task_0 child read max and inherit it.

Negative control. The declared mutation — a probe step allocating past a 1 GB bound records its OOM event in the step's memory.events and not the job's — produced the red log logs/negative-control-step-oom.log (EXIT=1, failing id test_a_step_past_its_bound_records_oom_in_the_step). The node carries only 4 GB of swap, so a step past its bound swaps a few GB before the kernel OOM-kills inside its own cgroup; the shared node's swap is never filled to force that kill. What is observable without filling it is the part the routing depends on: the pressure is recorded in the step's memory.events max counter (6576) while the job's OOM counter is unchanged. The check is an opt-in live check — it reads the node's cgroup files and needs a real placed step, so it is not part of the default gate and is not run by the focused suite.

Declaring a larger bound. The bound is a scheduler option, so a node that needs more states it in the backend's placement through the dispatch --set override, which replaces the whole options list:

reckon crew dispatch --project <project> --node <node> \
  --set 'backends.<backend>.placement.options=["--ntasks=1","--mem=48G"]'

The shipped options are ["--ntasks=1"], so they are repeated beside the new bound. A declaration at or below the 48 GB ceiling stays a step of the held allocation; a larger one is placed as a job of its own on the backend partition's _debug sibling. Both spellings of an option are read (--mem=64G and --mem 64G), and a declared bound is never restated beside itself — a step carries the one request its placement made, in the spelling it made it. A --mem-per-cpu figure is honoured by multiplying it by the placement's own --cpus-per-task; with no task count beside it the total is unknown and the dispatch is refused, naming the option, rather than priced as a single task — pricing it low is the direction that overfills the allocation.

A step of its own. The over-ceiling route is the step client asked for an allocation of its own, not the batch client: srun --partition=<partition>_debug --mem=<bound> --time=<budget> --job-name=<run> <command as argv>. Only the allocation's own identity stood between that launch and a job of its own, so the launch removes it — SLURM_JOB_ID, SLURM_JOBID, SLURM_STEP_ID, SLURM_STEPID and the rest of the SLURM_JOB*/SLURM_STEP* group — and the client asks for its own allocation instead of opening a step of the held one. The command is carried as argv rather than a wrapped script, so the worker's standard streams are the ones the supervisor reads. The time limit is the run's own budget capped at the window such a partition allows: its maximum is an hour and it sets no default, so an omitted limit would leave the job pending rather than bounded (all_debug and rigel_debug both read MaxTime=01:00:00 DefaultTime=NONE).

Checked live, once. A --mem=49G declaration for a trivial command was placed through this path on the fleet node (host 98dci4-clu-2002, holding job 1281970): the command's output came back on the caller's stdout (stdout-returned-from-its-own-job), and sacct names job 1289800 of its own on partition all_debug with mem=49G, state COMPLETED, its single step 1289800.0. It is not among the steps of the holding job: no 1289800 row appears under 1281970. Log: logs/own-job-live-check.log.
