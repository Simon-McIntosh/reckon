One promotion of a run at a time

What changed

reckon/crew/promotion.py complete now holds the run for the whole of its promotion behind a cross-host HostLease (reckon/crew/host_lease.py). The lease is named for the run (promotion-<run_id>) and lives in the fleet home every host shares; it is claimed by the atomic link HostLease.claim already provides, and it names its holder's host, pid and job. No new lock mechanism is written.

The claim is taken by a context manager (_promotion_host_lease) that complete enters inside its existing node-local pointer lock, before any store is written, and leaves after its landing or rollback. While the promotion runs a daemon thread renews the lease every LEASE_RENEW_SECONDS, so a slow landing on a congested shared filesystem is not taken over mid-landing. A promotion that finds the lease freshly held by another holder raises a CrewError at once without writing, naming the holder's host, pid and job; a lease past its stale window is taken over, and that promotion proceeds. The node-local pointer lock stays for the pointer's own read-modify-write.

A sweep inherits the claim because promote_review_run_itself reaches complete; its existing (CrewError, OSError) handling turns the held refusal into a refused self-promotion whose reason names the holder, rather than an error. reckon/crew/host_lease.py is unchanged — the lease type it already defines is what the promotion now uses.
