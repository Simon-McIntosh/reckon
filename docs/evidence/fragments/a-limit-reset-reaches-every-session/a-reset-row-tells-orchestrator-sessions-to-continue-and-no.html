A reset row says "continue", and says no worker lane opened

At the base revision a limit-reset row was composed as a free-text clause with the group, clock and instruction in it, and the two groups read limit reset · claude-orchestrators · five_hour · orchestrator sessions may continue · … and limit reset · claude-sub · five_hour · worker lanes may continue · …. The second is the reading the lead ruled against: a reset recorded under the worker lane's own budget group said that lane had no limit, which is not what an announcer recording a lead-reported reset recorded. The row also carried no stage cell of its own, so nothing on the line separated a reset from a lift beyond the clause's first two words.

The row now prints the stage cell limit, the budget group in the job cell — padded to the job column through the same cell a fleet row and a lift row use, so the trailing figures keep their places — and the instruction where the transition words would go: 09:06 limit claude-orchestrators limits reset (five_hour): orchestrator sessions continue; worker lanes keep their limits — <the announcer's reason>. Every reset row closes with the worker-lane clause whatever group it was recorded under, so a reset recorded on claude-sub reads limits reset (five_hour): worker lanes keep their limits and no row reads as opening a lane it never named. The wording is composed once, in ticker.limit_reset_text, which the pane prints; lift_watch._reset_detail now composes the event's own facts only — group, clock, instant, announcer, reason — because which instruction applies depends on the group's wallet, which only the rendering pane resolves.
