A recorded reset now says which sessions its broadcast reaches

At the base revision crew budget --reset --used wrote the durable reset event and said nothing about who hears it, so an announcer relaying a reset had no way to learn which sessions were left out and messaged every one of them by hand. The command's result now carries a reach block, folded from every mounted project's follower_registration.list_followers: reached lists the sessions holding a live registration, whose panes will draw the row, and unreached lists the sessions whose registrations are all dead, which are the ones to tell directly. The liveness verdict stays with list_followers — the report derives none of its own — and the read writes nothing. The block appears in the JSON document and in the pretty output, and the --used help and the payload's docstring say so.
