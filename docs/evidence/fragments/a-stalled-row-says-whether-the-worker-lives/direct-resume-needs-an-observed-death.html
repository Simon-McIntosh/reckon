A hand-typed resume waits for an observed end, as the sweep does

What changed. resume_plan in reckon/crew/dispatch.py carried only its own liveness guard — it refused a process this host found alive and nothing else. A run whose worker lives on another machine, or whose pointer recorded no process at all, therefore resumed as though its worker were dead: the collision the sweep's observed-end rule exists to prevent, reached by typing the command by hand rather than by sweeping.

The launcher now consults the sweep's own helper, resumption._observed_end_refusal, rather than a second composition of the rule, so what counts as an observed end cannot drift between the two doors. An observation is exactly one of two things: a pid checked on this host and found dead, or a supervisor's exit record. The refusal names the reading it holds (liveness unknown). It is consulted after the launcher's other guards — the launch kind, the proven-live process and the budget hold — and after the sweep consults them, so a run that fails several reports the reason the sweep would report for it too.
