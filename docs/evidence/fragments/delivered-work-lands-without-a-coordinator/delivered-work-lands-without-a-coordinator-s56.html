A landing arm that never reached the scheduler now says why

At the pinned base 398a0d09708dda09bec869b7a512404cf9eb2902, an arm whose sbatch --wait submission exits non-zero before printing a job id leaves landing-measure/measurement.json with elapsed_seconds near 0.01, no job_id, exit_status null and an empty log. The landing then refuses with the base arm did not run, which names the absence and nothing that could be acted on. The new test reproduces exactly that reading at the base revision: AssertionError: refusal read: 'the base arm did not run'.

What changed. The partitioned arm runner in reckon/crew/landing_measure.py now distinguishes a submission that never started a job from a job whose submission reported its status. A job writes the arm's log, so a receipt, a truncated log or the script's own tree-build refusal is evidence a job ran. When the submission returns non-zero with no job id printed before the wait and an empty log, the arm is recorded as submission_failed with an unreadable_reason naming the submitted command, its exit status and its own output, and the arm's status is 3. The measurement's refusal then reads the base arm never reached the scheduler: its submission never started a scheduler job: sbatch … exited 1 with …, quoting the submission's output.

Both landing paths read the same sentence. The lander's pass and crew complete --land both judge a stored measurement through queue_landing._measure_guard, and the reason is serialised in the measurement record it reads, so the refusal text is identical whichever entry point asks. The test asserts the pass's guard text and the stored-record guard text are the same string, and that the stored record carries the arm's submission_failed flag.

What does not change. An arm whose job starts and fails is recorded from its receipt as before: its exit status and failing ids come from the arm's own EXIT= line, and the case that asserts this passes at the base revision unchanged. An arm killed at its time limit keeps its timeout reading. The two fixtures that stub the scheduler were left as they are: their logs already carry the evidence that a job ran.

Measure. The importer population of reckon/crew/landing_measure.py — 41 files plus the node's own test — was run on all_debug at the base and at the head. The base arm reports 6 failed, 399 passed (two of the failures are the node's own new test running against the base source, and four are environment-dependent tests that fail identically at both revisions). The head arm reports 4 failed, 401 passed, whose four failures are the same four the base arm carries: no failure is added.
