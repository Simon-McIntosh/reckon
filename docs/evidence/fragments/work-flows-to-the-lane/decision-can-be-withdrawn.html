A decision can be withdrawn

Taking a decision out of a plan had no tool path. lock records an answer, and text mode refuses a replacement overlapping the decisions region, so a decision that no longer applied was removed with a direct hand edit — a write no version, no reason and no date survived, and one the next caller could not see had happened.

edit_plan now dispatches a withdraw op. It refuses an index, a missing decision, a locked decision (naming it, because a lock is someone's answer) and a withdrawal carrying no reason. Otherwise it writes once, at the expected version, storing the decision with no choice, the reason in rationale and the date in when, and clearing the sections it governed and the recommendation it carried — a recommendation left behind would let a later accept read the withdrawal back as a choice. The decision therefore stops being an open decision and stops appearing among the roadmap's blockers, while the record of why it went stays in the plan.

The cases the node pins

  • Withdrawing an unlocked decision on a temporary plan moves the version 0 → 1 and the plan's open-decision count 1 → 0; the roadmap row that read blocked with decision_blockers naming transport reads ready with no decision blocker, and the withdrawal is reported under the row's deferred decisions with its reason.
  • The kept record: the stored decision's rationale is the reason verbatim, by is the caller, choice and sections are empty, and when parses as a timestamp written during the call.
  • Withdrawing a locked decision is refused with op_error naming the decision and the word locked, and the plan is left at its version with the lock intact.
  • A withdrawal with no reason is refused naming the decision, the plan stays at its version, and the decision is still counted open.
  • A withdrawn decision cannot be accepted back: an accept over it is refused for the absent recommendation it used to carry.
