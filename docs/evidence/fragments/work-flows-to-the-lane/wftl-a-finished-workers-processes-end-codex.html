A terminal run drains its launch tree

reckon/crew/dispatch_launch.py caps the terminal delivery grace and the continuously rewritten manifest ceiling at 30 seconds, retaining the existing 10-second signal escalation. A terminal pointer sets a monotonic deadline for its current attempt. That deadline and the signal clock survive adoption of children, so the harness exiting after SIGTERM cannot restart the grace for its descendants. An override can shorten the grace but cannot extend the bound.

A terminal harness that crashes before the supervisor signals it also drains its descendants. The supervisor retains the harness PID and wait status while collecting those children. Its exit receipt carries forced_exit.reason=terminal-run, the terminal phase and the signal time only when it intervened; the existing ended_pids and sender records identify the processes actually signalled. Natural exit leaves ended_pids=[] and no forced_exit or sender record.
