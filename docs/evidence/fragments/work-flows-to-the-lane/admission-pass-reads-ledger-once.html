Section waits share the release stage's ledger snapshot

The section landing check in reckon/crew/admission/readiness.py passes its existing indexed ledger snapshot to reckon/crew/runs.py::_unloaded_sections through the optional ledger_rows argument. The shared helper retains its landing derivation and its existing read when no snapshot is supplied. The snapshot lives in the release stage's cache, so the next stage reads current history.

tests/test_an_admission_pass_reads_the_ledger_once.py seeds 2,000 per-run ledger records, warms their index, and resolves ten requests waiting on five sections. Eight requests release and two stay waiting, matching the whole-ledger derivation for the same records. The cases include a reachable promotion, an unreachable promotion, a section completed without a run, a discarded run, and a section with both reachable and unreachable promotions. A promotion written after the first snapshot leaves its two waiters pending in that stage and releases both on the next stage. Another case fast-forwards the temporary checkout to its pending revision after that section's first wait verdict; both waiters remain pending in the current stage and release on the next one, with one ledger load per stage and no per-run document reads.
