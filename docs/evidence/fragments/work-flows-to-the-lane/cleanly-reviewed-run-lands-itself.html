Reviewed deliveries land through a measured merge

The acceptance reflex measures a merge candidate before promotion and verifies the final merge before primary advances. It reuses the partition archive runner, review-tier checks, publication, pending-landing recovery, and section writeback. A locked landing decision with choice manual or coordinator reserves integration for the coordinator. A conflict or a failed candidate gate leaves the delivery unpromoted, with its landing refusal recorded. A merge refused after promotion names its pending landing and reports merged: false.

The temporary-repository cases prove the delivered head reaches primary, its introduced feature marker survives, the merged-head gate receipt is preserved in the run and ledger, and the section declaration is committed as done. Refusal cases exercise a conflicting head, a gate failing only at the merged tree, manual integration, and a conflict arising after promotion. Classification, monitor snapshots, weekly totals, departure wording and promotion settlement do not call the unmerged delivery landed. Pending recovery replays the gate and refuses a post-promotion failure. The weekly checks read an absent per-plan bucket as zero and include an explicit omitted-bucket case.
