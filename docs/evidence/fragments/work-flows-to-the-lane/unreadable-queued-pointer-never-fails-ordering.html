A pointer the ordering cannot place is set aside, not raised on

The ordering at reckon/crew/queue_order.py ranked every queued pointer by its queued_at and raised ValueError("queued pointer has no readable queued_at") on the first one it could not read. That refusal reached every caller that ranks the queue, so a single malformed pointer stopped admission ordering, the queue position a dispatch reports and every review compose, across all projects, until someone rewrote the record.

admission_order_details now leaves out the pointers it cannot place and logs their run ids; partition_placeable() returns that split to every caller that ranks or reports the queue. readable_queued_at() keeps a stamp that parses and replaces one that does not, and the three writers that could store an unusable arrival now stamp through it: runs.queue_dispatch for a caller that supplies none, and the two re-queue paths in reckon/crew/admission/launching.py.

The placeable/unplaceable split lives in queue_order.partition_placeable and the admission pass takes it from there, so the order a surface reports and the order the pass ranks by cannot disagree.
