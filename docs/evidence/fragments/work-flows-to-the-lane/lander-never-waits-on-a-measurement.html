Measurements leave the landing pass free to advance other deliveries

The scheduler path submits a whole comparison as one bounded batch job, records its job id, request, pinned primary and delivered revisions, result path and log on the run, and returns. Later passes read scheduler state and require a terminal EXIT=0 receipt plus a result matching those identities before attaching the measurement and landing. The job runs the existing suite pair, importer pair and isolated comparisons on its compute allocation, with its checkouts in node-local scratch. It does not submit a second allocation for each arm.

RECKON_LANDING_MEASUREMENT_WIDTH configures the global number of measurements in flight; its default is two. A cross-host landing-pass lock prevents concurrent passes from submitting the same delivery. Unknown job state retains its slot. Finished jobs release width even if their delivery now waits for review. Pending and completed job records survive a fresh lander, including an interruption before suite evidence was attached.

Failed, timed-out, cancelled and out-of-memory jobs refuse their own delivery with the job id and log path. A scheduler completion without a successful terminal receipt is refused too. Publication records that leave the measured product unchanged reuse the completed comparison and rebuild its candidate, avoiding another synchronous measurement after promotion. Explicit in-place measurement callers retain their existing behavior when no measurement partition is configured.

Scheduler-controlled tests use a temporary repository and crew home. Suite and importer arms ran separately on all_debug, with at most four test workers.
