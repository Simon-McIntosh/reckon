Killing a producer leaves a fresh one, and five ensures leave one

The restart this section asks for is already carried by the arming supervisor: a host that cannot enable lingering arms the producer as a plain background process under _WATCH_PRODUCER_SUPERVISOR, whose wait on its child treats a signal death as a crash: the supervisor's own argv is if not (1 <= status <= 127 or status < 0): raise SystemExit(status). reckon/crew/dispatch_watch.py has restarted a crashed fallback producer under the same backoff curve since 4152e8c57b (fix(crew): restart a crashed fallback watch producer, 13 October). No product change was needed, and none was made.

What was missing was a case pinning the measure end to end, and that is tests/test_a_killed_producer_is_restarted_without_linger.py. It arms through watch_unit.ensure_watcher_service with a manager whose enable_linger raises LingerUnavailableError, so the fallback is the path that starts the process, and the producer runs in a temporary RECKON_HOME.
