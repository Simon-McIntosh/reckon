The promotion queue drains at slot speed, not at pass speed

A freed promotion slot is taken up by the next waiting request on the promoting thread's own wakeup, with a two-second poll for requests another process queued, instead of waiting for the admission pass. The drain also watches the checkout's code stamp: once it moves nothing further starts, and the admission loop waits for the promotions in flight and re-executes the image. Every finished request is appended to a bounded drain log, and crew promotions reports the requests waiting, the oldest wait and the counts finished in a window.
