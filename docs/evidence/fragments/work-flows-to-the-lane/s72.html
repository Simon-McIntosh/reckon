§72 — the coordination services are the last thing an OOM kill reaches

A coordination service is started below every dispatched worker in the kernel's OOM ordering, so a full cgroup kills a worker rather than the admit service, the station or a watch producer. The change, the reading, the negative control and the note on the two-hour resident-memory measure are recorded in full under the node's landing record.
