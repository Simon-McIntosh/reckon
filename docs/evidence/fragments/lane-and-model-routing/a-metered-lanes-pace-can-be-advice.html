A flight can name groups whose pace is advice rather than a hold, and whose dispatch ceiling carries none of the three reserves

A flight's budget block is flat: one block bounds every group it covers, so a declaration that concerns some groups and not others has nowhere to say which. The new key budget.advisory_pace_groups names them. A group named there is governed by two changes and no others. First, its dispatch ceiling is the window's own: budget_group.effective_block now composes a third rule beside the credits lift and the tier reserve, and a declared group's resume_reserve_pct, coordinator_reserve_pct and bookend_reserve_pct resolve to zero. That function is the single block a dispatch (budget.preflight), a resume prediction (resumption), the picker (picker/snapshot) and the router all resolve through, so one edit moves the ceiling everywhere at once and nowhere else. Second, its pace hold admits at any utilisation the window still has: pace.allowance_for_group stamps advisory on the Allowance it returns, pace_report.group_pace carries that sign on the group's own allowance in the report, and pace_report.pace_hold reads it there rather than re-resolving configuration it may not hold. The sign is therefore visible to any reader of the pace report, not only to the hold that consumes it. The two hard stops are deliberately untouched, because they bound the account rather than the pace: an exhausted window with no credits lift is still held at the ceiling, and a credits lift's cap on drawn credits is still spent through. A group absent from the list is governed exactly as before, so omitting the key leaves routing unchanged.

The node's new test builds a conservative wallet at the figures §48 names and asserts the five cases it asks for. A group at 96% of its week 8.5 hours into the 168 — a burn multiple of about 19 against a 1.1 allowance — is not held for an implement dispatch, and its allowance reports the reading rather than withholding the dispatch. The same group's dispatch ceiling is 100, not the 92 that the resume and coordinator reserves withhold, and at 100% with no credits lift it is held on the window itself. Under a credits lift it admits at one unit above the cap's stop and is held at the same stop: the 90% stop on a 10,000-credit cap is drawn through even though the window is spent. A group declaring nothing at the same figures is held by its pace and withheld at 96% by its 92% ceiling. A declared group with no readable credit balance under a lift falls back to its ceiling rather than admitting, because an unread balance is not a fund. A seventh case holds a declared group at 100% with no lift, so the exhausted-window stop is shown not to read the declaration at all.
