The picker excludes a lane on the verdict a dispatch would take, and judges a reserved lifted lane by its credit pool

The picker's budget gate in reckon/crew/picker/snapshot.py no longer compares a lane's window against a raw ceiling of its own. A new _budget_exclusion_reasons resolves the group's lift-aware block through budget_group.effective_block and reads the verdict of budget.decide for the dispatch purpose, quoting the verdict's reason; where no credits lift rides it also applies budget.effective_ceiling, so the picker holds at the ceiling less the reserves a dispatch withholds. A credits lift therefore admits a spent window here exactly as it does at dispatch. _reserved_lane_exclusion gains a credit_lift term: under a credits lift its window term is satisfied by the credit pool, so a spent pool is withheld by the budget verdict's own reason and a funded one is offered on the tier term alone; the tier and attempts terms are unchanged, and a lane under no lift is judged by its window as before. The fresh-reading gate that offers a stale figure rather than refusing on it is unchanged.
