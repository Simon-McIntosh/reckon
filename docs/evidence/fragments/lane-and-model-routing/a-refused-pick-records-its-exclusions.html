A refused pick records its exclusions

The pick-timings log named a refusal's outcome and its one-line reason but not which lanes were excluded or on what ground, so diagnosing the 58 refusals of 26–27 October meant re-running a pick by hand to see that the local lane failed on context fit and codex on its tier term. A refusal of a node whose shape is lost could not be diagnosed at all. _record_pick_timings in reckon/crew/picker/__init__.py now shapes the line for a refuse or fallback outcome: an exclusions list naming each excluded candidate's backend, model and reason_code, and the node's estimated_context. A reason code is the reason's text before its first colon, or the whole reason when it carries none, so context-fit stands alone and reserved-lane: codex is reserved … yields reserved-lane — the stable prefix a reader counts by, without matching prose that changes. A routed pick records neither field, so the log grows only where a reader needs it.
