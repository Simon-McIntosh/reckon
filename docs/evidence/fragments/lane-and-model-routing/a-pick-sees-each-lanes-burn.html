Each metered candidate's row carries its lane's burn

A new derived block in reckon/budget.py states a metered lane's current spending rate, the share of its remaining window it can afford over the coming period, and — under a credits lift — the credit pool it has left, the rate the pool is being drawn at and the runway those two imply. burn_block(project, group, …) derives one lane's block; burn_blocks(project, groups=…) collects the readings once and returns a block per group, so a picker scan derives each wallet's figure once rather than per model sharing the account. budget_period_hours(config) reads the period from routing.budget_period_hours, defaulting to six hours, and the key is declared in reckon/schema/flight.yaml and regenerated into reckon/_flight_schema.py and docs/_shared/flight.schema.json.

The window rate is the trailing change in utilisation over the span the readings actually cover, measured over a three-hour horizon and withheld when the published or recorded readings inside it are less than thirty minutes apart, so a lane two percent into its window reports what it is spending now instead of dividing a fraction of a percent by a week. The window's even share is what is left of the window over the hours to its reset, quoted for one period. Under a credits lift the pool is the cap less its reserve less the credits drawn since the grant; the credit rate is the trailing fall in balance and the runway is the pool over that rate, falling back to the window's own remainder over the window rate with no lift. With a lift all four credit fields are present, null where nothing can be measured; with no lift none of them is present.

A lane's window rate and its even share are read on one window. A recorded block's utilisation_pct is the binding window — whichever the probe reported furthest through, never a fixed horizon — and the block names it: _CodexDialect.read_probe records rate_limit_type as the position it was binding (primary/secondary) with rate_limit_period_minutes as that window's length, while budget_from_rate_limit_info records the period name itself from unifiedWindows. budget._window_key resolves both through WINDOW_MINUTES_CLOCK, the module's one table mapping a window length to the name the headroom document keys the same window by (five_hour is 300 minutes, seven_day 10,080), so one table answers the question for every reader in the module rather than two that can drift apart; a length the table does not name keeps its own key rather than borrowing a name it does not measure. The window the newest recorded reading places then governs both figures: the series is filtered to it and _published_point is asked for that window by name, where a walk taking the first figure the document listed took the five-hour window on a document publishing both. A credit balance is window-independent and survives the filter, so a point on another window still dates a credit fall.

The picker derives the block once per scan in reckon/crew/picker/snapshot.py, keys it by the view row's declared group — _group_name resolves the row's own name, falling back to matching the declaration by the row's members where the composer left the row unnamed — and hands it to the candidate. A local lane, and a lane belonging to no metered group, carries none. Candidate.burn holds the mapping, _candidate_state renders it only where a candidate carries one, so a row with no burn block renders exactly as before, and the glossary defines every field it can carry: that the even shares are references rather than limits, and that a credits-lifted lane's pool is spent by priority rather than by evenness. The series is the live pointers alone: the pick already read the ledger once for the same scan, and the ledger's completed runs stamp hours behind the trailing window.
