Each metered candidate's row carries its lane's burn

A new derived block in reckon/budget.py states a metered lane's current spending rate, the share of its remaining window it can afford over the coming period, and — under a credits lift — the credit pool it has left, the rate the pool is being drawn at and the runway those two imply. burn_block(project, group, …) derives one lane's block from that lane's recorded readings; burn_blocks(project, groups=…) collects the readings once and returns a block per group, so a picker scan derives each wallet's figure once rather than per model sharing the account. budget_period_hours(config) reads the period from routing.budget_period_hours, defaulting to six hours, and the key is declared in reckon/schema/flight.yaml and regenerated into reckon/_flight_schema.py and docs/_shared/flight.schema.json.

The window rate is the trailing change in utilisation over the span the readings actually cover, measured over a three-hour horizon and withheld when the two readings in that horizon are less than thirty minutes apart, so a lane two percent into its window reports what it is spending now instead of dividing a fraction of a percent by a week. The window's even share is what is left of the window over the hours to its reset, quoted for one period. Under a credits lift the pool is the cap less its reserve less the credits drawn since the grant; the credit rate is the trailing fall in balance and the runway is the pool over that rate, falling back to the window's own remainder over the window rate with no lift. With a lift all four credit fields are present, null where nothing can be measured; with no lift none of them is present.

The picker derives the block once per scan in reckon/crew/picker/snapshot.py, keys it by the view row's declared group — matched by the row's members where the composer left the row unnamed — and hands it to the candidate. A local lane, and a lane belonging to no metered group, carries none. Candidate.burn holds the mapping, _candidate_state renders it only where a candidate carries one, so a row with no burn block renders exactly as before, and the glossary defines every field it can carry: that the even shares are references rather than limits, and that a credits-lifted lane's pool is spent by priority rather than by evenness.

The node's new test file asserts ten cases. The window rate reads 8.0 points an hour from 4% to 20% two hours apart at 2% elapsed, where the whole-window density the floor withholds reads null; a lane 96% through with six and a half days left affords about 0.15 points over six hours; the published headroom document supplies the current point alongside a recorded reading two hours old; a 10,000-credit lift reserving a tenth whose balance fell from 60,559.94 to 59,559.94 leaves an 8,000-credit pool under a 10,000 cap; balances an hour apart differing by 2,000 give a 2,000-credit rate and four hours of runway; 48 hours to the lift's end over the six-hour period gives a 1,000-credit even share; no lift leaves all four credit fields absent; one reading rates nothing; two readings ten minutes apart are one sample; and a pick's rendered state carries the burn block on the metered candidate and none on the local lane.
