one-automatic-repair-round-per-run — the reflex opens at most one automatic repair round per run

A finding-bearing review composes one repair for its round, keyed on the head it read, so a run reviewed again at a later head composed a second round and fired its own repair: one field run was resumed on three successive reviews scoring 82, 83 and 83 for exactly that reason. reckon/crew/recovery.py now records on the reviewed run's pointer every round the reflex has opened — a resume or a dispatch that actually started — and once one round has opened, a later round hands the run back to its coordinator instead. The hand-back records repair_dispatch status: handed-to-coordinator, naming the earlier round, its attempts and the new review's score, and resumes or dispatches nothing. The record advances once per distinct round, so the in-round retry of a resumed turn that ended unanswered is unchanged.

Measure. tests/test_one_automatic_repair_round_per_run.py (resume and dispatch stubbed) covers the first round resuming, a second round on a new head handing back and resuming nothing, and the in-round retry still working in round one.
