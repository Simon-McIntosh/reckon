an-all-follow-on-round-says-so — a round with only follow-ons records why it composes no repair

compose_repair_for_run returns None for a finding-bearing record in two cases: every finding is a follow-on, so nothing blocks and there is no work to answer, or the record the composer re-read differs from the one selected, a race the round leaves for a coordinator. dispatch_repair_for_run reported one string for both, so an all-follow-on round was misattributed to the re-read race.

reckon/crew/recovery.py now tells them apart from the findings it already holds: when none of the round's findings blocks, the reflex returns and records the reason the review round carried no blocking finding (N follow-on finding(s)), naming the follow-on count, and the re-read-differs case keeps no repair composes for this review round.

Measure. tests/test_repair_answers_only_blocking_findings.py gained a dispatch-level case driven through the reflex with the launch stubbed: an all-follow-on round records status: decline-only and a reason naming 1 follow-on, and a control arm with one blocking finding still reaches the dispatch. The gate ran only that file: 6 passed, EXIT=0.
