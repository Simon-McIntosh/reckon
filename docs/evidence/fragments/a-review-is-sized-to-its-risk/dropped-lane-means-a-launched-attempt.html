§5 — A lane is dropped only by an attempt whose worker launched

_failed_review_backend (reckon/crew/recovery.py) now returns a lane only when the recorded review_dispatch entry is for the run's current head, its status is dispatched, and the review run it named was not withdrawn before a worker launched. A dispatch refused at admission, one recorded as awaiting-lane, and one whose review run's own exit record says the attempt ended during launch with no stream byte read no longer demote the lane, so the next sweep may compose onto it.
