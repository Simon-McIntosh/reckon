Landed-work review, repair and re-review run without the orchestrator

What landed

A stored run review now carries an id on every mapping finding, stamped by the store from the shared repair.finding_id over the finding's own file, line and text. A repair answers a finding by that id, and the lifecycle reads the answer back, so the record on disk names the identity an answer targets rather than leaving each reader to re-derive it.

The reflex opens a repair round per reviewed head for up to three rounds and then hands the run to its coordinator: the fourth review opens nothing, dispatches nothing, and records one escalated dispatch naming the rounds spent, the review's score and the open findings with their stable ids. The record is written once per round, so a repeated sweep adds no second escalation. The re-review brief carries the diff from the head last reviewed to the new one and the answers already recorded on the review.

A disposition against a stored review is accepted when the run's head descends from the reviewed head, which is the state a hand-resumed repair leaves behind: the descent reader searches the staging store and the committed tree, and a divergent head still refuses.

Measure

The focused review/repair gate is 89 passed on the changed tree. The same targets on the base revision carry one pre-existing failure: a test compared a store-stamped findings record against its unstamped input, and now derives the expected id from the same function the store uses.

Negative control: restoring a one-round cap makes the second-round, third-round and escalation arms fail (3 failed, 2 passed), and the limit is restored afterwards.
