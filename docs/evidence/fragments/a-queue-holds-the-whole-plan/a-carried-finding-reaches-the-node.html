A carried review finding reaches the node, and a unit is reviewed at most twice

What changed. Answering a plan-review finding by editing the plan reopens the review gate, whose next review raises the next finding, so a section could loop. A finding can now be answered --carried instead: it is recorded against the unit its anchor names, closes the finding for the dispatch gate, and is delivered to the node that builds that section.

The answer. record_response in reckon/crew/plan_review.py accepts a third action beside acted and declined. A carried response stores the unit the finding is about under section, resolved by finding_unit: an anchor shaped <path>#<section> names its unit outright, and one shaped <path>:<line> is resolved through the reviewed text's own section spans (_line_unit_map, built from _plan_html.plan_headings). An anchor naming neither is the document unit rather than a guessed section. Because every recorded action closes its finding, the carried answer opens no review: the plan's fingerprint does not move, so no fresh review of edited content is owed.

The delivery. Every dispatch passes through plan_node in reckon/crew/dispatch_plan.py, which now appends the section's carried findings to the node's done-when under a Carried review findings: heading (carried_findings_for_section and with_carried_findings). Reading the answers at that chokepoint covers queued, re-dispatched and direct launches alike, and a section's node only ever receives the findings carried to its own unit.

The cap. review_burst_refusal counts the stored reviews that read a unit at the digest it carries now — a review of the whole plan counts for every unit when its fingerprint matches — so two reviews of one edit burst are the most a unit may spend. A third is refused, naming the open findings its author must carry or decline. A fresh edit is a new digest, which starts the count again. The command that composes a whole-plan review consults it (compose_burst_refusal, called from the review command's wrapper in reckon/crew_run_commands.py), so a third composition is refused before anything is dispatched; a dry run is left alone, so a caller can inspect the refusal before hitting it.

The surface. reckon crew review --answer FINDING --carried is the third answer beside --acted and --declined. Its answer branch lives in reckon/crew_run_commands.py and runs the review verb's own answer branch with the action named (plan_review.answering_as), so the covering-review resolution — the delivered-store read, the design-review fallback and the two-review ambiguity refusal — exists once. A carried answer joins the same heading on a later dispatch rather than being dropped because the heading is already there: the clause is keyed on the finding ids the done-when already names.

Measure. The gate command PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests/test_a_carried_finding_reaches_the_node.py reports 4 passed, EXIT=0. The declared negative control — dropping the carried text from the dispatch — fails the reaches-the-node arm (1 failed, 3 passed, EXIT=1), so the delivery is shown to be load-bearing rather than incidental.
