The review cap reads only what the composition reads

What changed. A composed plan review reads the units the plan is still owed a review of, but the cap that refuses a third review of one edit burst counted every unit digest in the document. A section no edit had touched therefore held the composition at its limit forever, and the plan could not be reviewed again. The cap also counted a design review against the content budget, so one design review beside one content review on the same prose read as a full burst and refused the second content review.

The defect, reproduced on the live store. compose_burst_refusal("reckon", "a-review-gate-judges-what-it-covers") at the base revision 0a44066307d7 returned unit s1 of reckon:a-review-gate-judges-what-it-covers has had 2 reviews for this edit, and a third is refused; carry or decline its open findings: none. s1 carried exactly one content review and one design review at one revision, both covering the section's present prose, and review_coverage reported the plan as covering s1 — so the refusal named two reviews of a unit the composition would not read, and named no open finding for the author to answer.

The unit set. compose_burst_refusal in reckon/crew/plan_review.py now takes its unit set from review_coverage's uncovered set — exactly what the composition will read — and returns None when nothing is uncovered. A covered section's stored reviews are never consulted, so a long-lived plan is not refused for the reviews its neighbours have already had.

The rubric. counts_for_rubric decides whether a stored review spends the budget of the rubric being composed, and both reviews_for_unit_burst and review_burst_refusal take a rubric argument (defaulting to the content rubric, which is what a composition composes). A record naming no rubric counts with the content rubric, so an older record is neither dropped nor able to refuse a design composition. A design review is a different read of the same prose and never spends the content budget; a content review never spends the design budget.

The cap still bites where it should. An incomplete review covers nothing, so a unit whose two reviews each left out an item of their own rubric is still uncovered, is still read by the composition, and its third review is still refused naming the open findings to carry or decline. A fresh edit is a new digest and starts the count again.
