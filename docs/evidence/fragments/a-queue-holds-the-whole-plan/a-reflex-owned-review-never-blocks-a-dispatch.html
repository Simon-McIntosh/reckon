overdue_unreconciled_runs excludes a scoring run while its session's armed reflex owns the review and its delivery age is inside the sweep window. It asks obligations._reflex_owns_review and reads the window from the follower cadence. A run without an armed follower, or beyond that window, still refuses a direct dispatch.

The existing sweep and refusal mechanisms meet the other two cases. After twice the window with no review in flight, the next sweep launches one review, records its id and the delivered head, and composes a brief naming that head. An undisposed blocking finding refuses the sweep, is recorded on the pointer, and gives the obligation the same cause and its crew dispose remedy, with no promise that a follower will compose the review.
