A queued request keeps its current holding reason

The admission pass now records the hold it acted on in the queued pointer's pass_hold and reason. A live claim uses the freshly read holder pointer, so the record names its run, session, phase and path. A launch refusal on a scope conflict or unreconciled runs keeps the named cause through a later retry backoff while those runs remain live. A repeated reading does not rewrite the pointer or renew recorded_at; a changed reading replaces it once.

The obligations view reads that pointer record. A terminal holder raises one duty on its own session naming the waiting request and its promotion command. Once that session has been unattended for six hours, the waiting session also receives the duty. The refreshed claim snapshot is shared by the claim decision and its record, so a second pointer read cannot race with a promotion.
