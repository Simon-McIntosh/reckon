A stopped host names its reason

SessionHost.stop takes its reason as a required argument, and the two paths that previously left the final census's stop_reason null now name one: the request descriptor reaching end-of-file records request stream ended, and the entry point's finally records host exited. The bare request_stop() fallback keeps the signalled reason. tests/test_session_host.py and tests/test_dispatch_session_host.py pass 24 tests with a bare EXIT=0; the two new tests fail with stop_reason: None when the end-of-file reason and the signalled fallback are removed.
