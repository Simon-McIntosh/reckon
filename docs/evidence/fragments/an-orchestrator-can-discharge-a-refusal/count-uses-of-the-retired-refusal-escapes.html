Evidence and controls

  • Coordinator sessions, new arm: 152 known, 72 with a transcript on disk, 23 with records in the window. Those 23 completed 2,201 of the 2,916 runs the week completed — 75 per cent — so absolute counts are a floor and the rate is measured over most of the fleet's work.
  • Comparator arm: the census's 39 frozen transcript snapshots and its inputs/runs.json.gz cohort, re-read with this instrument. The census itself reported neither count, so the comparator column is this measurement of that corpus.
  • Positive control: the scanner recovers the command for 143 of the 164 comparator promotions the ledger records as waived (87 per cent) and 117 of 166 (70 per cent) in the new arm.
  • Regime control: the ledger's corrective-run:* impl-move exemption reason is absent from the census window and present 327 times in the new week, so the two arms compare two regimes and not two readings of one.
  • Excluded from every figure: 2,604 text mentions of the flags in the new arm and 2,657 in the comparator, empty flag values, --outcomes, heredoc and script bodies, and sidechain records.
