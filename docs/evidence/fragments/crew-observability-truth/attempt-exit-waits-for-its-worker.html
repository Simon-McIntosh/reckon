An attempt's exit record waits for the worker it launched

Landed. The supervisor calls prctl(PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER) before it spawns, so a launcher that exits ahead of its worker hands the worker to the supervisor rather than to init; the supervisor reaps it from /proc parentage under the same terminal-manifest grace and stop bounds, and the attempt's exit record carries the worker's pid and its exit. A spawned child that ended by signal ended the attempt itself, so its signal is recorded at once rather than awaited through the leftovers it leaves.
