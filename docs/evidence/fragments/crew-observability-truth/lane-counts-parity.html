Execution record · 2026-10-01

The lane-counts parity is measured against the fixture

The case that pinned the carried lane counts compared the dispatch payload against a second reading of the same document through read_lane_counts, so the comparison was the reader against itself — a regression inside it moved both sides and the case stayed green. It now holds both sides against the counts each fixture document states as a literal. The same review found four lane-document keys the carry still spelled beside the reader that owns them; those reads now resolve through lane_document.read_lane_reading_fields, so the dispatch region that composes the reading holds no spelling of the document's keys.

§1 — What the review found

Two defects, both from the review of the node that moved the counts and the rate through the lane-document reader.

The parity case was the reader against itself. At base, test_the_document_reader_returns_the_counts_the_payload_carries in tests/test_lane_reading_carries_counts.py dispatched each fixture document, took the payload's generating and waiting, and compared them against read_lane_counts(written) over the same bytes. Both sides are the reader's answer, so a regression inside the reader moves both and the case stays green — and a reader regression is exactly what the case exists to catch. This is the general shape the repository keeps finding: a check whose two sides derive from one source reports agreement it cannot establish.

The carry still spelled four of the document's own keys. At the reviewed revision _lane_reading_carry in reckon/crew/dispatch.py resolved observed_at at :2661 and mean_context, binding_observed and suggested_shelf_life_seconds at :2692–:2696 through document.get(...) — a second reader of the same document beside the module that exists to own it (reckon/crew/lane_document.py). The counts and the headroom had already been routed; these four had not.

§2 — What moved

reckon/crew/lane_document.py gained read_lane_reading_fields(document), which resolves the stamp the reading carries and the figures published beside it: observed_at as published, mean_context, binding_observed (blank or null withholds the field alone) and suggested_shelf_life_seconds; a document carrying no string there resolves to None with a non-empty detail. The reader resolves the document's names and returns the stamp as published: what a date may look like is the carry's contract, and its strict parse stays in _lane_reading_carry. That retention is pinned by tests/test_dispatch_timestamps_reuse_the_parser.py, which records that the lane reading is the one place in dispatch.py that keeps datetime.fromisoformat rather than the shared parser, because the shared parser swallows the refusal the reading must quote. A first pass at this change moved that parse into the module and reddened the pin; the parse returned to the carry.

_lane_reading_carry — the region that composes the carried reading — now calls that reader and its sibling readers for every document-derived value it reports; grep -n 'document\.get(' reckon/crew/dispatch.py returns nothing. The keys the region still names are the payload's own output field names, whose values come from the readers:

§3 — The repaired case

The parity case now compares two independent things against the fixture's own statement. Each entry of the fixture list carries the document and the counts it states, and the case asserts both read_lane_counts(written) and the payload's generating/waiting against that literal. The payload is compared against the fixture's statement rather than against the reader's answer, so the two sides have independent provenance.

A new case, test_the_dispatch_carries_what_the_document_readers_resolved, patches both readers to sentinel answers — counts, and a stamp with the three figures — and requires the payload to carry them. The sentinel stamp is 23 seconds old where the document's own is 5, so the carried age proves the reading was dated from the payload's stamp rather than the document's; a dispatch holding its own reads of the document would carry the document's figures instead.

§5 — Boundaries

  • The change stays inside the lane-count reads. Other live nodes edit follower conditions, section routing and member resolution in reckon/crew/dispatch.py; this change touches only the _lane_reading_carry region and adds no name to it.
  • No figure. This node's done-when names no figure, and the change is a reader ownership move with no spatial or plotted relationship that a figure would carry better than the table above.
  • The carried shape is unchanged. The values, the unknown-not-zero rule and the detail strings are the ones the tests pinned before this change; the routing moves the keys and the strict stamp parse, not the semantics.
