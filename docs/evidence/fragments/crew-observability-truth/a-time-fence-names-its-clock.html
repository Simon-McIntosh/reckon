a-time-fence-names-its-clock — the fence names its clock

The dispatch prompt's FENCE — TIME line gave a bare duration, so a worker had no clock except the one it asked for and estimated elapsed time from the work it had done. The fence now states the attempt's launch instant and its deadline (launch plus the declared budget, ISO-8601 UTC) and names date -u as the command that checks them. dispatch() takes one clock read that is both the attempt record's launch instant and the instant its prompt states, so the two cannot disagree; resume_plan restates the fence on the launched resume prompt from the same instant the resumed launch records, with the node's recorded time_budget. Implemented in reckon/crew/prompts.py and reckon/crew/dispatch.py at e273934d4, plus one assertion in tests/test_crew.py whose pinned byte-equality predated the restatement (tests/test_a_time_fence_names_its_clock.py, 4 passed).
