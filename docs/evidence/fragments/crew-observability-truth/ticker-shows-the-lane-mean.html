The regression this repair closes

The first landing read the mean from event["lane_reading"], a key the watch path never sets: _watch_snapshot and _watch_transition rebuild each event field by field, and the live pointer's lane reading is not among them. The cell therefore returned nothing on every real row, and because it also withheld the run's own rate, the live pane lost the per-row generation rate it rendered before without gaining the lane's mean in its place. The test did not catch it because it built its events by hand — the shape the watch path produces is the shape the test now uses.

The cell now resolves the row's backend in the same four-layer flight configuration a dispatch reads, takes that backend's declared lane_document, and reads it through reckon/crew/lane_document.py: the reader judges freshness from the document's own stamp and shelf life, and resolves the throughput block's mean. A document that is absent, unreadable, past its shelf life or publishing no numeric mean yields the phrase; only a measured mean is printed. The read is held for a short reuse window, so a pane drawing a burst of rows resolves the config and reads the lane once rather than per row.

Two limits are stated rather than hidden. A row that names no backend adds no cell — the document is declared per backend, so such a row has no lane to read, and it keeps the exact shape it rendered before. And the cell is an addition the pane must have room for: at the grid's minimum width the fixed columns fill the pane and the cell is dropped rather than allowed to wrap the row, which spends the clause's margin and never the pane's edge.
