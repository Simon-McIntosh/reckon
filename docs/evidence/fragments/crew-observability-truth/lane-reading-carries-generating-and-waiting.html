Lane reading carries the lane's counts and its achieved rate

The advisory carry a dispatch rides on described the lane's window but not its work: it published a headroom, a binding constraint, a mean context and a stamp, and nothing about how much the lane was doing. A coordinator weighing two lanes had to open the lane document a second time, which is exactly the compensation the carry exists to remove. The carry now resolves the lane's own counts and its achieved rate in the same single consult, and every figure it cannot resolve is withheld as unknown rather than resolved to zero.

§1 — What the carry could not say

At the base revision the carried reading resolved four figures from the document: headroom, binding_observed, mean_context and observed_at, plus its own state, age_seconds and detail. The base gate test asserts exactly that set (tests/test_lane_reading_at_dispatch.py::test_payload_carries_the_four_reading_fields_from_the_lane_document), so the four-field shape is the recorded starting point rather than an impression. The live lane document read by this node on 2026-09-28T15:16:33Z published running: 7, waiting: 0 and no throughput block at all: a dispatcher reading only the carry could not tell that lane from an idle one.

§2 — What the carry resolves now

reckon/crew/dispatch.py at e6cf69e4 adds three keys to the fresh carry and a helper per concern. Resolution is per field, so a document that omits one figure still carries the others.

Carried keyResolved fromWhen the document is silent
generating the document's generating, else its running — the lane's current publication names its generating population running unknown, never 0
waiting the document's waiting unknown, never 0
throughput the document's throughput block: mean_tokens_per_second, aggregate_tokens_per_second and the runs they were divided by, stamped by the block's own observed_at when readable and by the reading's stamp otherwise a block with state: "unknown", all three figures unknown, and a detail naming why the rate was not measured

The counts stay apart on purpose: a lane serving seven requests while three queue is not a lane serving ten, and the two figures are what let a coordinator choose a lane that is finishing work over one that is accumulating a queue. The rate travels with its vintage and its denominator because either alone misleads — 1.44 tokens per second means nothing without the window it was achieved over, and the age says whether that window is still the lane's present. A rate is dated by its own stamp when the block carries one, so a rate sampled earlier in the window is not carried as if it were as fresh as the counts beside it.

Existing semantics are untouched. Whole-carry collapse still happens only for document-level defects — no document, one that will not parse, an unintelligible observed_at, or a reading past its stated shelf life — and the top-level detail still stays empty when the pre-existing figures resolve, so a healthy reading is not made to look degraded by the new keys. On the live document above the carry reads generating: 7, waiting: 0 and throughput.state: "unknown" with the explanatory detail, which is the forward-compatible path: the lane begins publishing a rate and the carry picks it up with no further change.
