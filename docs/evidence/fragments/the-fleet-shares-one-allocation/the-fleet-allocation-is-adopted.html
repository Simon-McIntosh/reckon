Adopt the live fleet allocation

ensure_reservation adopts a live supervisor-running fleet allocation into the one unkeyed record at crew_home()/placement/reservation.json, reading its partition, cores and memory from the scheduler and submitting nothing; with a record naming that job and a live probe it reports already-held, and with neither it keeps its hold behaviour. The resume launch path resolves placement exactly as a dispatch does, so a resumed worker of a placement-declaring backend runs as an overlapping step in the adopted job, and a backend declaring no placement is launched untouched.
