A re-exec reaps the children it inherits

The adoption that runs at the in-place image replacement now also registers every pid the kernel lists as this process's own child, read through recovery_liveness._process_children, with no _LAUNCHED_WORKER_RUNS entry, so the existing reaper collects them silently. Before this, a child alive across an execve stayed the process's child while every object naming it vanished with the old image, so nothing waited on it and it stayed a zombie for the life of the process. A pid carried through the environment keeps its run record unchanged, and a child the new image starts afterwards is never registered, so its own Popen keeps its exit status. queue_admission.admit_until_stopped and recovery_watch.watch_ticker call the adoption once at start-up, as the follower already did.

Measure: tests/test_a_reexec_reaps_the_children_it_inherits.py passes 5 of 5 at this change, and the file does not exist at the base revision. The importer gate over the 124 test files touching the three changed modules ran 1212 passed, 3 failed and 6 errors at this revision; every one of those ids is also present at the base revision (test_crashed_producer_reoccupies_the_seat[crash] and test_repeated_crashes_reach_a_reported_limit reproduce on an unmodified extraction of the base tree, and the six test_dispatch_returns_without_worker errors do too), so the change adds no failure. test_resume_reaps_its_child failed once under eight-way parallelism and passes 6 of 6 at both revisions when run alone. Negative control — the child scan skipped — leaves the inherited child in state Z and fails the first case with EXIT=1.

  • A process starts a child, replaces its image with a script that calls the adoption, and the child — exiting after the replacement — has no process-table entry within the reaper's poll interval.
  • A child the new image starts afterwards and holds as a Popen reports its own non-zero exit status from wait().
  • A launched-worker pid handed over through the environment keeps its run record.
  • The admission loop reaches the adoption exactly once at start-up.
  • The producer loop reaches the adoption exactly once at start-up.
