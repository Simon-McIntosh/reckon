A service waits for its in-flight dispatches before it re-executes

What changed. reckon/crew/dispatch_claims.py keeps a process-wide register of the dispatches it owes a worker: run_id to node id. It is entered where a node dispatch claim becomes this process's (_claim_node_dispatch), and left where that claim is given up (_claim_released_on_refusal, _NodeDispatchClaim.release) or where the launch reaches a process (_start_supervisor, the seam every fresh dispatch, resumption and review launch passes through, and _spawn_detached_worker for the in-process launches and probes). One bounded wait, wait_for_dispatches_in_flight(), serves the station (StationReloader.poll), a follower (_FollowerReloader) and the admit service (_reexec_into_current_code) immediately before each os.execve; none of them keeps a wait of its own. The bound is the module constant REEXEC_DISPATCH_WAIT_SECONDS, which the test patches, because a dispatch that never reaches its worker must not hold a reload open for ever. What the bound gives up on is logged by node on stderr, so a claim a later dispatch reclaims can be traced to the reload that left it.
