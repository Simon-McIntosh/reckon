Every summary lists open and recently closed sprints

Discovery's read_plan(project) and roadmap(view="summary") both call roadmap.sprint_summary_rows, so the two surfaces cannot disagree about which sprints are open or recently closed.

A row carries the sprint id, theme, stored status, the liveness fields, member and pending counts, completion percentage and, for a closed sprint, its close date. Liveness is the read the fleet pane uses; a stored marker is never consulted.

Ordering is live first, then open, then recently closed. The window defaults to 14 days and is read from flight config as sprint_recent_days.
