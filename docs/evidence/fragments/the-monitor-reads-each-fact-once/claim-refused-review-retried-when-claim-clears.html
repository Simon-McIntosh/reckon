A claim-refused review is composed again when the claim clears

A review dispatch refused on a write-scope claim is recorded on the reviewed run's live pointer under review_dispatch with status: refused and the refusal's own reason text. Before this change that record carried no machine-readable statement of which claim refused it, so no later sweep could tell a refusal whose cause still stood from one the release had already lifted; the reflex re-asked the same question on every sweep and recorded the same refusal, a rejection loop that read in the obligations view as a follower to inspect.

The attempt now records the holder's run id and the claimed path. The reflex asks that holder's own live pointer through the claim disposition every other claim reader uses: while it binds, the reflex composes nothing and records no second refusal; on the sweep after the holder is promoted, discarded or stopped with an empty worktree, the dispatch composes again. The obligations duty names the holder to clear rather than a follower.

Measure

One test on a temporary crew home drives the reflex's review pass three times. Pass 1, with a live review of an older revision holding the reviewed run's record claim, is refused and records claim.run_id naming the holder. Pass 2, holder still live, dispatches nothing and records no new attempt. Pass 3, holder's pointer removed, dispatches the review. Negative control: a build that honours the stored refusal unconditionally fails the third pass.
