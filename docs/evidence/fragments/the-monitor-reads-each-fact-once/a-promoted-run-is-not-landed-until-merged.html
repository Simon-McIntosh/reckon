A promoted run is not landed until its head is merged

The field the follower read landed from. _recorded_pointer_word (reckon/crew/recovery_watch.py) answers promoted the moment the run's ledger row exists, and the pane maps promoted to its landed word (PANE_STATES, reckon/crew/ticker.py). Promotion writes that row before the delivered head is merged, so a landing interrupted between its promotion and its merge read landed while its change was on no branch.

Change (commit 5f81f71e4c). classify_pointer (reckon/crew/recovery_classification.py) now reads the landing's own pending record — one file under the crew home that crew complete --land writes before the promotion and removes only once the run's delivered head is on the primary branch. While that record exists the run reads complete (the pane's delivered word) instead of promoted, and its detail names the landing's own status: awaiting-merge-repair when the landing pass recorded a merge conflict, awaiting-merge otherwise. The landed word returns when the landing pass clears the record after the merge, and the run's departure then carries it.

Gate. tests/test_a_promoted_run_is_not_landed_until_merged.py builds a real repository whose primary branch does not carry the delivered head, plus a live pointer, a completion manifest and the run's ledger row. The first case adds the pending landing record with a recorded merge conflict and asserts the follower's own state is not promoted, is complete, and its detail contains awaiting-merge-repair. The second case merges the delivered head into the primary branch locally and clears the record, as the landing pass does, and asserts the state is promoted. tests/test_staged_write_inventory.py also imports the changed module.
