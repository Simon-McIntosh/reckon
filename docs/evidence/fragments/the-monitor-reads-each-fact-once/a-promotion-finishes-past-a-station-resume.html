A promotion finishes past a station resume

The defect. A promotion writes its ledger row and its landing commit, and only then retires the live pointer. A station resume can take the pointer inside that window, so the removal's compare-and-remove meets the attempt of a launch it never read and refuses with changed while promotion ran. The row is already written at that point, so the run is promoted and its pointer is stranded: in the recorded incident the coordinator stopped the run twice and discarded the new review by hand.

What landed. Cleanup asks whether the attempt now on the pointer recorded its own end — its attempt-N-exit.json, the same receipt that frees its launch reservation — and, when it has, removes the pointer under the pointer lock while it still names the attempt that was read and returns the promotion to its remaining stages. A pointer taken by a later launch after that reading is left to its own promotion.

When the newer attempt has not ended, the refusal now names the attempt and the process that launched it: the attempt number from the pointer, and launcher_pid, launcher_host and supervisor_pid from the attempt's own attempt-N.reservation. The pointer is kept and marked pending_landing_repair as before, so a live worker never loses the file it owns and the reviewed head still has a landing path.

Two cases, one pointer that moved

The negative control. Restoring the base reckon/crew/promotion.py over the head tree and running the same command reports 2 failed in 50.25s, EXIT=1. The first case fails because the promotion completes its row and then refuses: run ... changed while promotion ran: attempt 4 was recorded after this promotion read the pointer. The head arm passes that case, so the refusal is the behaviour the change removes rather than a passing suite that never exercised the guard.

The reservation read a refusal quotes is the launch module's own _read_reservation and _reservation_launcher, and the test proves its pointer path resolves inside its temporary crew home instead of reading the operator's live directory.
