No steady reader reads a whole stream

What was wrong

Three production readers asked the events cache for the whole stream, so each paid a whole read for every stream the 32 MB byte bound had evicted: _latest_attempt_capacity_refusal (needs the latest terminal event), _admission_refusal (folds three flags across the stream) and observe_log's fallback. The per-stream summary already held a resume cursor and folded observations; it held nothing these three could answer from, so the cursor never reached the census's 42.7 % row.

What changed

Each reader states its question as a fold from a seed over the records in order, and _backends.cached_stream_fold keeps that state in the per-stream summary: the whole stream is read once, and every later tick folds only the bytes gained. The observation fold is keyed by the records — backend settings and receipt — and not by the caller's elapsed time; a new elapsed reading is applied to the folded state, so a tick that passes a tick-later clock misses no fold. The rewrite check samples one record's opening (1 KiB) instead of 8 KiB and a growth read carries the fingerprint it just verified, because that sample is paid once per stream per tick.
