A refused landing is finished at the head it cited

crew complete --land writes its pending-landing record before it promotes. The record now carries the revision the landing cited, read from the run's own tree before the promotion runs. The pass that finishes an interrupted landing merges that revision and nothing later: a resume or a repair round moves the worktree, the live pointer and the ledger row to a head no review read, and none of them can substitute for the citation.

A cited head already on the primary branch clears the record with nothing merged. A live pointer whose run the ledger already holds a row for is skipped by the lander as landed-elsewhere before its reviews are read, so a promoted run is never held awaiting-review at a head a resume reached.
