The worker manifest field follow_ons is renamed outside_scope, and the entries it carries reach the dispatching coordinator instead of being converted into plan followups.

What changed

  • reckon/crew/reports.py names the field outside_scope in _MANIFEST_LIST_KEYS, and maps the retired spelling onto it in the parser on the way in, dropping the retired key so it is never written back.
  • The conversion followup_ops_from_manifest and its export from reckon.crew are deleted.
  • reckon/crew/promotion.py carries the entries on the promotion report under outside_scope and writes the extracted paths on the ledger row under outside_scope_paths.
  • The worker prompt asks for the new field and states the rule: the coordinator answers each entry as a plan section or as an instruction in the next dispatch's prompt.
  • Both skill references (worker-protocol.md, coordinator-reference.md) tell the coordinator to answer each entry, and neither names the deleted conversion.

Negative control

Restoring followup_ops_from_manifest and its export reddens the case that asserts the conversion is gone.

Outside scope

Test files that call the deleted conversion or assert the retired manifest key directly red at head, and are outside this node's write scope; their ids are recorded in the manifest under follow_ons so the coordinator can grant them.
