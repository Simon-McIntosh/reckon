A finding's declared severity is blocking or non-blocking. FINDING_SEVERITIES and NON_BLOCKING_SEVERITIES name those two words, the review and figure-review prompts teach them, and every emitted message that names a severity names the current word. repair.follow_on_findings is renamed repair.non_blocking_findings, and the recovery reflex's two messages carry no retired word.

A record stored under the retired spelling follow-on reads as non-blocking: review.declared_severity maps the retired word to the current one on the way in and never writes it back, so an older review's judgement survives the vocabulary change.
