read-every-spelling-of-deferred-work — the deferred-work rule reads the whole census and is refused at every body write

_DEFERRED_WORK_TERM in reckon/doccheck.py now matches every spelling the census found, in forms that name their object so ordinary prose is not caught: carry, carries or carried followed by a section number; go or goes followed by a section number; the run-together followon and the spaced follow on beside the hyphenated forms; and an onward phrase (left for, deferred to, sent to, going to, go to, goes to) aiming at a follow-up, later, next or separate node, section, plan or figure, with up to two words between the parts so go to a follow-up figure node reads as one statement. Every group stays non-capturing, because deferred_work_terms reads the pattern with re.findall and a capture group would put a tuple where the refusal quotes text. The <pre> and <code> exemption and the non-match on a landed-section citation such as landed in §4 are unchanged.

The store refuses the same term at every site that writes a comment or followup body: the comments branch of _apply_append, its followups branch, _carry_section_moves (a section move's reason is written into a comment), append_to_list for followups — the route the followup-add operation takes. followups.<id>.body is refused in apply_followup_field_set, where the field is assigned. All of them call one new predicate, _refuse_deferred_work_term, built on the one prose reader, so the audit warning and the store refusal widen together. A sections.<id>.body set already reached the same reader through _require_authored_value, so it widened without a change.
