A local worker compacts and continues instead of dying

The question

The local lane is expected to compact a conversation that approaches its window and carry on, and nothing in this repository had shown it doing so on this lane at this window. This node dispatched one local worker whose task is designed to exceed the lane's usable window partway through — a synthetic corpus of eight parts, 2 000 lines and roughly 55 000 tokens each, read in order with a small written result after each part — and recorded what the run did at the boundary.

Result: the worker compacted and continued. The run's own stream carries six compact_boundary records. The worker wrote its opening result before the first of them and its closing result after the sixth, all eight of its recorded checksums equal the values planted in the corpus, and the run ended by writing a complete manifest rather than by dying at the boundary.

The probe

The worker is its own probe: no second dispatch, no sub-agent, no background task. Its brief told it to read the corpus parts in order and append one line per part holding that part's planted checksum, and the corpus was built so that the anchors could be obtained only by reading it. Part NN carries, at file line 1 000, a line naming CHECKSUM-NN; the eight expected values live in a separate file the worker compared against only when it finished.

  • lane: clive, model deepseek-v4.1-flash, effort xhigh, time budget 90 minutes
  • declared window: effective_input_window = usable_input_window = 230 144 tokens
  • corpus: 8 parts × 2 000 lines, about 222 KB and 55 000 tokens per part, about 440 000 tokens in total
  • results: measure/probe/results/0-before.txt, readings.txt, 9-after.txt and compactions.txt, all under the run directory

Reading was done with the file-read tool only. Reading the corpus through a shell tool (grep, sed, head, wc, python) would have defeated the measurement, and the brief said so: the reading is what grows the conversation.

Six compactions, all automatic

Each boundary is a stream record of type system, subtype compact_boundary, triggered auto. The turn column counts the assistant records preceding that boundary in the same stream, so it is the worker's conversation depth, not wall clock.

Every boundary compresses the conversation down to between 21 052 and 30 955 tokens, and every boundary fires between 196 889 and 205 512 tokens — at 86–89 % of the declared usable window. Between 24 632 and 33 255 tokens of the declared window therefore go unused at each compaction: 24 632 at the fifth boundary, whose pre-token count is the largest, and 33 255 at the fourth, whose pre-token count is the smallest. That is the same operating point the pre-existing census of the local lane found (median 197 386 tokens across 190 boundaries in 164 streams), so these six boundaries are ordinary for the lane and not an artefact of the probe.

Each compaction also announces itself first: five to nine system/status records carrying status: compacting arrive immediately before each boundary. The announcement and the boundary are separate records, and the gap matters — the same census found two local streams that announced a compaction and recorded no boundary, and those are the streams that died.

Results written either side of the boundary

Each reading is the planted checksum of one corpus part, appended with the UTC time of the append and matched to the stream position at that moment. The measured thing is not that the worker put text on disk; it is that the checksum it reported is the value planted at file line 1 000 of a part it could only have read. Eight of eight match.

Three of the six compactions landed inside a part rather than between two: boundary 1 between page reads of part-01, boundary 3 between page reads of part-04, boundary 5 between page reads of part-07. Two more landed between a part's last page and the append that recorded it (boundary 4 after part-05, boundary 6 after part-08), and one fell between two parts (boundary 2, between the part-02 append and the first part-03 page). The worker resumed inside a part from the retained context each time: the reading of a part that straddled a boundary still produced that part's correct checksum.

Figure

The figure is drawn by plot_compaction_drops.py from the run's own stream, not by hand from the table, so it cannot drift from the data. Its labels are written as vector paths rather than as text elements: the picture therefore carries its own axis titles, tick values and annotations wherever it is rendered, including a rasteriser that has none of the fonts the plotting library names.

What this supports, and what it does not

It supports the positive arm of the plan's measure on this lane at this window: a dispatched worker whose task outgrows the window compacts mid-task and carries the task through to a complete manifest, with results on both sides of every boundary. The death arm the plan describes is not exercised here — no boundary was reached without a following boundary, and the stream's last record at the time of writing is an assistant record rather than a terminal error. The census above is where that arm's evidence lives: 125 of 164 boundary streams reached a result record, and two announced a compaction without recording a boundary. Those two do not share a terminal signature, and the difference is the datum the death arm turns on: r-20261018T192200408612-review-of-tcv-liuqe-known-answer stops with a system record of subtype status, while r-20260926T195126315924-resume-offer-needs-a-dead-process reaches a result record carrying is_error: true and the text Prompt is too long — its census row records last_type: result. A reader building the RCA from the census must therefore read each row's own terminal record rather than generalise one stream's signature to the other.

Two limits on the reading. The task writes a result after every part, so this run shows that results already on disk survive a boundary; it does not show what happens to a manifest that is still unwritten when the boundary arrives. And the corpus is synthetic and homogeneous — plain sequential reading — so the run does not measure how a conversation carrying many tool results of varied shape compacts.
