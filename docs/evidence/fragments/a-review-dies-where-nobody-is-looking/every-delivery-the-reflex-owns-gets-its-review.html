Every delivery the reflex owns gets its review

What changed. The reflex's dispatch decision withheld a review on two defences that a caller could not see. The first was a resumed attempt's delivery: the classifier judged it against the run's first dispatch while the reflex judged the same delivery against the resume baseline, so a repair round that rewrote its manifest while it ran read as delivered to the classifier and not delivered to the reflex, on every sweep. The reflex refused the review of the delivered head with the resumed attempt has not delivered; no review is composed, recorded nothing, and the review-missing duty went on promising a duty the reflex kept declining. A delivery now also settles on the supervisor's exit record for the attempt it names, so both readers agree. The second defence was the whole early-refusal family — a resume still launching, a recorded process not yet gone, a deferred obligation — which the read-only refusal predicate did not ask. Both the dispatch and review_dispatch_refusal now ask one shared predicate, the dispatch records the refusal on the run it acted for, and the review-missing duty names that refusal and the command that dispatches the review by hand instead of sending its reader to inspect a follower with nothing wrong with it. The duty reads the refusal the run recorded when the read-only predicate would not answer — a dispatch refused at the launch leaves its reason on the run, and that is the refusal a coordinator needs — and the hand-dispatch command it prints comes from one builder shared with the code that renders it.

Measure.
