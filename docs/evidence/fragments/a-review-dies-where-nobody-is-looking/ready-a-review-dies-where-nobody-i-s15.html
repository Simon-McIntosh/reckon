A reopen never launches on a refused advice

crew resume --reopen --write-path P fell through into the resume it was combined with, so it started an attempt whose advice had been composed for the state the run had left, and consumed the advice a refused resume was holding for the next one. A reopen now widens the run's write paths and launches nothing; it reports the refused advice still waiting, and the caller's separate resume carries that text ahead of its own, as before.

The defect: a widen that started a worker

Measured 2026-11-12: crew resume --reopen --write-path reckon/crew/admission/launching.py on r-20261112T064442039104 launched attempt 4 at once. Its advice was resume-refused-advice.txt, which an earlier resume had refused because attempt 3 was in flight; the launch consumed it into attempt 4's resume-3-advice.txt. The caller had asked only to widen the fence, and the attempt started on instructions written for a state the run had left.

The reproduction is the negative control recorded below: at the base revision the same three cases fail, because the reopen reaches _resume and starts an attempt.

What changed

  • reckon/crew/resumption.py gains reopen_run: it widens the claim through widen_claim, clears the delivered verdict exactly as a resume does, records the grant, and returns a payload naming the refused advice waiting. REOPEN_RECORD_NAME and _record_reopening move here from the command module, beside the refusal and delivery-reset code they drive. waiting_refused_advice reads the slot without consuming it.
  • reckon/crew_run_commands.py: the --reopen branch delegates to reopen_run and returns, so no attempt follows. Combining --reopen with --advice is now refused: there is no turn for advice to enter, and accepting it would look like it had been delivered.
  • tests/test_a_reopen_launches_no_worker.py is the gate. tests/test_crew_families_fold.py carried the old expectation that a reopen launches; it now asserts the grant and launched: false.

The gate

  • test_a_reopen_with_advice_waiting_starts_no_attempt — a resume is refused with advice while a launch is in flight, the launch ends, and the reopen then grants a new path. No resume-*.jsonl appears, the pointer keeps its attempt, pid and phase, the launch seam is never called, and the output carries launched: false and the kept advice text and path.
  • test_the_resume_after_a_reopen_carries_the_refused_advice_first — the caller's next resume composes the kept advice into its prompt ahead of its own text, and the slot is emptied as it is read.
  • test_a_reopen_with_nothing_waiting_names_no_advice — an empty slot reports empty rather than a stale one.
