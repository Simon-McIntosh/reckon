Landing record — the census instrument and its review-missing sampler

The census behind §1 lived in a coordinator's scratch directory and could not be re-run by anyone else. This node commits it as scripts/census/monitor_census.py, beside the two existing census modules, with its co-located test. It reclassifies every transition in every project's watch stream through ticker.stream_carries and ticker._pane_word, so it counts what the pane actually showed rather than the raw transition volume.

Three halted kinds are counted, one per false-halt mechanism the plan repaired, all read from the run's own directory beside the stream because a row alone cannot separate them. process_alive: the row's own event records process_alive: true while its destination draws halted. returns_to_progress: a later row for the same run draws working, delivered, in review or landed, with no attempt claim between the two. after_status_repair: the row was printed within 60 seconds after the run's status-repair.json and no attempt claim falls between the repair and the row.

Claim times are compared at second granularity at both bounds, strictly on each side: a claim whose truncated time equals either bound's second is not between, because an attempt claimed in the same second as a row cannot have run. The rule is pinned by its own test arm — a claim 0.4 s into the halted row's own second and a claim 0.9 s into the progress row's second both leave the row counted, while a claim 30 s inside the interval excludes it.

The module also carries the review-missing sampler. Each review-missing duty in every session's obligations snapshot is judged by obligations._current_review_in_flight on the reviewed run's own live pointer — the reader that gates the duty when it is published. A duty counts as the defect when that reader returns true and the snapshot's computed_at is at or after the review pointer's launched_at; a snapshot computed before the launch counts separately as stale_snapshot. Counted pairs are appended to ~/.cache/reckon-population/a-live-worker-census/review-missing-samples.jsonl. The sampler is committed, not started: a crew worker has no turn after its last one.
