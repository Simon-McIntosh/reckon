reservation-states-its-reach — the reservation states its roster cap's host-wide reach where it is armed

Defect. A placement is declared per project, but the record it resolves against is one unkeyed file at crew_home()/placement/reservation.json and the roster cap is summed over every project's placed live runs. A session reading its own flight configuration therefore cannot see that arming the reservation places a ceiling over every project on the host: the scope of the declaration and the scope of the thing declared do not match, and the blast radius is invisible at the point of the decision. The hold detail named only the cap — held reservation 1274099 on all with 32 cores and 128 GB; roster cap 25 — and the published record carried no reach at all.

Fix. Both moments at which a reservation is armed state the reach. _roster_reach() reads the live pointers through the same population and the same liveness reading the dispatch guard counts with (occupying_the_reservation over list_live()), names the projects whose placed runs are counted at that moment, and builds one statement. The hold detail and the adoption detail both quote that statement, and the record each publishes carries it as a roster_reach field (scope, projects, statement), so a reader of the reservation file sees the same reach the arming session was told. Runs that are not placed are not named: the cap counts placed runs only, and the statement is read at the moment the record is published rather than copied from the arming project.
