Landing record: a stranded launch releases its review claim

A dispatch cut off between publishing its pointer and spawning the supervisor leaves a pointer at a pre-spawn phase with no pid, no worker record, no stream and no launch log. Nothing about it is in flight, and nothing ended it, so it held its claim — including the review claim a scoring run was told was covered. The review reflex keyed a standing review on the pointer alone and waited on it indefinitely.

The classifier now reads a pre-spawn pointer with no launch evidence past a 900-second bound as a stranded launch, named in the launch-failed family with a clause saying not even an end was recorded. The reflex releases the claim: it discards the pointer, writes the reason onto the discard marker and onto the reviewed run's dispatch record, and composes the review again in the same pass. A pointer inside the bound is still a young launch and stays in flight.
