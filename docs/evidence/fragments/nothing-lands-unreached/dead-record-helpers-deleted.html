Deleted reckon/crew/reports.py::_is_quoted_scalar, reckon/crew/repair.py::reviewed_run_test_paths (with its sole-user _is_test_path and _TEST_PATH_PREFIX), reckon/crew/placement.py::clear_reservation and reckon/crew/placement.py::reservation_step_argv — 66 lines removed, nothing added; grep -rnw for each over reckon/ tests/ skills/ returns no hit. The census's other five reckon/_schema.py candidates (_valid_status, _valid_links, _valid_capability, _consistent_section_records, _serialize_authored_transition) are kept: they are @field_validator/@model_validator/@model_serializer methods that pydantic calls by registration, not by name, and each fires — reproduced by constructing a SectionRecord with an off-enum status and a PlanState whose section record disagrees with its declaration, both of which raise.
