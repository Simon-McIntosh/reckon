How the leaning cases resolved

Case 9's lane count reads one under the live wall clock because the probe renders the surface with no injected time, so the schedule's own 60-hour retention filter drops every fixture bar against stamps fixed on 2026-09-02 to 09-04. The explicitly-clocked sibling tests pack two lanes, which is what establishes the schedule is correct and the test is the defect — the migration injects the fixed clock rather than moving the expected number.

The pager case was the opposite finding: this plan's decision section records that the reader plan carries a section titled The readout counts one behind its own position, so the current behaviour may be a real defect that the assertion is correctly catching. That case was left entirely alone here; its repair belongs to the reader plan, and nothing in this record counts it as landed.
