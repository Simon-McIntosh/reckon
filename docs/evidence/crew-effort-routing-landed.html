a model comparison: luna produces thinner work than solan 8-minute fence against a 40-minute one — 115 insertions against 806, same model, same node, same base

a pre-existing failure at a base revisiona row a later commit had already fixed, reported from a tree that did not have it

The corollary is the uncomfortable half. None of the three contained a false number, and all three would have survived review — because reviewing a measurement checks its arithmetic, not its attribution. Every one was caught by re-measuring under a changed condition, and none by anyone re-reading the record. A fourth was avoided the same way and not by inspection: a green suite at 819e63d was about to be read as evidence for a change that came down a sibling line, and an ancestry check rather than a careful reading is what stopped it.

So the operational rule is not "be careful with baselines". Prose telling a reader to be careful cannot catch this class, because the reader has nothing to compare against. Vary one condition and see which recorded conclusion fails to survive. The shadow re-run in this plan is the worked precedent: holding every recorded condition of the primary equal and substituting only the model turned a confident model verdict into a budget artifact in a single dispatch. This is the same argument as AGENTS.md's own — a rule without a bound check decays — applied to measurements rather than to naming, and it is why both halves of that finding went to binding-rules-need-bound-checks rather than becoming four repairs.
