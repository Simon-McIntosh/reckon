§4 — the review reflex asks before it re-queues

Run r-20261015T125110840273-the-review-reflex-asks-before-it-requeues, merged into main as 7722ccd7d at reviewed head 676130afb. This builds the last sentence of §4. Before this change, the reflex composed a review onto the local lane every time. When that lane's router granted the session no worker, the review was held and queued, and the next sweep queued it on the same lane again.

reckon/crew/recovery_review_dispatch.py gains _zero_slot_held_lane. It reads the lane named by a recorded hold (status lane-paused or awaiting-lane) and asks that lane's published admission whether it grants the session nothing, through dispatch_plan._dispatch_lane_allowance, the same reader §2 gave the picker. That check tells a zero-slot hold apart from a paused gate. It also gains _reroute_held_review, which asks the picker once with that lane left out of the offer, using _config_without_lane. The answer then decides the outcome:

  • a route carries the review to the lane the picker named;
  • a hold or a refusal is recorded as awaiting-lane rather than overridden;
  • a fallback keeps the existing path.

Both asks are written to the run's dispatch record. The question is asked only when _review_lane_candidates offers another lane. Without a lift, review_excluded_backends leaves no other lane, so the retry re-queues locally exactly as before.
