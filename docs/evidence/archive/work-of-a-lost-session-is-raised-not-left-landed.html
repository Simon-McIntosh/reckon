Landed on 20 and 21 November 2026

All 33 sections are on the primary branch. A coordinator session is read as lost from the instant it was last heard. Its implement work is raised as one decision that the lead answers by adopting each run into a live session or retiring it; its reviews retire; waits that can no longer resolve are removed and idle ones reported. A worker's treatment is stated once, keyed on its lane and role, so the local harnesses behave alike.

Observed live on 21 November. The first admit pass after the decision section landed raised two lost sessions, last heard on 27 October and 8 November, holding three runs. All three were adopted through the decision. Two had their commits on the primary branch already and their pointers were retired; the third stays queued behind its upstream under the adopting session.

Gaps recorded by the code reviews and not acted on. The drainer's lane change in the resumed-lane section has no case that fails if it is reverted. The landed-head settlement requires a ledger row, so a delivery the primary branch carries with no row still replays its gate. Both are non-blocking findings on their runs' review records.
