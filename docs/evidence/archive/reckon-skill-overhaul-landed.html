Reckon skill overhaul — cumulative execution record

The evergreen plan owns the design and decisions. This record preserves the observed execution failures, the commits that repaired their contracts, and the measures that make the result independently auditable.

Live use exposed guidance that review had missed

Five defect groups were found by sessions actively using the ship skill, not by a static review. In four of the five, the correct rule already existed somewhere, but the acting agent had no trigger to open it at the decision point. The repair principle was therefore locality and executable intent: put the contract where the action happens, make examples agree with emitted commands, and test the behavior the guidance must induce instead of pinning incidental wording.
