§5 — The check, and the refusal it produces

The dispatch record now carries the plan's parsed impl at dispatch, on both the live pointer and the ledger row. Promotion reads the plan's impl at completion and compares the two for implement and test roles whose gate passed. When they are equal the promotion is refused, quoting both figures and the plan's remaining sections, before either store is written:

run 'r-20260918T080000000000-node-a' promotes a passing implement run, but plan
'plan-a' impl did not move: 0.5 at dispatch and 0.5 at completion. Sections
still to land: s2, s3. Advance the plan's impl as the work lands, or record why
it did not move with `reckon crew complete --run ... --gate passed
--no-impl-change REASON` (the reason lands on the ledger row); if the plan's
impl is not this run's to move, state that reason

The waiver records its reason on the ledger row beside the dispatch figure, and the exemption is recorded as data rather than as silence: each exempt run carries impl_move.verdict = "exempt" with the reason that exempted it (role-not-enforced:<role>, failure-classification:<classification>, gate-not-passing, no-impl-recorded-at-dispatch).

An unreadable or unset impl is recorded as absent, never as a zero, so a plan that has never moved cannot be confused with a plan unreadable at dispatch. The plan reader lives in the promotion module and is imported lazily by dispatch, so the figure recorded at dispatch and the figure compared at completion come from one implementation.
