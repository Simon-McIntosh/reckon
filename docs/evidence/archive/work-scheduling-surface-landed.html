§5 — The check, and the refusal it produces

The dispatch record now carries the plan's parsed impl at dispatch, on both the live pointer and the ledger row. Promotion reads the plan's impl at completion and compares the two for implement and test roles whose gate passed. When they are equal the promotion is refused, quoting both figures and the plan's remaining sections, before either store is written:

The waiver records its reason on the ledger row beside the dispatch figure, and the exemption is recorded as data rather than as silence: each exempt run carries impl_move.verdict = "exempt" with the reason that exempted it (role-not-enforced:<role>, failure-classification:<classification>, gate-not-passing, no-impl-recorded-at-dispatch).
