§7 — The context fit gated on the boundary the lane actually refused at

The defect, reproduced from the recordings. The plan records a run that passed the pre-dispatch context fit at an estimate of roughly 285k against a declared usable_input_window of 480000 and was then refused by the endpoint after three records, with no deliverable. Reading every recorded run stream under ~/.config/reckon/crew/runs whose last result is an error carrying the refusal text finds 18 such runs, of which five were served on the flash model this lane carries. Every one of those five announces a context window of 200000 tokens in its own modelUsage block, against the 480000 the configuration declares — the declared figure overstates what the endpoint accepts by more than a factor of two.

What landed. reckon.crew.context_budget.refusal_census reads every run stream and keeps those whose last result is an error carrying the refusal text, taking each run's estimate as the context window its own result announced for the model it was served on, and returning the lowest of them as the effective boundary. A boundary belongs to one lane, so the census is narrowed to the lane's own model — pooling every model returns the minimum across lanes, which is the narrowest neighbour's figure rather than this lane's — and the runs that narrowing excludes are reported alongside rather than dropped. The estimate is deliberately the lowest figure the recording supports rather than a usage count: the rejected request produced no usage record of its own, so the announced window is what bounds it from below, and a run carrying no modelUsage block is reported unmeasured rather than zero, so it cannot move the boundary. reckon.crew.routing._context_fit_verdict then gates on the smaller of the declared window and a declared effective_input_window, and a refusal inside the band between the two names both figures. Either figure may be absent: a lane declaring no window is unbounded and its recorded boundary alone gates it, and a lane with no recorded boundary keeps its declared window.
