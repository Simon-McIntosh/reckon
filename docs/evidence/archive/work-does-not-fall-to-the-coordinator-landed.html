§5 — Per-node wall time and width-at-start

reckon/crew/summary.py:run_rows returns the project's committed run rows, each carrying two derived figures: wall_seconds from that row's own dispatched_at and completed_at, and width_at_start from the measured spans of the same row set at the row's dispatch instant. The derivation reuses reckon/ledger.py:_worker_seconds rather than restating the subtraction, so the read and the ledger agree on what a span is by construction. Landed in 20496d11.

What reaches a reader, and what does not

A figure the row's stamps cannot support is None, never 0 — an absent measurement and a zero-second span are different facts and the second is a plausible-looking lie. A ratio of 0.99 between the widths at two dispatch stamps that are close together is possible, so no rounding or floor was applied; the counts are integers over the measured spans. No surface reaches this read. crew ledger --view summary resolves to ledger.summary (reckon/cli.py:3122), the aggregate, which carries no per-run rows; crew ledger --view records reaches ledger.runs (reckon/cli.py:3117), the rows before either figure is derived.
