§7 — a watch producer sweeps the whole fleet every second

Each producer cycle lists every project's live pointers, walks run directories, parses streams and calls git on a 1 s beat whether or not anything moved. That costs about 1,880 opens, 1,575 stats and 1.25 git-shim calls per cycle, which is 114 to 115 git executions in 32 s, independent of the live pointer count. Five producers make roughly 9,000 GPFS opens a second and about 35 of the node's 80 process creations a second. The change-driven fix is to gate the sweep on inotify, or on one mtime stat of the crew home, and keep only the liveness probe on the beat. §3 — a stopped worker ends inside its step

A step-placed worker is stopped by signalling the processes below its sandbox's PID-namespace init. The step task (the outer bwrap) and the srun client's local subtree are never signalled, and the escalation is SIGKILL to the same inner processes. The supervisor reads the task identity from a record its wrapper writes before it execs the sandbox (job, step, pid and start tick). It checks membership from the task's cgroup and start time. The wrapper execs only after its record is written. Live probes on the fleet allocation, where the worker and its child both ignore SIGTERM against a 55 s grace: steps 1281970.6964, .6972 and .7012 ended FAILED|9:0 in about 60 s, the sender receipts name only the inner pids, and the node state was unchanged. The controls were step .6508 (client signal, CANCELLED) and step .6958 (signal to the outer task, CANCELLED|0:15 in 3.02 s). A run whose step has not started is ended by one client SIGTERM, by pid, sent only when squeue lists no step of the fleet job named after the run (each placed step is named with --job-name). §6 — a worker step carries a memory bound

Every fleet worker step now carries an explicit --mem from placement.step_prefix. It is 24 GB by default (DEFAULT_STEP_MEMORY_GB, overridable with RECKON_STEP_MEMORY). fleet_node.placement_argv composes through it, so the bound is stated once. A node that needs more declares it through the existing dispatch override, which replaces the whole options list: --set 'backends.<backend>.placement.options=["--ntasks=1","--mem=48G"]'. One parser reads the declaration for both placement and the admission summary, in both --mem=N and --mem N forms. A declaration above the 48 GB ceiling leaves the shared 96 GB allocation and runs as srun in a fresh allocation on the partition's _debug sibling, with its SLURM_* job identity removed and --time at most an hour. Its stdio and its stdin prompt stream as a step's do (live jobs 1289800 and 1289936 on all_debug). A probe step placed with --mem=1G shows cgroup memory.max = 1073741824. Over its bound, a step swaps first; the node has 4 GB of swap, and the step's own memory.events max counter records the pressure while the job's OOM counter stays unchanged.
