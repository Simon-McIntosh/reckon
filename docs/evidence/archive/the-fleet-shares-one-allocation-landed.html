The block carries no `job_id_probe`, and that is a measured deviation from the field list this
node's brief gives. No probe can answer for an `--overlap` step under the current composition:

  named `bwrap` (`squeue --steps -h -j 1277272 -o '%i %j'`; the argv in §4 shows `bwrap` is the

  `JOBID.STEPID` (not all-digits, and the probe parser accepts only `token.isdigit()` —
  `dispatch.py:6415-6417`), and the only step-valid format fields are `%i %j %N %M %P %u %A %S %b`
  (measured field-by-field; `%T` is invalid for steps).

Declaring a dud probe would record `job_id_status: "probe answered no identifier …"` on every

substitute `{run}` into a step `--job-name` (or otherwise name the step after the run) and accept
