A lane reading degrades field by field

Landed 2026-09-21, commit abbce128. reckon/crew/lane_document.py reads one published lane document and resolves every field it carries on its own, so a missing precondition costs its own field and never the whole document. Before this, the reader that carried a lane reading into a dispatch resolved the document wholesale: the first field that did not parse returned unknown for the entire carry, and four fields the document actually publishes — running, waiting, concurrent_requests and kv_occupancy — were never surfaced at all.

The defect reproduced before it was repaired. A serving lane publishes its occupancy under _instant keys beside a null binding and a null mean context, with state: measured beside them. Driving the existing reader with that real shape at a fixed instant:

The sharpest form is a document whose headroom is present and numeric and whose mean_context alone is null: the old reader discarded a document that carried a real headroom of 96 and a real run count of 0, returning unknown for both, because one sibling did not resolve.
