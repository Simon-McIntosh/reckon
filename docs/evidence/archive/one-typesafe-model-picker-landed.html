§4 — Jev as the router: design and replay landed

Five nodes landed on 2026-10-02. picker-reuse-map named 28 existing definitions the picker wraps (review 87). jev-call-probe established the typed call: POST openrouter.ai/api/alpha/decisions, typesafe/jev-1.13, full option distribution at a median of about 0.3 s and about $0.00002 per call; typesafe/jev-router is a chat router with no distribution (review 77; durability below its floor, folded into the prototype's mocked client tests). run-time-profile derives median and 90th-percentile run times per backend, effort, role and spec level, plus the local lane's live load (review 90). picker-prototype built reckon/crew/picker/ with strict Jinja templates, a reused-gate candidate filter, the Decisions client, recorded fallback and reckon crew pick (32 tests, review 90).
