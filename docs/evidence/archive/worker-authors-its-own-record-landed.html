§3 — What the coordinator does instead

The coordinator half lands the same review in the coordinator's own prose. The landing beat now reads the worker's committed record — its plan section comment and its evidence anchor — merges it, and may edit or append to it: a review of an authored record rather than a transcription of a manifest. Both worker limits travel beside the landing contract, each with the reason attached: a worker must not resolve its own driving followup and must not set a terminal status, because only the coordinator observes the other nodes — a worker knows its node landed, not whether the section closed.
