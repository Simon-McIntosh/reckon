§1 — Product contract

Reckon will expose three live artifact classes. A plan authorizes and tracks work and is the only class with status-to-completion and an implementation percentage. Research precedes or informs work and records sources, methods, assumptions and recommendations. Evidence follows work and records what was built, how it was verified, quantitative results, provenance and negative outcomes.

§2 — Schema and relationships

  • Add first-class research and evidence types; retain doc only as a migration alias with an explicit target type.
  • Add qualified relations: informs from research to plans and evidence_for/verifies from evidence to project-qualified plan or stage keys.
  • Reject or ignore plan-impl, sprint, ROI and effort on non-plan artifacts; expose lifecycle-neutral fields such as reviewed-at, recorded-at, verdict and supersedes.
  • Keep archive as location/lifecycle, not a competing live document type.

§3 — Server and UI

Split the SPA into Plans, Research, Evidence and Archive facets. The Plans view retains roadmap, sprint, dependency and percent-complete UI. Research cards show source quality, freshness and plans informed. Evidence cards show verdict, stage, environment, commits, artifacts and plans verified. The graph renders arrows into plans for research and arrows out of plans for evidence. Search remains global but returns typed, bounded result sets.

§4 — Migration and gates

Inventory existing doc, research, archive and landed-stage pages; classify without inventing progress; backfill source-plan links; and keep compatibility reads during migration. Acceptance requires schema round trips, MCP filters, archive exclusion, static and live SPA parity, and an audit that reports unlinked research/evidence without treating them as incomplete plans.

§ Decisions

How should actionable work and its knowledge artifacts be displayed?

Research feeds work and evidence follows it. Only actionable plans have a meaningful percentage complete.
