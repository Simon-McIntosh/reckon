§1 — The symptom and the session

The power_convertor lead asked three times on 7 November why “the monitor is not showing any work”. The session was c71ae21b-bd1a-4f28-a356-27ade6673050 on clu-2068, building plan filter-recommendation-claims. Its coordinator, power-convertor-16, reported nine defects behind that one symptom. Three other sessions met several of them the same day: nova-8a lost three hours to one, and reckon's own admission work was blocked twice by another. This page gives each defect's cause as measured, the plan section that repairs it, and its state when written. Evidence for power_convertor's runs is under ~/.cache/power_convertor-filter-review/ and ~/.config/reckon/crew/runs/<run>/.

§3 — What a coordinator can do until the repairs land

  • A review-missing row that says the reflex owns it: read review_dispatch on the run's live pointer. If it is a refusal for an undisposed blocking finding, dispose the finding (folded when the new head fixes it) and the reflex composes the next review itself.
  • A silent pane, or no review after crew resume: run reckon crew follow --project <project> --session <session>, which makes the host re-arm the session's follower.
  • A run blocked on a read-only worktree (Errno 30): run crew stop on it, then crew resume. The tree at the path, and any uncommitted work in it, is kept.
  • Batch entries: set spec_level, negative_control and an integer time budget (90m, not 1h30m) before queueing.
