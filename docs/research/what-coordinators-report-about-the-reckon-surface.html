§1 — Method

On 2026-10-19 every live coordinator session on the workstation was asked for a return-on-experience report on the reckon surface. There were sixteen, all interactive sessions in a project's main checkout and none of them a headless worker. Each answered the same five headings: hand edits of plan HTML, CLI friction, drift between skills and tools, MCP availability, and the three changes that would cut most turns. Fifteen replied within two hours, and their reports are reproduced verbatim in §5.

Beside the reports, a census read the 37 coordinator transcripts under ~/.claude/projects modified in the four days before 2026-10-19. It counted every edit_plan call and its refusal code, every Edit or Write on plan, research or evidence HTML, and every reckon CLI verb invoked through Bash with its exit status. The CLI tree was read from the installed command group at d8ff471c9. The census counts calls, not distinct intents: a call retried after a refusal counts twice.
