Why. Work that waits on the lead's word is held in whichever session placed the hold, and the lead learns of it only by reading that session. On 15 November the local lane sat at 5 of 12 with room for more while seven nova requests waited on three decisions the lead had not been shown together: the basis choice for iron-core-magnetisation §5, how to end kernel-compile-barrier §2 after a partial result, and a repair-first ruling on sub-millisecond-forward-path. Finding them took eleven session messages and prose replies of differing shape, which one coordinator then summarised by hand. A session message is also a poor carrier for a decision: it can be held for approval, expire, or be lost when the receiving session's context is summarised, and a closed session cannot answer at all.

This plan makes a decision that waits on the lead a record, written where the hold is placed and readable from any session. One session presents the open records to the lead as choices and writes each answer back; the owner learns of the answer from the monitor, with no message to deliver. The first surface is the terminal.

A trial on 15 November put the same request to fifteen live sessions by message. Thirteen replied within about half an hour and two had not; nine decisions came back, one of them already settled. The lead answered the other eight, and one from the collecting session, in three sets of choices, choosing against the owner's recommendation in four. §1 — Every decision waiting on the lead is one read

Deliverable. One read, available from the command line and to any agent, returns every open decision that waits on the lead across the mounted projects. Each record carries the question, two to four sentences of context, two to four options with the owner's recommended option first, the owning project, session, plan and section, the time it was recorded, and what an answer releases: queued requests, runs, deferred sections, or a stated risk when nothing is queued. A record also states any action the answer needs from the lead, whether its recommendation comes from the plan's owner or from a reviewing session, and whether any live session owns the plan. A decision that will be needed only when a named run or gate reports is recorded as pending on it and is listed apart from the decisions that are ready. A decision a session has already taken under a standing rule may be recorded as taken and reversible, and is listed apart as well. The records are ordered by the amount of held work, largest first. An open decision already written in a plan's decisions block appears in the same read.

Done when. A test with a temporary crew home and two mounted projects records three decisions, one holding four queued requests, one holding one and one holding none, and reads them back in that order with every field present and the held requests named. A decision written only in a plan's decisions block, with no choice set, appears in the read with its plan and section. An answered decision does not appear, including one the lead answered directly in its owning session. A decision recorded as pending on a run appears in the pending list and not among the ready ones until that run reports. Negative control: at the base revision the read does not exist and the test fails at its first call; the failing run is logged to docs/evidence/fragments/lead-decisions-reach-one-place/s1.html. The tests run on all_debug.

§2 — A hold on the lead's word carries its decision

Deliverable. A queued request or a section can be held for the lead only together with a decision record. A hold that names the lead as what it waits on and carries no record is refused, and the refusal states the fields a record needs. Holds that do not wait on the lead are unchanged.

Done when. Tests with a temporary crew home show that a hold for the lead with a complete record is accepted and the record appears in §1's read holding that request; that the same hold with no record, or with a record that has fewer than two options, more than four options or no recommendation, is refused naming the field at fault and holds nothing; and that a hold with another stated reason is accepted with no record. Negative control: at the base revision the hold with no record is accepted; the failing run is logged to docs/evidence/fragments/lead-decisions-reach-one-place/s2.html. The tests run on all_debug.

§3 — An answer reaches its owner and releases what it held

Deliverable. An answer recorded as the lead's closes a decision, storing the chosen option, the lead's note if any, and the time. Only an answer given through the presenting session is accepted; a session cannot answer a decision for itself or for another session. The owning session sees the answer as an obligation naming the decision and the choice, and its monitor pane draws a row for it. The requests held behind the decision leave their hold at the next admission pass. A decision whose owning session has ended can still be answered, and its held requests are still released. A decision written in a plan's decisions block is still answered by locking it in its plan, and the read in §1 drops it once it is locked.

Done when. Tests with a temporary crew home show that an answer recorded as the lead's stores the choice, yields one obligation for the owning session and one monitor row for its project, and that the two requests held behind it leave their hold at the next admission pass; that an answer attempted by a worker or by a coordinator session outside the presenting path is refused and changes nothing; that a decision whose owner has no live session is answered and releases its request; and that a decision written in a plan's decisions block, once locked in its plan, leaves the read and releases the request held behind it. Negative control: at the base revision a held request stays held after the answer is written; the failing run is logged to docs/evidence/fragments/lead-decisions-reach-one-place/s3.html. The tests run on all_debug.

§4 — One session presents the decisions as choices

Deliverable. A reckon-select skill, run in any one session, presents every open decision to the lead as a choice. Each choice shows the owning repository and session, the plan and section, the context, the options with the recommendation first, and what the answer releases. Choices are presented largest held work first. The skill records each answer through §3, and its report marks each answer that differs from the owner's recommendation. Before presenting, it asks each live session once for any decision that waits on the lead and is not yet recorded, and asks the session to record it; a session that does not reply is named as unreached in the skill's report. Two sessions running the skill at once do not present the same decision twice.

Done when. The skill's contract tests pass with the skill listed beside the other reckon skills and every command it names resolving. A recorded rehearsal on the live fleet, with at least three open decisions from at least two projects, shows each presented with its repository, session, context, options and held work in held-work order, each answer recorded and its held requests released, and every live session either replied or named as unreached; the record is docs/evidence/fragments/lead-decisions-reach-one-place/s4.html. A test with two presenting sessions reads each decision claimed by exactly one of them. Negative control: at the base revision the contract test fails because the skill does not exist; the failing run is logged to the same fragment.

§5 — The read tells apart what waits, what was taken and what was withdrawn

Deliverable. The read from §1 sorts every decision into one of four lists, and each record says which it is in: ready, pending on a named run or gate, taken by a session under a standing rule and reversible, and written in a plan's decisions block with no work held behind it. Only the ready list is what §4 presents first. A decision written in a plan's decisions block has no owning session, no recorded time and no held work; the read reports each such field as absent by name, never as empty, and §4's presentation shows it as absent. A decision found to be already settled is recorded as withdrawn, naming the decision that settled it; a withdrawn decision appears in no list and releases whatever it held.

Done when. A test with a temporary crew home and two mounted projects records one ready decision, one pending on a run, one taken and reversible, and one written only in a plan's decisions block with nothing held, and the read returns each in its own list and in no other, with the plan-block record naming its owning session, recorded time and held work as absent. A second report of the ready decision, recorded as withdrawn and naming the first, appears in no list, and the request held behind it leaves its hold at the next admission pass. Negative control: at the base revision the taken decision and the plan-block decision both appear in the ready list, and the test fails on it; the failing run is logged to docs/evidence/fragments/lead-decisions-reach-one-place/s5.html. The tests run on all_debug.

§ Decisions

Which surface is built first?

The records are held outside any one surface, so a terminal skill and a browser page can both read them.

The lead works in the terminal, and the choice dialog already exists there.

In what order are decisions presented?

The lead answers a few at a time, so the order sets which work is released first.

Each answer then releases the most lane work available.

How are decisions that are not yet recorded collected?

Until §2 lands, sessions hold work for the lead without a record.

The record stays the source; the message only asks a session to write it, and a session that does not reply is named.

Which holds must carry a decision record?

A record is the question, its context, the options, the owner's recommendation and the held work.

Only a hold that waits on the lead's word is refused without a record. Other coordinator holds are unchanged.

Who may answer a recorded decision?

An answer releases the work held behind the decision.

A session cannot answer its own question or another session's, so no session can release work the lead meant to hold.
