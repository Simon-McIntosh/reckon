§1 — Why: reviewed, measured work halted on form

The lead's direction on 7 November: the crew must stop halting on checks that protect nothing, and must accept that a recorded way through is sometimes necessary. That day showed four such halts.

  • A negative control refused for its log's shape. Runs r-20261107T092649116030 (review 97, importer arms no added failure) and r-20261107T111357058603 (review 110) were refused at promotion because their control logs carried no terminal EXIT=<n> record or no failing test id, although the reviewer had checked each declared mutation. Both landed only with --waive-negative-control on the lead's instruction. The dispatch brief never states the control-log contract the promotion reader enforces (reckon/crew/promotion_evidence.py near lines 1021–1092).
  • A budget fence with no way through. nova session 35a66cc6 could not dispatch nodes whose gate is a 2–5 h H200 or Sirius job: 240 m, 120 m and 90 m were each refused with “budget exceeds the resolved fence 60m” (reckon/crew/node.py near line 1363).
  • A resume that cannot change its budget. crew resume reuses the stored time_budget, so a run blocked on budget can only be discarded and dispatched again, losing its session and colliding with its own leftover worktree.
  • A picker budget below the work's own compute. The picker gave six brief-based nova nodes 8 minutes each; three needed H200 or Sirius jobs, wrote a checkpoint at the deadline and blocked with their measure unmet.
  • Plan reviews re-owed for answering a review. Coordinators spent three to seven content-review rounds per plan, because an edit that does exactly what a stored finding asked still uncovers the section and owes a new review, which finds the next detail.

Gate time not counting against the budget is owned by test-time-is-not-the-nodes-time; a keyed waiver for every refusal is owned by an-orchestrator-can-discharge-a-refusal §4. This plan makes the specific halts above judge substance and gives each a recorded way through.

§7 — A queued launch is not refused on its coordinator's backlog

Why. Queued work exists so that it runs when a lane frees, whether or not its coordinator is awake. On 7 November the local lane stood at 5 of 12 while request r-20261024T225749536157-skills-load-then-steer, queued since 24 October, failed at launch on each admit pass with UnreconciledRuns: project has 6 unreconciled run(s) older than the 15m grace and went into retry backoff. reckon/crew/queue_admission.py near line 407 passes unreconciled_override from the request's stored allow_unreconciled_runs, which is false for an ordinary dispatch, so dispatch (reckon/crew/dispatch.py near line 1837) re-applies the coordinator's backlog at launch. The six runs were delivered reviews waiting for the coordinator to promote them. A coordinator stopped by a usage limit cannot promote anything, so the guard stops every queued launch exactly when the queue is meant to carry the work. The guard's purpose, making a coordinator reconcile before it asks for more, is served when the request is queued, not when the lane frees.

The admission launch passes the override and records it on the run as an admission waiver, for example {"requested": true, "source": "admission-launch"} plus the runs it covered, so the ledger shows the launch was not a coordinator waiver.
