§1 — Why: refusals that guard nothing

Every surveyed coordinator lists refusals among its wall-clock costs, and the ones they name guard a form rather than the work: the goal lint refusing a semicolon, "and", "then" or a quoted <path> in a single-deliverable goal (s21 lost about eight dispatches to rewording); the watcher-required refusal firing after a follower's lifetime ends while runs are still in flight; the impl-must-move refusal on corrective nodes, waived "about every other time" (s23); --outcome demanded on review runs; and verify-gate refusing a checkout two ledger commits past the merge (crew pattern review §3). The review that motivated this plan was itself refused for the word "TODO" inside a done-when that named a marker to count (§9). The validator also caught two real brief defects in one session; those must keep refusing.

§2 — The contract lint refuses placeholders, not punctuation

Deliverable. The single-goal and fully-specified checks refuse an unresolved template placeholder (an angle-bracket token outside quotes or code, a goal that is only a placeholder word) and a goal naming two deliverables joined by an action verb; they no longer refuse a semicolon, "and" or "then" inside one deliverable, or any word inside quoted text or a done-when.

Done when a table-driven test replays the refused goals named in the survey and this review's "TODO" done-when as accepted, and replays the two brief defects s22 reported, plus a goal that is two deliverables, as refused.

§3 — A re-armable follower is a follower

Deliverable. When the session has an expired follower whose seat is intact and runs still in flight, dispatch re-arms or warns and proceeds instead of exiting 8; the refusal remains for a session that never armed one.

Done when tests cover both cases, and the warning names the attach line.

§4 — Bookkeeping follows the run

Deliverable. A run whose lineage marks it a repair, resume or re-cut of a landed node promotes without an impl-move waiver; a review run's outcome defaults from its stored review record; verify-gate accepts a checkout whose history contains the merged revision rather than only the merged revision itself.

Done when each of the three has a named test, and over a week of coordinator transcripts the count of --no-impl-change and --outcome uses on corrective and review runs falls to zero.
