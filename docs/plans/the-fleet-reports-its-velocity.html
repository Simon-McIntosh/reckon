§1 — Why: the fleet cannot see its own rate

The review of the dispatch pattern needed six study nodes to answer how much work lands and how fast, and the most important trend it found — promotions up 77% while source output fell 27% in the week review became mandatory — was visible to nobody while it happened (crew pattern review §4). The burn analysis needed three SLURM scans of 2.9 GB to price a run, the run store's backend column is empty on every row since 20 September, codex resume streams report thread-cumulative usage so summing them overcounts by 57%, and the budget preflight never projects the weekly window forward, so it did not hold when the codex window reached 87% 57 hours into its week (reckon-a0, §3). The census scripts written for the review work; they belong in the product, not in a data directory.

§2 — A velocity view

Deliverable. crew(view="velocity") and reckon crew velocity, computed in Python from the committed ledgers and primary-branch history, reporting per project, lane and day: promotions by class; product (source, tests) and record (plan, evidence, research HTML, figures, state) lines landed; the seven-day deletion share of mature product lines; dispatch-to-completion and dispatch-to-promotion median and p75; attempts per landed node; and the review share of promotions. The implementation starts from docs/research/data/crew-pattern-review/velocity/census.py and keeps its denominators and censoring rules.

Done when the view reproduces the review's §4 headline figures within 1% on the same pinned window, and a parity test compares the view's JSON with the census's compact output.

§3 — Coordinator cost per landed node

Deliverable. The view joins each promoted run to its coordinator session through node_definition.coordinator and reports coordinator turns and input and output tokens per landed node, using the coordinator-overhead census as its reference.

Done when the figure agrees with that census's per-session totals for the window.

§4 — Quota runway and session hygiene

Deliverable. Before each wave the preflight reports, per metered lane and role, runs remaining until the weekly reset from measured quota per run, and holds a wave that would exhaust the window before its reset; per-run burn uses per-attempt deltas rather than summed cumulative streams, and the run store records the backend. The view also reports, per day, the share of dispatches that inherited another task's session, so a regression of task-keyed sessions shows the same day.

Done when a fixture of three resumed codex streams prices to the rollout totals rather than 157% of them, and the preflight holds a synthetic wave projected past its reset.

§5 — Work in progress is visible

Deliverable. The view reports, per project and ISO week, plans opened and plans closed (shipped, done, archived or superseded), and the count of pending plans.

Done when it reports reckon's 60 opened and 9 closed for the fortnight ending 2026-09-26, matching the review's §9 count.
