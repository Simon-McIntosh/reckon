§1 — Why: the fleet cannot see its own rate

The review of the dispatch pattern needed six study nodes to answer how much work lands and how fast. Its most important trend — from the week of 14–20 September to the week of 21–26 September, when review became mandatory, promotions rose from 830 to 1,473 (+77%) while source lines landed fell from 85,685 to 62,775 (−27%) — was visible to nobody while it happened (crew pattern review §4). The burn analysis reported by the reckon-a0 coordinator in the review's survey (§13) needed three SLURM scans over 2.9 GB of streams to price a run; found the run store's backend column empty on all 851 rows written since 20 September; found that codex resume streams report thread-cumulative usage, so summing them gives 157% of the rollout totals; and found that the budget preflight never projects the weekly window forward, so it did not refuse a wave when the shared codex window reached 87% 57 hours into its week. The census logic written for the review works; the product should carry it, while the study's scripts stay where they are as the review's reproduction artifact.

§2 — A velocity view

Deliverable. A product module, reckon/velocity.py, ported from docs/research/data/crew-pattern-review/velocity/census.py with its denominators and censoring rules, serving crew(view="velocity") and reckon crew velocity for a window the caller names. It reports, separately by project, by lane and by day, and in project-by-lane-by-day cells: promotions split into implementation-class and review-or-investigate; lines landed on the primary branch in six classes — product (source, tests), record (plan, evidence and research HTML; figures; docs/state) and other, the last reported on its own; the seven-day deletion share, meaning deletions within seven days over additions that have a full seven days of follow-up; dispatch-to-completion and dispatch-to-promotion, each with median and p75; attempts per landed node; and the review share, meaning review-or-investigate promotions over all promotions.

Done when a parity test runs the view over the review's pinned window (2026-09-12T00:00:00Z to 2026-09-26T10:00:00Z, primary heads as recorded in the census's summary.json) and matches that file's totals and per-project rows — counts within 1% relative, shares within 0.5 percentage points — including the week-over-week pair quoted in §1.

§3 — Coordinator cost per landed node

Deliverable. For each coordinator session named by a promoted run's node_definition.coordinator.runtime_session_id, the view reports the session's assistant responses, input tokens (cache reads reported separately) and output tokens, divided by the number of nodes that session landed in the window. Runs with no coordinator identity are counted in an explicit unattributed bucket.

Done when over the pinned window the per-session figures agree within 1% with docs/research/data/crew-pattern-review/coordinator-overhead/overhead.json.

§4 — Quota runway and session hygiene

Deliverable. Before each wave, the preflight reports for each metered budget group and role the runs remaining before the weekly reset, from the mean quota cost per run over the trailing seven days, and refuses to open a wave whose projected cost exceeds the remaining window, naming the runs remaining and the reset time. A resumed run's burn is its cumulative total minus the previous attempt's last cumulative total. Dispatch writes the backend into the run store. The view reports, per lane and day, the share of all dispatches that continued another task's session.

Done when a fixture of three resumed codex streams prices within 1% of their rollout totals rather than at 157%, and a synthetic wave projected past its reset is refused with the runs-remaining figure in the refusal.

§5 — Work in progress is visible

Deliverable. Per project and ISO week, the view reports plans opened (the week of the commit that added the plan file) and plans closed (the week the plan's status became shipped, done, superseded or abandoned, or its archive flag was set, counted once), and the count of pending plans — every plan not closed. It also reports the cohort reading the review used: of the plans opened in a window, how many are closed as of a named commit.

Done when the cohort reading, run as of commit 23eb2545 over the pinned window, reports reckon's 58 opened and 7 closed, matching the review's §9, and the test pins that commit rather than today's date.
