§1 — Why: the backlog grows whether or not the fleet improves

In the four August weeks reckon closed about nine in ten of the plans it opened. Between 12 and 26 September it opened 58 plans and, by the end of that window, had closed 7 of them; 62 reckon plans are pending, most of them about the crew itself. reckon took 48% of the fleet's 2,303 promotions in the same fortnight, and its plan, evidence and research HTML grew almost twice as fast as its source (crew pattern review §4, §9). Each measured defect becomes a new plan and nothing retires one. nova shows the same shape, with eight open sprints and 32 pending plans. A cap on open work makes opening the costly step, so that closing a plan becomes the cheaper way to make room.

In this plan a plan is pending when it is not closed, and closed when its status is shipped, done, superseded or abandoned, or its archive flag is set. §2 can start now. §3 waits on the two decisions below, which the lead locks; they are held because the repository keeps any tightening of plan creation pending the lead's review under plan-lifecycle-hygiene.

§3 — A limit on pending plans per project

Deliverable. A per-project limit on pending plans in project configuration, enforced at plan creation in the mode the lead locks, with the refusal or warning naming the three pending plans nearest to closing.

Done when creating a plan beyond the limit behaves as locked, in a test, and the velocity view's weekly opened-and-closed figures show reckon closing at least as many plans as it opens for two consecutive weeks.

§ Decisions

How is the per-project limit on pending plans enforced at plan creation?

A warning does not change behaviour here: the plan-review gate has warned in report-only mode on every build dispatch and nothing changed, and the backlog grew to 58 opened and 7 closed under advisory hygiene. Plan creation is rare (about four a day), so a refusal costs little; it names the three pending plans nearest to closing, so it is dischargeable rather than a stop; and a recorded lead override keeps the authority with the lead and leaves a reason on the plan it lets through.

What is reckon's limit on pending plans? The triage proposes 25.

The lead's choice, matching the triage's proposal: 25 pending reckon plans, against 62 pending on 2026-09-26.
