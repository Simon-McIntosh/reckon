§1 — What the lead and three coordinators reported

The lead reported five defects in reckon crew follow from one pane on 2026-09-22, following the nova session s18-fable-20260922. The rows are scoped to a session while the trailing 5w· 1b· 5u figures count the whole project. A blocked or unpromoted run is shown once, so a coordinator must remember it. The grid goes ragged because model aliases have different widths. The from-and-to transition spends two state cells where the destination alone is what a reader acts on. Several cells always read –, and a cell reading 0mu is not decodable. Above all, followers go stale and are re-armed regularly.

Two coordinators answered a request for their experience the same evening, and their reports agree on the main cause. nova-a9 re-armed fourteen times in nine and a half hours and imas-efit-e3 nine times in eight hours. Every re-arm followed the host's Monitor expired after 30m notice, and neither saw a follower die on its own. Both first armings failed with exit 127, because the attach_line reckon emits is a bare reckon that is not on the Monitor shell's path. imas-efit-e3 lost three unpromoted runs for between 1h21m and 2h42m each because the transition was shown once, during a re-arm gap. nova-a9 found that observing a peer session caused the follower's recovery sweep to dispatch a review onto that peer's run from its own runtime.

Reading the code confirms three of the lead's points directly. The 0mu cell is the age of the oldest unpromoted run, printed as minutes followed by the bucket letter, from _age_cells in reckon/crew/ticker.py. The – cell is the generation rate, rendered as the absence marker whenever the transition carries no rate. The model cell is ten columns wide and deliberately lets a wider alias overflow, and the locally served lane's alias dsv4.1-flash is twelve. The session-scoped count helper landed on 2026-09-15, but the follow command at reckon/cli.py still calls format_watch_transition without session=, which is open work on a-scoped-follower-counts-its-own-session.

The sections below measure what the reports claim, so that the repair plans are sized from counts. Each is one node on the locally served lane, delivering one JSON data file under docs/research/data/ and a manifest. Every node runs as the implement role in a full worktree sandbox, because on this lane read-only workers die mid-thinking in 34 of 36 measured cases. No node changes source code.
