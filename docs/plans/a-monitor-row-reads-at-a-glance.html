§1 — What the row carries, and what its readers use

The lead's pane on 2026-09-22 showed this row, among others:

21:34:16  review  review-of-pta-adopted-read-cost-red…  working → unpromoted  dsv4.1-flash xhigh  3w· 1b· 7u  57:01  –  1mu

Four coordinators said which cells they use. All four act on the destination state, the node name, and the reason text at the end of an actionable row. Three use the elapsed time to spot a run over its budget. Three used the model and effort pair to catch a review routed to a metered lane despite --local, so that pair earns its place. None of the four could decode the age cell, which prints the age of the oldest unpromoted run as minutes followed by the bucket letter. None used the generation-rate cell, which renders the absence marker whenever the transition carries no rate. The row census in the measurement plan §3 supplies the counts across four projects.

Two layout defects compound this. The model cell is ten columns and deliberately lets a wider alias overflow, and the locally served lane's alias dsv4.1-flash is twelve, so every row on that lane shifts the cells after it. The node cell cuts at 36 columns from the right, so two reviews of similarly named nodes render identically, which nova-37 and imas-efit-e3 both hit. And ANSI colour reaches an agent reader as escape sequences inside the notification, as imas-ambix-b6 reported, so any state carried by colour alone is lost to the reader who most needs it.

The row census in the measurement plan §3 rendered every recorded transition across four projects. The model cell overflows on 47.5 percent of reckon rows and 78.2 percent of imas-efit rows, and dsv4.1-flash alone overflows on 3,954 rows. Model-cell text reaches 19 characters in nova. The rate cell renders the absence marker on 3,847 of 4,025 reckon rows, and every project carries age cells of the 0mu form. A real blocked row's reason clips at every width from 145 to 198 columns, which includes the 160 columns the follower normally runs at.

The open section crew-observability-truth §20 measured the same dead cells over 366 rows, with the rate populated in 1.9 percent of them, and moves here. Its sibling §19, where a discard and a stop render as promoted, stays with that plan because it waits on the lead's disposition decision there.

All changes here are in reckon/crew/ticker.py, with its tests in tests/test_crew_ticker.py and tests/test_crew_ticker_layout.py. The sections share those files and run one after another.

§ Decisions

Which time and spend measures stay on the row?
